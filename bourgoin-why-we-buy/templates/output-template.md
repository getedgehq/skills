# Buyer-insight and trigger-test brief

Replace the bracketed fields. Keep unknowns visible. Remove instructions from the final deliverable. Use the whole template for research and campaign work; retain only relevant fields for a narrow copy edit. Customer evidence identifiers such as E1 refer to the local ledger, while [S#] identifies the public method source.

## 1. Decision and scope

- **Requested deliverable:** [Asset or decision the user needs.] Name its audience and destination.
- **Growth objective:** [One measurable outcome.] Specify qualified demand if raw traffic is insufficient. [S1]

- **Scope and constraints:** [Available channels, product capabilities, budget if supplied, timing, and authorized actions.]
- **Evidence status:** [Interview-backed, artifact-backed, or provisional.] State what is missing.

## 4. Message decision

- **Main barrier:** [Attention, belief conflict, provider doubt, switching friction, or anxiety.] Tie it to evidence.
- **Selected presentation:** [Buyer language, reassurance, respectful belief update, credibility, curiosity, pain plus relief, or discretion.] Explain why it fits; cite the relevant method source. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7][S8]
- **Buyer-language candidates:** [Real expressions with evidence IDs.] Mark paraphrases and invented draft copy separately.
- **Promise:** [Specific progress the product can actually support.]

## 5. Finished asset

**Headline or subject:** [Ready-to-use wording addressing a relevant buyer moment.]

**Body:** [Explain the problem and progress, give the promised answer if using curiosity, and support the promise with relevant proof.] [S6][S7]

**Offer:** [What the buyer receives, scope, price if known, and any real conditions.]

**Call to action:** [One clear action with an honest expectation of what follows.]

**Limitation or fit statement:** [Material constraints buyers should understand.]

Do not leave internal evidence IDs or research caveats inside public-facing copy unless they help the buyer decide. Put unresolved evidence issues in the brief.

## 7. Selected first test

- **Recommendation and reason:** [One candidate tied to evidence and feasibility.]
- **Learning question:** [What this test can establish; what it cannot isolate.]
- **Smallest implementation:** [Asset, distribution, audience, and owner if known.]
- **Primary metric:** [Numerator / denominator, qualification criteria, tracking source.]
- **Comparison:** [Baseline or control and known differences; exploratory if neither exists.]
- **Observation period and limits:** [Dates or duration, reach or budget limits, material dependencies.]
- **Decision rule:** [Pre-agreed threshold if justified; otherwise describe evidence needed and uncertainty.]
- **Next action:** [Proceed, revise, interview, or collect more data depending on the result.]

Metric and comparison fields are package conventions for making the public trigger-test idea reviewable. Do not imply a universal sample size or guaranteed lift.
