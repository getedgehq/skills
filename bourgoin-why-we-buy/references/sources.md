# Sources

All eight sources were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02 and returned HTTP 200 with readable public content. Only the named articles and Bourgoin's own LinkedIn post inform this package. Advertisements, reader comments, linked books, and paid products were excluded. Numbering is local to this package and replaces the original file's numbering. The method is paraphrased; there are no verbatim source excerpts.

1. **[S1] Katelyn Bourgoin's Trigger Technique Framework**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/the-trigger-technique-turn-buyer-stories-into-smarter-marketing-campaigns/
   Contributes recent best-fit buyer stories, primary jobs, four cornerstone insights, target moments, three candidate ideas, and one first trigger test.
2. **[S2] Five buyer interview mistakes**, Katelyn Bourgoin's public LinkedIn post. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/katebour_i-did-300-customer-discovery-interviews-before-activity-7275865176548028416-L-UM
   Contributes warnings about product wishes, future predictions, opinions, unsuitable participants, and poorly timed interviews.
3. **[S3] Buyer-isms**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/buyer-isms/
   Contributes distinctive customer language for headlines, names, and short copy, plus checking wording with buyers rather than assuming the team's preference is correct.
4. **[S4] Confirmation Bias**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/confirmation-bias/
   Contributes shared-belief audiences, purchase reassurance, and a respectful route for buyers to revise identity-linked beliefs.
5. **[S5] Credibility**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/credibility/
   Contributes expertise, honesty, affinity, similarity, relevant proof, and acknowledging limitations as ways to support attention, belief, and action.
6. **[S6] Information Gaps**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/information-gaps/
   Contributes personally relevant curiosity, revealing enough context to earn attention, and fulfilling the question raised by a hook.
7. **[S7] Approach and Avoidance Motivation**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/approach-and-avoidance-motivation/
   Contributes pain-and-relief framing and explicit warnings against fearmongering and leaving a promised answer unresolved.
8. **[S8] The Spotlight Effect**. https://learnwhywebuy.com/the-spotlight-effect/
   Contributes buyer-centered relevance, repeating core messages, discovering actual alternatives, and reducing anxiety around socially sensitive purchases.
