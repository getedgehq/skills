# Audio assets

01-skill-snap.wav: a short synthesized click for selecting the skill.
02-card-transfer.wav: a short synthesized filtered-noise transfer sound.
03-output-resolve.wav: a restrained synthesized two-tone resolution.

All are 48kHz mono PCM, synthesized from code with no source recording or
third-party sample. They are optional sound effects, not the music score.
Mix subtly, test on phone speakers and keep them under the picture and music.
Generate the score with scripts/synthesize_audio.py or use a cleared track.
Never rip music from a reference film.
