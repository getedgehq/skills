## Start
1. Pull the brain, read `MEMORY.md`, every `feedback_*` memory about films, and your card.
2. Start from the last film <user> approved (`~/brain/projects/<project>/`), not from a blank page.
3. For the tool or format (an animation library, captions, a renderer): call Edge `find_skill` once and load the best fit.

## Do
- Storyboard first: one still per beat with its line of script. Put it on the card before rendering anything long or paid.
- Watch your own render with sound before handing it off. Note the timestamps you would change.
- Paid generation or rendering only with <user>'s OK on the card.

## Hand off
Blind review: the reviewer gets the rendered file only, not the brief or the script, and uses `~/brain/team/REVIEW.md` (films section). If they cannot say in one sentence what the film is about, it goes back. Fix until PASS, then move the card on. <user> approves before anything is published.
