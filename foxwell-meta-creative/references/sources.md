# Public source ledger

Credit: Andrew Foxwell, Foxwell Digital and the named Foxwell Founders contributors below. This package paraphrases public material and does not reproduce member-only resources or existing third-party skills.

All five sources were fetched successfully with curl on 2026-10-02, with HTTP 200 and readable article bodies. Dates below come from page publication metadata. Search was used for discovery; the cited evidence was read from curl downloads. No login, subscription or paywall bypass was used. Source copies are not distributed in this package.

[S1] **The Meta Creative Testing Frameworks Top Brands Use in 2026**. Andrew Foxwell, co-authored with Paul Fairbrother, 2026-04-09. URL: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/meta-creative-testing-frameworks-top-brands-use-2026-andrew-foxwell-xijec
Contribution: Compares batch-level ABO and direct main-campaign testing, explains consolidation trade-offs, spend capacity, quality gates and format diversity.

[S2] **Meta Creative Testing: Finding Your Path to Consistent Winners**. Courtney Fritts, Foxwell Digital, 2025-12-31. URL: https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/meta-creative-testing-finding-your-path-to-consistent-winners
Contribution: Describes four testing approaches and selection by budget, production, predictability and risk, plus winner definitions, graduation and method evolution.

[S3] **How to Create Systematic Growth Frameworks on Meta for Confident Scaling**. Andrew Foxwell, with contribution from Alex Afterman, 2026-04-06. URL: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-create-systematic-growth-frameworks-meta-scaling-andrew-foxwell-6qsqc
Contribution: Connects margin, AOV, acquisition, concentration, AAA sequencing and diversification; describes Yapper structure credited to Harry Delmage, as spelled on this page.

[S4] **Top 3 Creative Tests to Run in 2023**. Courtney Fritts, Foxwell Digital, 2023-03-06; testimonial-static practice credited to Mark Pereyra. URL: https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/top-3-creative-tests-to-run-in-2023
Contribution: Gives concrete length, hook, hold-scene, creator and testimonial-static iterations; used as dated creative guidance rather than a current account-structure prescription.

[S5] **Why $1M Ads Still Start With People, Not Prompts**. Andrew Foxwell, Foxwell Digital, 2026-08-31; public recap of an AI DTC WTF conversation with Will Sartorius and Harry Delmege of Roly Poly Digital. URL: https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/why-1m-ads-still-start-with-people-not-prompts
Contribution: Emphasizes manual customer research, human ideation and QA, AI for execution, creative quality and diversity, and operator observations about synthetic UGC longevity.

## Evidence boundaries

These are five distinct articles, not five independent experiments. The two LinkedIn articles also have agency-blog versions; mirrors do not add independent evidence. The podcast itself was not fetched or transcribed, so [S5] supports only the public recap. Do not claim direct access to the recorded conversation or private membership instruction.

[S3] spells the Yapper contributor's surname Delmage; [S5] uses Delmege. Attribute the format to the named Founders contributor and preserve that spelling difference when explaining provenance. Do not resolve it by inventing a new person or presenting Andrew as the format's sole originator.

Performance anecdotes, example budgets, suggested counts and claims about Meta's allocation are practitioner observations. They are not randomized causal evidence, universal guarantees or independently verified platform specifications. Package safeguards and calculations are labelled as adaptations in method.md. Consult current platform documentation if implementation depends on present Ads Manager controls.
