## 3. Framework and funded structure

- **Chosen method:** [ABO / CBO minimums / direct competition / manual bid / purposeful hybrid].
- **Fit assessment:** [Budget, volume, data density, predictability, risk and learning requirement]. Address each factor briefly.
- **Campaign/ad-set arrangement:** [Existing lane, new lane, concept batches and destination]. State each lane's job.
- **Allocation:** [Daily budget by lane, minimums, remaining flexibility]. Confirm sums fit the total.
- **Concurrency:** [Simultaneous briefs and assets per brief]. Explain why exposure will be useful.
- **Bid control, if used:** [Exact control and calibration assumption]. Verify current mechanics before implementation; never claim guaranteed CPA.
- **Main trade-off:** [Learning clarity / efficiency / simplicity / spend risk]. Include the likely failure mode.

## 4. Creative hypotheses and briefs

Repeat this block for each concept:

- **Concept ID:** [Stable ID]. Connect the brief to result reporting.
- **Customer situation and research:** [Specific friction, evidence source]. Use verified customer or founder detail.
- **Angle and mechanism:** [Why the product helps]. Preserve effective psychology when diversifying.
- **Exploration or iteration:** [Which, and why]. Identify changed variables and retained elements.
- **Format:** [Founder / Yapper / testimonial / static / demo / other]. Define execution rather than naming a trend.
- **Proof and claim limits:** [Substantiated details, permissions, gaps]. Leave unsupported proof blank.
- **Scale-potential quality gate:** [Why this idea could plausibly sustain meaningful spend]. The source's $10,000 prompt is a quality challenge, not a required test budget.
- **Asset plan:** [Versions, duration, speaker, production constraints]. Counts must fit capacity.

For a Yapper, add:

- **Speaker and truthful context:** [Who can naturally tell this story].
- **Hook:** [Relevant counter-conventional observation].
- **Lived problem:** [Concrete moment the viewer recognizes].
- **Product bridge:** [Natural connection after the problem].
- **Gentle invitation:** [Non-demanding next step].
- **Proposed spoken script:** [Copy for speaker validation]. Do not imply a generated story is a real testimonial.
- **Variant plan:** [Alternate hooks, stable body, separate natural takes].
- **Recording note:** [Conversational single take, natural setting]. Avoid a forced commercial voice.

- **Review dates and owner:** [Named person and dates]. Include lag-aware final review.
- **Maximum loss and action:** [Amount, calculation, pause/review rule]. Label as local policy.

- **Graduation path:** [Scale in place / move / both]. Include destination validation and fallback.
- **Budget expansion:** [Proposed change and stability requirements]. Do not infer authorization from a planning request.
