# Lichtenstein Creative Testing

Based on Denys Lichtenstein's public method. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Denys Lichtenstein.

## When to use

Use this skill to turn an account's creative bottleneck into a repeatable testing and production cycle. It supports a new testing agenda, a refresh plan for fatigued campaigns, UGC creator briefs, and a review of results that should inform the next wave. Produce a practical plan using [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md), with hypotheses, briefs, measurement rules, responsibilities, and a learning log.

The core distinction is between Exploration, which discovers substantially different concepts, and Iteration, which improves an evidenced winner. Keep both visible. A backlog of cosmetic variations cannot substitute for new ideas, and continually replacing everything cannot substitute for exploiting what already works.

Treat the published volume ranges as starting points: 8 to 12 substantively distinct creatives per campaign, replenished through weekly waves of 3 to 5 variants. Adjust explicitly to budget, production capacity, and the signal each test can realistically receive. These are different counts, not requirements to launch 12 new ads every week. No universal budget split, CPA threshold, or conversion minimum is supplied by the public method.

## Procedure

1. **Establish the decision and evidence.** Read the attribution boundaries in [references/method.md](references/method.md#1-scope-and-evidence). Record the product, offer, objective, existing results, capacity, and missing inputs. Separate account facts from assumptions. Use [references/sources.md](references/sources.md) to check the origin of methodological claims, especially when adding a numerical rule.

2. **Check measurement and economics.** Follow [references/method.md](references/method.md#2-measurement-and-business-context). Identify the conversion event, attribution window, CPA or other business target, and tracking gaps. Put account-level MER and contribution margin beside platform results. A measurement problem makes the test inconclusive rather than proving the creative failed.

3. **Classify the creative inventory.** Apply [references/method.md](references/method.md#3-two-separate-testing-lanes). Label each asset as exploratory, an iteration of a named winner, or unproven. Count genuine concepts separately from exports and hook variants. Preserve parent asset IDs so learnings remain traceable.

4. **Build a ranked hypothesis backlog.** Use [references/method.md](references/method.md#4-hypotheses-and-substantive-diversity). State the customer situation, expected response, and reason. Cover Emotion, Funktion, and Social Proof where appropriate. Rank by useful uncertainty and production feasibility; do not fill a quota with unsupported messages.

5. **Specify what each test can teach.** Follow [references/method.md](references/method.md#5-discovery-versus-variable-isolation). For exploration, compare clearly named concept packages and limit conclusions to those packages. For iteration, select one lever, starting with the hook, and hold the rest stable. Record unavoidable confounders before launch.

6. **Fix the cadence and feasible volume.** Use [references/method.md](references/method.md#6-cadence-volume-and-capacity) to schedule production, release, and review. Reserve room for both lanes without inventing a mandatory ratio. If budget cannot support useful signal, narrow the simultaneous batch and explain the adjustment.

7. **Write briefs creators can execute.** Fill the brief fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). Specify one angle, a concrete opening shot and hook, a problem-to-solution-to-action structure, evidence, and creative freedom. Batch related footage in one session while preserving distinct concept IDs.

8. **Set decisions before seeing results.** Apply [references/method.md](references/method.md#8-predeclared-review-and-routing). Define sufficient evidence, attribution maturation, winner and loser conditions, spending boundaries, and a bounded retest for borderline results. Mark missing thresholds as unresolved account choices, never as Lichtenstein's defaults.

9. **Review performance and fatigue together.** Follow [references/method.md](references/method.md#9-fatigue-and-the-next-wave). Examine CPM trends with frequency and CTR, then check market or account changes. Route supported winners into iteration, evidenced losers into the learning log, and ambiguous results into a defined follow-up.

10. **Deliver the next executable cycle.** Complete [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md), compare the plan with [examples/worked-examples.md](examples/worked-examples.md), and run [checklists/review-checklist.md](checklists/review-checklist.md). Include owners, dates, decisions, and uncertainties. Delivering this plan does not itself authorize publishing ads or changing spend.
