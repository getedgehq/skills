# Public source register

Credit: Denys Lichtenstein and Social Cooks. Supporting material is credited to its actual author or publisher below.

All entries were fetched as public HTML with curl -L on 2026-10-02 and returned HTTP 200. Only visible public content was used. No paywalled article, gated download, private newsletter, existing skill, or podcast audio transcript was used. The English and German framework pages are translations of one source, not two independent sources. Eleven distinct sources are listed, excluding that translation duplicate. Citation IDs refer to these entries throughout the package.

## [S1] Creative Testing for Meta Ads: The Framework for Scalable Ads

- Author: Denys Lichtenstein, Social Cooks.
- URL: https://socialcooks.com/en/blog/creative-testing-framework-meta-ads
- German version also fetched: https://socialcooks.com/blog/creative-testing-framework-meta-ads
- Contribution: Core two-lane method, 8 to 12 unique creatives, weekly waves of 3 to 5 variants, hook priority, fatigue indicators, and four-part UGC briefing practice; German version confirms the method terms.

## Evidence limits

This package paraphrases the fetched sources without direct source quotations. The method's framework and numbers come primarily from [S1] and [S2]. Related agency pages supply context; they are not independent verification of Meta's algorithm or performance promises. Public case studies do not disclose enough experiment detail to establish causality. No explicit exploration/iteration budget ratio, standard stop-loss multiple, universal minimum conversions, or required statistical confidence level was found. Implementation choices and invented examples are identified accordingly.
