# Creative testing cycle template

Fill every relevant field. Use unknown where evidence is missing, and distinguish facts, assumptions, and proposed account decisions. Remove guidance text when preparing the final deliverable. The schema is a package implementation of [method.md](../references/method.md), not a published Social Cooks form.

## 1. Decision and context

- **Account / product / offer:** [Identify the commercial proposition and destination.]
- **Cycle dates and timezone:** [Production, launch, evidence cutoff, attribution maturation, review.]
- **Business objective:** [Acquisition, sales, or another actual objective.]
- **Deliverable requested:** [Plan, briefs, result review, or a full next cycle.]
- **Evidence supplied:** [Link or name the reports, customer information, footage, and existing briefs.]
- **Facts / assumptions / unknowns:** [List separately; do not turn a plausible angle into customer research.]
- **Published rules used:** [Cite relevant [S#] entries; distinguish adaptations below.]

## 2. Measurement and economics

- **Primary outcome KPI and target:** [Name the metric, formula, target, and reporting source.]
- **Target basis:** [Explain margin, returns, costs, or another business reason.]
- **Conversion event:** [Define exactly what is optimized and counted.]
- **Attribution definition and maturation:** [Use a consistent window and allowance for delayed reporting.]
- **Tracking status:** [Verified, unresolved, or unknown, with the evidence supporting that label.]
- **Baseline comparison:** [State period, campaign scope, placements, and limitations.]
- **MER:** [Same-period total revenue / all-channel ad spend; specify both sources.]
- **Profit context:** [Margin, return rate, product mix, variable costs, and missing inputs.]

Do not invent a universal target or apply MER to an individual asset. Keep attention metrics diagnostic unless the business objective itself is attention.

## 4. Ranked testing agenda

| Rank | Test ID | Lane | Customer situation | Hypothesis and reason | Evidence or assumption | Learning unit | Variable / constants | Decision usefulness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Rank] | [ID] | [Lane] | [Who and when] | [Expected response and why] | [Source or untested premise] | [Concept package / isolated lever] | [Changed lever and stable context] | [What finding would change next work?] |

Rank useful uncertainty, supporting evidence, distinctiveness, and feasibility. State the inference limit when concept packages differ on several components.

## 5. Weekly wave and production calendar

- **Cadence:** [Recurring brief, shoot, edit, launch, and review dates.]
- **New variants this wave:** [Published starting point 3 to 5 weekly; actual chosen count.]
- **Capacity:** [Creator sessions, footage, editing time, and owner.]
- **Total test budget and concurrency:** [How many assets can plausibly collect useful signal?]
- **Volume adjustment:** [Reason if smaller or larger; do not fragment spend merely to hit a quota.]
- **Reserve concepts:** [Fresh angles available if fatigue becomes supported.]
- **Delayed evidence handling:** [Maintain review cadence while an immature test waits.]

## 6. Creator brief, repeat per planned asset

- **Asset ID / concept ID / parent:** [Traceable identifiers.]
- **Lane and test question:** [Discovery or the isolated lever being tested.]
- **One angle and idea:** [What this asset communicates.]
- **Customer situation:** [A concrete use context.]
- **Opening visual or action:** [What appears in the first seconds.]
- **Hook message:** [The opening proposition; specify rather than asking for engagement.]
- **Structure:** [Problem, solution or evidence, CTA.]
- **Evidence and claim limits:** [Real demo, authorized interview, substantiated benefit, or evidence still needed.]
- **CTA and destination:** [The intended action and actual offer.]
- **Creator freedom:** [Where natural language and presentation are welcome.]
- **Constants for iteration:** [Body, creator, offer, CTA, or other retained elements.]
- **Batch shot list:** [Reusable body footage and separately identified openings or concept shots.]
- **Production specs and owner:** [Only specs relevant to delivery, plus handoff date.]
- **Human review:** [Named owner checks clarity, credibility, and brand fit.]

## 7. Predeclared decision contract

| Test ID | KPI target | Sufficient evidence requirement | Observation and maturation | Winner rule | Loser rule | Spend ceiling | Borderline retest | Inconclusive conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ID] | [Target] | [Account-specific evidence and rationale] | [Dates / lag] | [Target plus evidence and economics] | [Evidence-based failure condition] | [Amount and consequence] | [Question, one bounded follow-up, date, cap] | [Tracking / sparse delivery / immature data] |

Do not claim statistical significance from a chosen conversion count. State who owns each decision and whether a spending change is a proposal or already authorized. Reaching a ceiling before sufficient evidence stops spending without automatically proving failure.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ID] | [Spend, impressions, reporting cutoff] | [Purchases, CPA, diagnostics] | [Changes and uncertainty] | [Winner / loser / borderline / inconclusive] | [What the comparison establishes] | [Iterate / stop / bounded follow-up] | [Accountable person and deadline] |

- **Fatigue review:** [CPM, frequency, CTR, outcome trends; comparable periods and market-change check.]
- **Business reconciliation:** [MER and margin context beside platform results; avoid ad-level causal claims.]
- **Next wave:** [Named hypotheses and parent winners, owners, planned release.]
- **Open decisions:** [Missing inputs and the exact decision each prevents.]
- **Review outcome:** [Pass/fail checklist result and remaining fixes.]
