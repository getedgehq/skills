# Nonfiction output template

Use this template for an essay, article, post, or chapter. Scale the structure to the assignment; headings and multiple sections are optional. This is an agent deliverable scaffold, not a template prescribed by Shapiro. The underlying progression follows his objective, exploration, rewriting, and reader-payoff approach. [S2] [S3] [S4]

Replace the brace fields. Keep the planning record separate from the finished piece. Deliver only the piece when the user requests clean copy.

## Finished piece

# {Title}

{Opening: establish the relevant situation and why the reader should care. Raise a truthful question or promise that the piece will answer. For direct instructions, give essential information immediately.} [S2]

## {Supporting point or first discovery}

{Explain the prerequisites and first part of the argument in plain language. Include enough reasoning to connect evidence to the claim. Keep required qualifications.} [S3] [S4]

{Add a concrete example or contrasting case if it makes the distinction easier to understand. Label an invented scenario when readers might otherwise mistake it for evidence.} [S4]

## {Further reasoning or consequential insight}

{Develop what follows. Use only as many sections as the material earns. Let the strongest insight become recognizable without burying the explanation behind teasers.} [S3] [S4]

{Use one relevant story, analogy, or vivid detail if it helps the idea resonate. Preserve the author's natural voice. Omit this field when the piece already works without it.} [S5]

## {Ending, with optional heading}

{Resolve the opening promise. State the understanding the reader can now carry away, or a specific next step justified by the argument. Avoid a generic motivational sign-off.} [S4] [S6]

## Optional editorial handoff

- **Objective and insight:** {one or two sentences identifying the final direction}.
- **Changes made:** {the consequential structural changes or cuts, when requested}.
- **Verification and limits:** {facts checked, remaining uncertainties, and clearly labelled illustrations}.
- **Reader validation:** {real reactions and scores, or audience feedback pending}.

These handoff fields are an agent reporting adaptation. Omit fields that provide no value for the user.

## Optional feedback request

Ask intended readers to identify passages where attention faded, meaning was unclear, and an insight felt worthwhile. Ask for an overall satisfaction rating from 1 to 10. If multiple readers respond, keep their actual annotations and calculate the actual mean. Shapiro's suggested target is at least 7.5 across several readers, not universal approval above 9. [S4]
