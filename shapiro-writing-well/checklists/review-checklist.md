# Review checklist

For each item, record **PASS** or **FAIL** with a brief location or reason. An applicable failure triggers revision. Optional devices such as stories and diagrams need not appear; pass when their omission suits the piece. For external feedback, pass requires actual responses. If unavailable, record **FAIL: audience validation pending** and deliver with that limitation disclosed, rather than manufacturing a result.

## Finished-output gates

- [ ] **Purpose:** The piece has a discernible objective and gives its intended reader something worthwhile. [S2]
- [ ] **Substance:** The central insight matters, is not merely unfamiliar trivia, and has adequate reasoning or honest scope. [S2] [S3]
- [ ] **Opening:** Enough context establishes why the reader should care; the promise accurately matches the body. [S2]
- [ ] **Fulfillment:** Every significant question or teaser receives its promised answer. [S2] [S4]
- [ ] **Argument:** Readers can follow the prerequisites, support, and consequences without a missing logical step. [S3] [S4]
- [ ] **Clarity:** Wording is concrete, sentence load is manageable, and simplification retains causal explanation and nuance. [S4]
- [ ] **Examples:** Where needed, a concrete example or contrasting case clarifies the boundary; optional examples earn their space. [S4]
- [ ] **Concision:** Each section and paragraph contributes understanding, persuasion, or resonance; the requested length contains no filler. [S4]
- [ ] **Intrigue:** The piece has an effective opening, a recognizable high point, and an ending that makes reading worthwhile, adapted to its format. [S4] [S6]
- [ ] **Voice:** Language fits the author; imagery and analogy aid the idea rather than imitate another writer. [S5] [S8]
- [ ] **Copy edit:** Paragraphs are approachable, verbs are precise, modifiers matter, and punctuation supports the intended meaning and house style. [S6] [S9]
- [ ] **Factual integrity:** Claims, anecdotes, quotations, and examples are supported or appropriately qualified; fictional scenarios are identifiable. This is an agent safeguard.
- [ ] **Completion:** No unresolved drafting placeholders or planning instructions remain in the reader-facing piece. This is an agent safeguard.
- [ ] **Self-review:** The revised piece was reread for changes in meaning, omitted qualifications, and awkward transitions. Structural compression did not damage the argument. [S4]
- [ ] **Audience validation:** Actual intended readers supplied reactions; confusion and weak stretches were addressed. Real scores, if collected, meet the chosen stopping criterion or the remaining gap is reported. [S4]

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | What reveals it | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic topic mistaken for insight | The thesis could fit hundreds of interchangeable posts. | Find a specific mechanism, disagreement, or precise articulation that matters. [S2] |
| Novelty without merit | A bold claim is the whole argument. | Add reasoning and evidence, narrow the claim, or choose a better insight. [S2] [S6] |
| Question without stakes | The opening is mysterious but irrelevant. | Connect the question to a recognizable concern before withholding the answer. [S2] |
| Empty promise | The ending never answers the opening. | Supply the missing explanation or rewrite the opening to match the actual piece. [S2] |
| Premature polishing | Fluent paragraphs conceal undeveloped thinking. | Return to the objective and draft missing support with explicit placeholders. [S3] |
| Endless weak alternatives | New versions repeat the same defect. | Diagnose the defect, change the premise or obtain missing information, then try again. Agent adaptation of [S7]. |
| Oversimplification | Shortening deletes why something happens or when it does not. | Restore the causal link and qualification using simpler sentences. [S4] |
| Word trimming without restructuring | Every sentence is shorter, but the section still wanders. | Rebuild from an essentials summary, then compare for omissions. Agent adaptation of [S4]. |
| Decorative resonance | Metaphors are memorable but the claim remains unclear. | State the mechanism plainly and keep only imagery that reinforces it. [S5] |
| Borrowed persona | A restrained author suddenly sounds theatrical. | Return to the author's phrasing and distinctive observations. [S5] [S8] |
| Artificial suspense | Definitions or needed facts are repeatedly delayed. | Reveal prerequisites immediately and reserve only consequential outcomes. Agent adaptation of [S8]. |
| Approval-driven bloat | Each respondent's preference adds another tangent. | Fix recurring defects; reject incompatible additions that weaken the objective. [S4] |
| Invented validation | Scores or reaction counts have no actual respondents. | Remove them and report audience feedback pending. Agent safeguard for [S4]. |
| Mechanical punctuation rules | Copy editing damages meaning or contradicts house style. | Apply clarity as the goal and use the relevant language convention. Agent adaptation of [S9]. |

## Stopping decision

Deliver once applicable substantive gates pass and the requested scope is complete. When audience feedback exists, Shapiro proposes a satisfaction mean of at least 7.5 across several respondents and warns against chasing universal scores above 9. Do not let that heuristic replace a factual-integrity check. Without respondents, disclose the pending check and stop after self-review unless a concrete unresolved defect requires further work. [S4]
