# Fetched public sources

All sources are by Wes Kao. Each URL below was fetched successfully with curl on 2026-10-02, and its public article body was inspected. Only those fetched pages inform this package. No book text, paid material, or linked third-party material is used. The method and guidance are paraphrased; article titles are retained for identification.

[S1] **Spiky point of view: Let's get a little controversial**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/spiky-point-of-view-lets-get-a-little-controversial
Contribution: Defines the five traits, personal credibility, discovery prompts, iterative sharpening, contestable frames, and dialogue as the goal.

[S2] **My spiky points of view: 15 examples to inspire your SPOV**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/my-spiky-points-of-view-15-examples-to-inspire-your-spov
Contribution: Recommends a wider candidate inventory and explains defensible conviction without excessive defensive framing.

[S3] **Why high performers make assertions: The difference between insights, suggestions, and assertions**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/why-a-players-make-assertions
Contribution: Distinguishes observation from advocacy through action, conviction, ownership, and an explicit consequence.

[S4] **Asking questions = no skin in the game. If you ask a question, try to answer it**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/questions-are-overrated
Contribution: Explains why useful questions should lead to attempted answers and forward progress.

[S5] **Tone and words: Use accurate and precise language**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/tone-and-words-use-accurate-and-precise-language
Contribution: Calibrates wording to certainty, consequences, intent, and power dynamics, including situations where softeners help.

[S6] **Be objective, not detached**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/be-objective-not-detached
Contribution: Shows why facts need interpretation and proportionate emotional context rather than detached reporting.

[S7] **How to be concise**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/how-to-be-concise
Contribution: Treats concision as density and clear thinking while warning against removing necessary detail.

[S8] **My episode on Lenny's Podcast**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/wes-on-lennys-podcast
Contribution: Kao's own recap introduces anticipating the obvious objection and matching expressed confidence to evidence.

[S9] **The Super Specific How: How to make your cohort-based course more rigorous**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/super-specific-how
Contribution: Demonstrates actionable teaching through scripts, screenshots, examples, and concrete behaviors, adapted here to opinion writing.

[S10] **Using affirmative phrases: "Do this" versus "Don't do that"**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/do-this-versus-dont-do-that
Contribution: Explains how affirmative instructions reduce interpretive effort and improve tone.

[S11] **Take 3 minutes to delete these words and improve your writing forever**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/take-3-minutes-to-delete-these-words-and-improve-your-writing-forever
Contribution: Provides contextual edits for intensifiers, defensive wording, double negatives, and unnecessary formality.

[S12] **Eyes light up (ELU)**
URL: https://www.weskao.com/blog/eyes-light-up-elu
Contribution: Tests messaging through observable interest, follow-up questions, and remembered language instead of polite approval.

[S13] **Rigorous thinking: No lazy thinking**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/rigorous-thinking
Contribution: Pressure-tests logic through upside, downside, grounded evidence, constraints, and explicit assumptions.

[S14] **Sales, then logistics**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/sales-not-logistics
Contribution: Establishes reader motivation and buy-in before procedural instructions, with an exception when buy-in is secure.

[S15] **Strategy, not self-expression: How to decide what to say when giving feedback**
URL: https://newsletter.weskao.com/p/strategy-not-self-expression
Contribution: Filters persuasive communication for behavior change and warns against venting, punishment, and seeking the last word.
