# Pass/fail review checklist

Run this review before delivering a positioning package and again before handing off each editorial batch. Mark every applicable item PASS or FAIL and give a short evidence note. Use N/A only with a reason, such as no profile changes requested. These review fields are implementation conventions grounded in [../references/method.md](../references/method.md).

## Positioning and roadmap

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Company and personal objectives are distinguished; confidential motivations have an appropriate disclosure boundary.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A specific priority Zielgruppe and its real concerns replace an audience of everyone.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The Superkräfte-Portfolio uses supported capabilities, knowledge, experience, personality, and relevant resources.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Emerging capabilities and aspirations are distinguished from demonstrated achievements.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The Personal Branding Pitch connects the person, audience, and useful contribution in understandable language.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The roadmap connects foundations, positioning, themes and messages, content, and suitable digital or analog channels.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The Positionspapier contains a focused theme set, actual messages, voice guidance, boundaries, and profile direction.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Selected themes have a coherent connection and fit the executive's knowledge and role.

Method basis: Roadmap and real-attribute principles [S4]; CEO objectives and strategy [S1] [S8]; holistic presence [S6].

## Draft quality and recognition

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Each draft gives its intended reader a useful explanation, lesson, perspective, or decision aid.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: First-person anecdotes, numbers, claims, and attributed views have traceable client evidence.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The executive could explain and defend the draft in a live conversation.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The opening is relevant and truthful; the text contains concrete substance instead of unsupported superlatives or buzzwords.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The voice and public facets match the person in this professional setting.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Private boundaries and permission for stories involving others are resolved.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Visuals clarify the message and have an identified approval and usage status.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: No post is a copied formula or a corporate announcement with an invented personal sentence attached.

Method basis: Digital and live alignment [S1]; facets and values [S9]; useful writing and visuals [S7]; personal versus private [S11]; substance and originality [S3]. Evidence and asset records are package conventions.

## Editorial control and handoff

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Positioning preceded the strategy session and the paper records its substantive decisions.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Recurring communication-team meetings are scheduled or proposed with named owners.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: CEO participation for content approval is set for every two weeks, independently of publication rhythm.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Each ready-to-publish item has explicit CEO approval for the recorded text and asset version.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Revised or held items are clearly separated from approved items.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Missed meetings and unresolved facts lead to a replacement review or hold, with no assumed approval.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The publication owner is the CEO or a designated internal owner; the adviser or agent does not collect credentials or take over the account.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Comment guidance, relevant conversations, and response ownership accompany the content where requested.

Method basis: Process, approval cadence, no takeover, and response training [S1]; hybrid support [S8]; current agency client-control practice [S7]. Version states and hold handling are package conventions.

## Measurement and attribution

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Success indicators reflect the agreed business or career objectives, with platform indicators interpreted in context.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The starting baseline or its absence, evidence route, and review date are stated.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Business attribution is marked reported, corroborated, or unknown rather than assumed from timing.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: No follower count, post frequency, case result, or short-term reach promise is presented as a guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A role change includes an explanation and a plan to reinforce the new association over time.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Method claims cite fetched sources, distinguish agency guidance from Zayats's own statements, and avoid claiming full access to paid chapters.

Method basis: Goal-dependent measurement [S7] [S11]; focus and long-term repetition [S10]; illustrative case limitations [S12]. Attribution labels are package conventions.

## Release rule

A positioning proposal can be delivered with open decisions clearly marked. A publication handoff fails if any fact, public permission, CEO sign-off, or publication-owner check fails. Resolve the problem or hold the affected item. This release rule governs the package's execution, not an attributed quotation from Zayats.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calendar before positioning | Activity has no audience-linked direction. | Revisit goals, stakeholders, strengths, and pitch before choosing topics. |
| Twelve unrelated themes | The audience cannot form a clear association. | Choose two or three connected themes and store other ideas separately. |
| Theme mistaken for message | A category such as leadership says nothing about the person's viewpoint. | Write the specific lesson or position within that theme. |
| Generic executive voice | The public persona cannot be recognized in real life. | Interview for an actual observation and test the language with the CEO. |
| Invented anecdote or numerical proof | Persuasion outruns evidence. | Verify with the client, remove the claim, or use a clearly framed general explanation. |
| Company publicity pasted onto a personal profile | The person becomes a distribution channel without a distinct contribution. | Explain the executive's own perspective and give the audience a useful takeaway. |
| Authenticity treated as oversharing | Professional relevance and personal privacy are lost. | Choose suitable public facets and keep agreed private subjects out. |
| Algorithm tactics replace substance | Reach becomes detached from the intended stakeholders and outcomes. | Start from a real audience question and supported insight. |
| Two-week review mistaken for two-week posting | An approval process is confused with output frequency. | State meeting dates and publishing rhythm in separate fields. |
| CEO approves strategy but never sees posts | Strategy agreement is misused as publication consent. | Review the actual content version and record its approval. |
| Adviser quietly runs the account | The workflow loses the required control and proximity. | Return publishing to the CEO or internal owner and provide reviewed drafts. |
| Missed review treated as approval | Silence becomes a decision the CEO did not make. | Hold unapproved items and arrange a replacement review. |
| Likes presented as recruitment impact | Attribution is unsupported. | Seek applicant or recruiting evidence and mark unknown cases honestly. |
| One transition announcement | Old audience associations persist. | Repeat the revised contribution through relevant examples and profile alignment. |
| Agency case copied as a universal method | Context and authorship are misrepresented. | Use the case to illustrate a principle, label its provenance, and design an original suitable format. |
