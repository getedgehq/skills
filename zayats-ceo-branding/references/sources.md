# Public source register

Credit: Marina Zayats's public work and Schaffensgeist's published practice underpin this package. Agency articles by Tomas Herzberger are identified separately.

All sources below were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02 and their accessible bodies were inspected. Discovery also used web search. Source titles are metadata; method descriptions are paraphrased. No paid chapters, private newsletters, authenticated posts, or third-party skills were used. Podcast pages provide descriptions, not verified audio transcripts. Relative LinkedIn dates are not treated as exact publication dates.

- [S1] Julia-Eva Seifert, OMR, 700 Euro pro Stunde? Linkedin Ghostwriter werden immer gefragter und immer teurer, 2022-11-17. URL: https://omr.com/de/daily/linkedin-ghostwriter . Contribution: Joint Zayats and Herzberger interview documents positioning, strategy with a Positionspapier, recurring editorial sessions, CEO approval every two weeks, no agency account takeover, and response training.
- [S2] Schaffensgeist, Unsere Bücher. URL: https://www.schaffensgeist.com/bucher . Contribution: Agency description of Digital Personal Branding confirms positioning before visibility and the emphasis on personal value contribution.

URL: https://www.mareikelist.de/blog/personal-branding-linkedin-interview-marina-zayats . Contribution: Zayats discusses focus, audience service, understandable language, meaningful outcomes, and communicating a positioning change.
- [S4] Marina Zayats, Digital Personal Branding, freely accessible front matter, Springer Gabler, 2020, especially printed pages VII-X and XIII-XIV. URL: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bfm:978-3-658-30174-3/1 .
