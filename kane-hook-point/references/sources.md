## Reading boundaries

The dated LinkedIn article includes a specific historical ad budget and variant count. Those are an example, not a current platform default or a universal minimum. Agency proprietary framework names indicate the existence of commercial systems; their full internal mechanics are not disclosed by these pages. This package does not reconstruct them or reproduce books. Source [S7] discusses serial cliffhangers; the package's stronger payoff requirement is a practical trust safeguard for promised educational or sales deliverables, not a claim that Kane bans serial storytelling.
