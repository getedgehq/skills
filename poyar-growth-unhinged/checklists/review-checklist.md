# Finished Output Review

Record **PASS** or **FAIL**, with a short evidence note, for each applicable item. Record **OUTSIDE SCOPE** with a reason for genuinely irrelevant items. Missing evidence can still pass when the output explicitly remains provisional and specifies how to learn; unsupported certainty fails. Do not silently treat an unexamined topic as passed.

## Common failure modes and fixes

1. **Tactic before diagnosis.** Replace the acquisition or seller hiring prescription with a journey map and one evidence-backed constraint. If the cause is uncertain, name the competing causes and a learning action. [S1]
2. **Qualification theater.** Replace ever-higher lead scores with a best-fit account denominator, meaningful stages, and observed transitions. Preserve activity metrics only as supporting indicators. [S6]
3. **Monetizing before shared value.** Inspect invitation and collaboration gates; make a credible adoption bridge and graduate customers when needs deepen. A lower price alone may not fix weak team formation. [S2]
4. **Fashionable pricing unit.** Compare the proposed unit with seats and other plausible units, then calculate bills across the lifecycle. Reject a unit that raises spending without understandable added value. [S7] [S8]
5. **Package sprawl.** Give each tier a buyer job, remove redundant distinctions, and justify add-ons with separate demand, budget, timing, or costs. Recheck the average initial transaction for unnecessary extras. [S3]
6. **Sales harvesting existing demand.** Specify assistance, comparison, and incremental economics. Check whether commissions and discounts simply convert healthy self-service spending into lower net revenue. [S4] [S9]
7. **Champion mistaken for buyer.** Add a route to deployment authority, budget ownership, and buyer-facing value rather than repeating end-user feature benefits. [S4] [S5]
8. **Predictability solved by overcommitment.** Offer a realistic entry purchase, then evaluate annual allowances and alerts using actual usage history. Do not give every buyer an automatic grace period or deep discount. [S7] [S9]
9. **Weak experiment presented as certainty.** State sample and selection limits, use a defensible comparison, separate simultaneous changes, and allow an inconclusive result. Avoid aggressive arbitrary live price variation. [S7] [S8]
10. **Cash or retention vanity.** Recalculate payback from recognized contribution and full costs; interpret retention with customer segment and acquisition motion. Label historical numbers as historical. [S9]

## Release decision

Revise applicable failures before presenting a final recommendation. If a failure cannot be resolved with available evidence, narrow the recommendation to research or a pilot and state the remaining uncertainty. The output is ready when its recommendation is proportionate to the evidence and its next action can be evaluated. Review approval does not itself authorize live pricing changes, seller outreach, or publication.
