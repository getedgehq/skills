Use this fill-in template for a complete playbook or a focused decision. Replace bracketed fields with case evidence. Mark an irrelevant section as outside scope and give a short reason. Unknown values remain unknown; do not insert invented precision to complete a table. Guidance paragraphs may be removed from the finished deliverable.
