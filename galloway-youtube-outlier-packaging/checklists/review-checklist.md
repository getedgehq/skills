Evidence normalization, status labels, and feasibility fields are package safeguards. Their presence should not be presented as a separate published Galloway checklist.

A planning package can pass with three complete briefs while image production remains outstanding, if that is the requested scope. An upload-ready package cannot claim completed thumbnails until actual images exist and have been inspected. Missing baseline evidence may be accepted as a clearly stated limitation; fabricated evidence never passes.

## Common failures and repairs

| Failure | Why it weakens the output | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large raw view count called an outlier | Channel-relative performance is unknown | Obtain the source channel baseline or relabel as an unverified comparable. [S1][S2] |
| New channel assigned invented internal winners | There is no upload history to support the label | Use external research and innovation hypotheses; establish a baseline over time. |
| Mixed Shorts, long form, and unequal upload ages | Comparison can confuse format or age effects with idea strength | Split cohorts and disclose observation windows. |
| Exact imitation of another package | It adds no creator-specific execution reason | Transfer a mechanism and rebuild around the creator's advantage and new evidence. [S1][S2] |
| Technical title only insiders understand | Casual and new viewers cannot see the stakes | Keep the substance and reframe its consequence. [S6] |
| Broad framing loses the core audience | Attention is detached from the channel's value | Put specialist depth back into the premise and payoff. [S1][S6] |
| Ten titles differ only by adjectives | The team has not tested alternative promises | Explore comparison, tension, stakes, and question angles. [S2] |
| Attractive but crowded thumbnail | Viewers cannot process the visual argument quickly | Remove competing text or objects and repeat the small-size test. [S6] |
| Unknown experiment result asserted as fact | The package cannot yet be fulfilled truthfully | Use a question or process promise until results exist. [S4] |
| Opening postpones the promised subject | Viewers lack immediate confirmation of their choice | Show or state the promised subject first, then build the story. [S4] |
| CTR target dictates every change | Context and delivery problems are ignored | Examine views, retention, audience, and the actual package together. [S2][S3] |
| Successful format abandoned out of boredom | Creator novelty preference overrides viewer evidence | Make a related episode with fresh stakes, while preserving the mechanism. [S2][S5] |
| Every upload requires exceptional spending | The channel's capacity becomes unsustainable | Reserve larger attempts for selected ideas and apply a budget ceiling. [S4] |
| Packaging revisions never end | No published learning enters the loop | Set a decision deadline, select a strong truthful pair, and publish. [S4][S6] |

Record: [Ready for planning / ready for production / ready for upload / revise], [remaining work], [owner], and [deadline]. A passing package communicates a defensible viewer promise and a way to learn; it does not certify future views.
