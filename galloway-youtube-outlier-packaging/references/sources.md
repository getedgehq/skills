# Fetched public sources

All six sources were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02 without authentication. Only publicly returned content was used. The attached strategy image in [S1] was also fetched and visually inspected. No book, paid newsletter, or paywalled text was used. Method prose is paraphrased; source titles below identify the material rather than serving as advice quotations.

1. **[S1] Paddy Galloway, One page YouTube strategy for 2026.** [Public LinkedIn post and attached strategy image](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paddy-galloway-459b8913a_one-page-youtube-strategy-for-2026-our-activity-7412172554393812992-qF2S). Contributes operating models, four advantage types, viewer groups, 100-idea brainstorming, outlier definitions, and recurring study actions; the detailed method is in the image.
2. **[S2] Paddy Galloway, I just turned 29 and realised I have spent more than half my life studying YouTube strategy.** [Public LinkedIn lesson list](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paddy-galloway-459b8913a_i-just-turned-29-and-realised-ive-spent-activity-7327726416354037772-jvX0). Contributes ten title candidates, three thumbnails, external outlier study, format adaptation, repeat winners, and practical business cautions.
3. **[S3] Paddy Galloway, I just turned 30: 30 lessons for creators.** [Public LinkedIn lesson list](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paddy-galloway-459b8913a_i-just-turned-30-ive-dedicated-over-50-activity-7460328048635191296-IFmy). Contributes five thumbnail concepts with three executions, protected planning time, study routines, and warnings about context-free metrics.
4. **[S4] Paddy Galloway, A playbook for making a viral YouTube video (in any niche).** [Public LinkedIn article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/playbook-making-viral-youtube-video-any-niche-paddy-galloway-zt94c). 5. **[S5] Paddy Galloway, A playbook for becoming the biggest channel in your niche.** [Public LinkedIn article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/playbook-becoming-biggest-channel-your-niche-paddy-galloway-frzqc). Contributes core/casual/new framing, novelty and clarity tests, peer reactions, thumbnail simplicity, visual embellishment nuance, and limits on perfectionism; automated transcription can contain errors, so avoid relying on uncertain wording.

## Citation convention

[S1] through [S6] always refer to the entries above. Citations beside rules identify their public basis. Evidence collected about a user's channel belongs in the deliverable's separate idea ledger, not in this source numbering. Invented example analytics are explicitly labelled and are not source evidence.
