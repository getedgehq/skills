# Meta ads decision brief template

Use this template for a launch, audit, or troubleshooting deliverable. Replace bracketed fields; remove instructions from the final client-facing brief. Keep account observations, source-based recommendations, and hypotheses distinct. Figures require a period, denominator, and source. Record unknowns explicitly.

## 1. Assignment and intended outcome

- Business and offer: [What is sold, price, relevant product facts, and real offer terms.]
- Assignment: [Launch, account audit, or specific problem diagnosis.]
- Business outcome: [Purchases, contribution, qualified leads, or another measurable result.]
- Success criterion: [Target and business rationale; label estimated targets.]
- Scope and constraints: [Geography, eligibility, available resources, account access, authorized actions.]
- Sales cycle: [Typical time to outcome and how mature data must be.]

Guidance: A requested platform metric is not necessarily the business outcome. Avoid inventing eligibility rules from a persona.

## 2. Evidence and unknowns

| Observation or unknown | Source and period | Reliability or limitation | Effect on decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Account fact] | [Export, CRM cohort, event test] | [Volume, maturity, missing data] | [What it supports] |
| [Unknown] | [Unavailable] | [Required evidence] | [Which action depends on it] |

Guidance: Use data of comparable age and definition. Label interpretation as interpretation; correlation is not a demonstrated cause.

## 3. Performance goal and economics

- Campaign objective: [Account-supported choice.]
- Performance goal and conversion event: [Exact requested action, count or value, and location.]
- Why this signal: [Connection to the business outcome; limitations of any proxy.]
- Budget: [Daily or lifetime amount, currency, and available scope.]
- Expected event-cost range: [Basis and uncertainty.]
- Estimated events per funded unit: [Budget calculation, not a forecast.]
- Economics: [CPA, contribution, ROAS, qualified-lead cost, or cohort value as relevant.]

Guidance: Check event support and timeliness. A low-volume purchase campaign can still be worthwhile; an abundant proxy can still be worthless.

## 4. Measurement readiness

- Browser event coverage: [Verified, defective, or untested.]
- Server and CRM events: [Relevant outcomes, matching, and delivery status.]
- Event correctness and deduplication: [Test journey and findings.]
- Attribution setting for delivery: [Actual setting and buying-cycle rationale.]
- Reporting views: [Available click, view, engagement, conversion-count, and audience-segment views.]
- Reconciliation: [Business records, revenue definitions, repeat buyers, time windows.]
- Repairs before conclusions: [Concrete defect and verification needed.]

Guidance: Distinguish raw events from attributed results. Do not equate a report label with unique new customers or causal lift.

## 5. Campaign structure and delivery

- Baseline: [Simplest viable campaign and ad set arrangement.]
- Necessary separations: [Distinct objective or business requirement and funding impact.]
- Controls versus suggestions: [Actual account behavior for each relevant setting.]
- Audience Segments: [Definitions, completeness, availability, and observed warm delivery.]
- Placements and enhancements: [Baseline plus specific documented exceptions.]
- Value-rule verdict: [Both gates pass, fail, or remain unknown; supporting quality data.]

Guidance: A preference for control does not justify extra funded units. Any value-rule percentage needs an account-specific rationale and review.

- Testing approach: [Natural delivery or supported native test; budget and interpretability.]

Guidance: Diversify persuasion and presentation. Do not promise equal spend or treat tiny edits as sufficient conceptual diversity.

## 7. Change decision record

| Proposed change | Proven problem or required constraint | Evidence | Mechanism and alternative | Review or reversal condition |

Guidance: Include a hold recommendation when no evidenced problem exists. Label experimental interventions as hypotheses.

## 8. Evaluation and next actions

- Decision state: [Hold, repair, revise creative, test an exception, or scale.]
- Aggregate unit: [Ad set or campaign, matching budget allocation.]
- Primary outcome and quality measures: [Business-relevant definitions.]
- Evaluation window: [Dates, attribution lag, and cohort maturity.]
- Diagnostic branch: [Attention, conversion experience, or attribution.]
- Next action, owner, and review condition: [Concrete action and when to reassess.]
- Uncertainty: [What remains unsupported and the evidence needed.]

Guidance: Prefer business outcomes to isolated asset averages. Scaling may increase costs; do not apply a universal percentage increase.
