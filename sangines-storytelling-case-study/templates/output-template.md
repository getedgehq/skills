# Deliverable template

Use the sections relevant to the requested task. For a complete case-study package, fill the internal brief and evidence record, provide the audience-facing post, and finish with a handoff. For an interview-planning request, deliver the brief and questions first. For a revision, return the improved post and the evidence gaps that affect it.

Guidance and evidence fields are internal. Remove bracketed placeholders and drafting instructions from finished public copy. Unresolved facts must not appear as asserted results. This template is an implementation adaptation of [S1][S3][S4].

## 1. Internal brief

- **Author:** [Founder or executive and relevant role.] Use the actual speaker whose experience supplies the story.
- **Intended reader:** [Role, company type, recognizable pain.] Define who should recognize themselves in the starting situation.
- **Offer connection:** [What the business helps this reader do.] Explain relevance without making the case a generic advertisement.
- **Objective:** [Trust, buyer education, qualified inquiries, or other requested goal.] Select an observable purpose.
- **Story candidate:** [Client transformation and why it fits.] Identify the starting obstacle and supportable change.
- **Identity and disclosure:** [Named, anonymized, or undecided; permission status.] Do not infer permission from available raw notes.
- **Voice notes:** [Preferred vocabulary, formality, personal viewpoint.] Derive from supplied writing or speech, not Sanginés's mannerisms.
- **Cadence:** [Weekly default or justified alternative.] Record why an archival or less frequent workflow is sufficient.
- **Profile or destination readiness:** [Relevant proof and next step already available, or recommended change.] This is a recommendation, not permission to edit an account.

## 2. Interview brief

- **Input available:** [Recording, transcript, notes, archive, client report.] Do not claim to have listened to an unavailable recording.
- **Recent-work recap:** [Changes, wins, setbacks from the recent period.] Use it to surface a lived story.
- **Audience feedback:** [Prior comments or buyer questions.] Identify what needs a more useful answer.
- **Pillar ideas:** [Tactical / aspirational / insightful / personal.] Brainstorm three per pillar when planning a full call; select one per pillar for initial preparation.
- **Selected case outline:** [Opening possibility, starting situation, stages, outcome.] The outline identifies evidence needs rather than presupposing a success.
- **Advance questions:** [Three or four initial questions for each chosen idea.] Send or present these within the authorized task scope.
- **Case follow-ups:** [What happened? Why that action? What changed the plan? How was the result measured? What continues?] Probe until the story has substance.
- **Recording and notes plan:** [Tool if needed, who records, where input lives.] Any suitable recording or transcript workflow can support the method.
- **Additional evidence requested:** [Missing facts and person who can supply them.] Keep this specific and proportional.

### Client's starting situation

[Who they were and what they wanted.]

[The familiar barrier, followed by the distinctive complication. Show how an existing strength could coexist with the struggle.]

### Transformation process

[Stage noun]: [Concrete action and why it mattered.]

[Stage noun]: [Next action, discovery, and real adjustment if one happened.]

[Additional stage only when needed to explain the transformation.]

### Resulting situation

[Supported outcome with timeframe and measurement context.]

[Qualitative change and any material attribution qualification.]

[Continuing relationship if true; otherwise a lasting benefit or accurate closing lesson.]

[Optional next step if requested and appropriate.]

The four headings are drafting scaffolding. They need not appear in the final LinkedIn copy. Do not pad the post to fit a word count or invent a stage to fill the template.

## 5. Internal handoff

- **Review outcome:** [Pass / revise / insufficient evidence for completed case study.] Apply the review checklist.
- **Blocking gaps:** [Facts required before the proposed claims can be used.] Provide a usable narrower draft where possible.
- **Changes from prior draft:** [Material changes and their rule basis.] Include only if revising.
- **Calendar placement:** [Aspirational pillar and proposed slot.] Show how it sits beside other pillars for a full-system request.
- **Owner and next action:** [Who reviews, measures, and handles commercial follow-up.] Do not fabricate owners or deadlines.
- **Measurement:** [Relevant audience response, substantive comments, inquiries, qualified demos; available tracking.] Distinguish observations from attribution assumptions.
- **Distribution recommendation:** [Organic test; possible repurposing or amplification later.] State recommendation separately from any executed action.
