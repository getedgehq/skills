## Evidence boundaries

These are first-party accounts and promotional case studies, not independently audited performance studies. The 60% inbound and 60% revenue statements have different denominators. Neither establishes an expected lift or causal guarantee for another business.

[S1] describes Vicktoria's later 23,000+ follower result; [S8] retains a 12K title and contains differing newsletter totals across sections. Keep each figure attached to its source and timeframe rather than combining them into a single current snapshot. [S9] contains competition-entrant copy unrelated to Instaffo in its before/after block; do not use that block as evidence. [S7] contains repeated role labels in testimonial markup; favor substantive case narratives over duplicated presentation labels.

The agency sitemap and resources page were also fetched for navigation. They are not substantive method citations: the resources page contains placeholder blog entries and does not establish the content of a paid product. No useful publicly accessible talk or podcast transcript was needed to reach ten distinct substantive sources. The workshop citation is its public written recap, not the gated recording.
