## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identical pitch in email and LinkedIn | Ignores complementary channel roles | Keep email relevant and make the connection contextual |
| Connect every emailed lead | Removes route three's scarce-capacity decision | Freeze the cohort and rank its top decile |
| Call the top 10% a reply-rate goal | Confuses selection with an outcome metric | Define selection over eligible leads before observing replies |
| Connect after an absence notice | Treats automation as human commitment | Keep route one pending until a suitable human reply |
| Email before acceptance in route two | Skips the specified trigger | Hold the handoff or explicitly select a different route |
| Pitch after an unsubscribe | Mistakes a reply for relationship openness | Suppress further outreach and record the stop |
| Claim the prospect saw a post | Invents an unobserved event | Reference only the verified connection or actual exchange |
| Generic tips plus inflated result | Substitutes claims for experience | Use a documented intervention and scoped metrics |
| Invent a private three-stage formula | Overstates the fetched evidence | Label organizing functions as an adaptation |
| Optimize comments without qualification | Measures attention as pipeline | Track buyer fit, relevant need, and meetings separately |
| Promote more leads when selected buyers are connected | Expands the decile to fill a quota | Keep the original ranked allocation and leave capacity unused |
| Treat this package as permission to send | Confuses drafting with execution authority | Return drafts or execute only within separately granted scope |
