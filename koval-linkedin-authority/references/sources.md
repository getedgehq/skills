Credit: Arthur Koval, author of the public LinkedIn posts summarized here. This package is an independent paraphrase.

All five sources below were fetched as public HTML using curl with redirects and a browser user agent on 2026-10-02. Each returned HTTP 200 and exposed the author's post body without authentication. Titles below are descriptive paraphrases. No comments, private messages, gated downloads, or third-party skills were used as method evidence.

## Retrieval and coverage limits

Search discovery covered Koval's name with agency, blog, podcast, interview, newsletter, email, and system terms. It surfaced additional indexed profile excerpts, but curl requests to his public profile and regional variants returned HTTP 999. Those excerpts are excluded from the evidence base and are not counted as additional sources. No separate relevant agency blog, talk, podcast, or public newsletter article was verified in this research. These five posts are distinct sources from one author on one platform, not independent corroboration.

[S2] calls its resource the LinkedIn Money System, while [S5] names the LinkedIn Authority System. Their advertised components overlap; this package does not establish that the private resources are identical. The public overview mentions three-stage content but does not disclose its full stages in the fetched posts. Exact DM scripts and AI prompts are offered through a requested breakdown, not printed in these sources. This package supplies original adaptations instead of reconstructing unseen material.

Revenue, contract value, impressions, and meeting totals in these posts are Koval's self-reported claims. They were not independently audited. [S1]'s invitation-limit and email-scale statements are historical claims in the post, not verified current platform specifications. All working material is paraphrased; no direct source quotations are reproduced.
