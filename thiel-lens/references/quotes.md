# Peter Thiel: verified public quotes

Short excerpts from a published essay, a recorded lecture, interviews and the book Zero to One, each with its source. Essay, lecture and interview excerpts were matched against the linked source when collected; links can rot, so re-check one before relying on it. Book excerpts cite Zero to One without a page reference: check the wording against the book before quoting, and note that the numerals in 11 and 25 were confirmed through secondary sources only. Excerpts are short on purpose: quote only these words. Numbers are referenced from SKILL.md.

Independent reference list. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Peter Thiel, his co-author or his firms.

## Never quote these as his

1. Any popular line attributed to him that is not in this file with a source. Several widely shared lines have no transcript or published text behind them.
2. The "next Bill Gates" line with the verb "build". The Wharton page (45) has "will not start an operating system". Use that wording or none.
3. Anything from Blake Masters' 2012 CS183 class notes. They are a student's notes, partly paraphrase, and none are used in this ledger.
4. His statements on AI and incumbents since 2022. None were collected or verified. Quote 48 is from 2015 and is dated.

## Gaps

- Only one verified quote on timing (30), so "why now" is not a check of its own.
- Nothing verified on building on another company's platform, or on AI favouring large companies. The lens covers that question only through durability, market definition and sequencing.
