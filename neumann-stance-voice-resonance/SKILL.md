# Neumann: Stance, Voice, Resonance

Based on Bernhard Neumann's public method. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bernhard Neumann.

## When to use

Use this skill when an experienced professional needs their public presence to reflect the quality of their actual work. Suitable requests include defining what a name should stand for, developing a recognizable executive voice, turning recurring conversations into LinkedIn posts, and connecting visibility to useful business conversations.

Begin with the person's decisions, experience, and market relevance. The deliverable is a practical system: positioning, topic architecture, voice guidance, interview material, supported drafts, distribution actions, and a measurement loop. Scale it to the request. A single-post assignment needs a small positioning check and source-backed draft, not an unnecessary strategy engagement.

This package reconstructs public practices rather than claiming access to Builderz's internal playbook. Read the evidence boundaries in [references/method.md](references/method.md). Operational prompts, templates, and examples are package adaptations. Do not present them as Neumann's exact wording or proprietary process. Use the source register when explaining attribution.

## Procedure

1. **Establish fit and purpose.** Read the fit rules in [references/method.md](references/method.md#fit-and-purpose). Identify the desired business conversations, intended readers, existing expertise, and current public perception. If the raw material is absent, return discovery questions rather than inventing authority.

2. **Collect usable evidence.** Follow the evidence rules in [references/method.md](references/method.md#evidence-and-boundaries). Gather interview notes, decisions, examples, approved results, and existing writing. Mark unknowns and confidential details explicitly. Keep public method sources separate from the subject's own proof.

3. **Distill Stance.** Use [references/method.md](references/method.md#stance). State the market question this person can credibly own, whose judgment they influence, and why that reader would seek their help. Write one positioning sentence and identify its supporting evidence.

4. **Build the topic architecture.** Follow [references/method.md](references/method.md#topic-architecture). Choose repeatable decision questions that reinforce the position. Separate useful adjacent topics from distractions. Give each proposed pillar an audience tension, an available proof item, and a reason to revisit it.

5. **Align the profile.** Fill the profile fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). Make the headline, About section, and Featured proof support the same market position as the posts. Recommend only changes the brief needs and avoid invented credentials.

6. **Document Voice.** Use [references/method.md](references/method.md#voice). Capture the person's sentence rhythm, preferred expressions, judgment patterns, boundaries, and words they reject. Distinguish observed traits from provisional assumptions and learn from corrections.

7. **Run the weekly conversation.** Use the interview guidance in [references/method.md](references/method.md#weekly-conversation). Ask about actual situations, choices, disagreements, and consequences. Follow up until a defensible judgment emerges. If no conversation is available, list the questions needed to finish the draft.

8. **Turn judgment into drafts.** Apply [references/method.md](references/method.md#editorial-construction) and compare [examples/worked-examples.md](examples/worked-examples.md). Build each post around a market decision, concrete support, and a useful implication. Preserve the speaker's thinking while improving clarity; vary the structure when the material calls for it.

9. **Review and obtain the person's approval.** Run [checklists/review-checklist.md](checklists/review-checklist.md). Check factual accuracy, voice, evidence, disclosure boundaries, and reader value. Hold unsuitable drafts and revise them. Record corrections as updates to the voice profile.

10. **Plan Resonance.** Follow [references/method.md](references/method.md#resonance). Specify relevant discussions, substantive comment opportunities, responses, network development, and a route into appropriate follow-up conversations. Deliver drafts and recommendations; publishing or messaging requires authorization within the user's actual scope.

11. **Measure and refine.** Use [references/method.md](references/method.md#measurement-and-learning) and the review fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). Compare audience relevance, profile interest, and conversation quality over time. Revise weak positioning, voice, or distribution without chasing unrelated reach.

Return a coherent, editable deliverable using the template. Clearly label unresolved facts, illustrative material, approval status, and proposed next actions. A smaller truthful output is preferable to a complete-looking package filled with invented experience.
