Credit: Michael Asshauer and XHAUER for their published Performance-Content-Strategie. This package is an independent paraphrase, with implementation guidance explicitly identified.

All sources below were fetched as public HTML with curl on 2026-10-02. HTTP 200 and substantive readable content were confirmed. Search was used for discovery; source evidence came from the curl responses. Titles are identifiers, not copied teaching passages. No gated downloads, paid course material, newsletter issues behind registration, or existing third-party skills were used.

## Access limits and interpretation

The supplied profile at https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelasshauer/ returned no usable profile text. A regional profile variant returned HTTP 999. They are discovery attempts, not numbered evidence sources. The attempted Sandra Holze episode URL and journal short link /822 returned HTTP 404 and are excluded from evidence. No authentication or access-control bypass was attempted.

LinkedIn relative age labels are unstable and not publication dates. This package records retrieval date and preserves the actual experiment duration. Public pages may be edited after publication. Comments and third-party testimonials are not instructions from Asshauer. Podcast notes support only what those notes say, not an inferred full audio method. Current Campaign Manager functionality and ad eligibility require verification when implementing a campaign.
