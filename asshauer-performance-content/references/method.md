# The public method and its application

## Evidence boundary

The central method combines performance distribution with useful expert content: generate precise contacts, nurture trust and demand, then create sales opportunities. It is a system rather than an isolated ad trick. The agency's public description connects LinkedIn Ads, systematic content, Google remarketing, and webinars. [S1]

Rules below label **Public basis** separately from **Application guidance**. Guidance makes the public method executable but is not presented as a private XHAUER playbook. Numerical examples in the examples file are invented. Implementation details not established by the sources, including bid settings, permissions, form fields, tracking configuration, and sample-size requirements, must be checked or chosen for the user's situation.

## R1. Start with the exact buyer and Trigger-Symptom

**Public basis:** Awareness copy should express a buyer's real pain points and content angles in the audience's language. Trigger-Symptome are recognizable manifestations of the problem, not abstract product categories. [S5]

**Application guidance:** Write a buyer statement containing role, company context, geographic scope, observable symptom, and consequence. Use supplied interviews, sales notes, or evidence. For example, a missed release caused by manual regression review is more actionable than digital transformation. Identify who experiences the pain and who authorizes the purchase. If they differ, choose one primary audience for each asset rather than blending their needs into one vague promise.

## R2. Give the expert a defensible position

**Public basis:** Asshauer's positioning sequence starts with a Hauptbotschaft that challenges the status quo, then establishes Kompetenzvermutung through useful content, increases Mindshare through reuse and ads, builds Zielgruppenbesitz, and invites inbound conversions. [S3]

**Application guidance:** State an opinion plus the reasoning and practical consequence. Do not manufacture controversy or expertise. Kompetenzvermutung means the audience comes to expect competence because it has experienced useful thinking. Require an accountable expert to verify the asset's advice and evidence. The position should connect to the buyer problem, not simply declare the brand an industry leader.

## R3. Capture a reachable audience, then nurture demand

**Public basis:** The demand versus lead article advocates useful content in the ad, additional useful content after registration, early contact capture, and reusable nurturing on an owned contact base. It warns against algorithm dependence and the Content-Hamsterrad of constantly producing new material. It also warns against immediately having sales call newly captured leads. [S7]

**Application guidance:** Zielgruppenbesitz refers here to a maintained, permissioned contact relationship in an email list or CRM, not ownership of people or immunity from platform and delivery constraints. Map distinct jobs:

| Job | Reader's next useful action | Business evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Recognize a symptom or learn a decision rule | Relevant audience consumption |
| Lead generation | Request a practical continuation | Valid, fitting contacts captured |
| Demand nurturing | Apply advice and understand the solution | Replies, meaningful engagement, readiness signals |
| Conversion | Choose a relevant conversation or demo | Accepted opportunities and eventual revenue |

A person can enter at different stages. Someone explicitly requesting a demo can go directly to sales. A document download alone does not justify that classification.

## R4. Turn existing expertise into a useful lead magnet

**Public basis:** Asshauer recommends adapting existing internal checklists, processes, guides, and documents into assets that solve bounded customer problems. Giving useful knowledge away demonstrates the quality of implementation help. [S6]

**Application guidance:** Inventory material the company has permission to share, remove confidential details, and choose one self-contained outcome. The asset should help a suitable buyer do something even without purchasing. Define the title as problem plus useful result; prefer a checklist, guide, or handbook over a generic corporate whitepaper label, matching the public preference. [S7]

The public download catalog names Story-Teach-Tool-Schema. Use that name only for the visible concept: a concrete situation, a useful lesson, and an actionable aid. The gated template was not accessed, so do not claim its exact structure, prompts, or formulas. [S10]

## R5. Show meaningful value before a Document Ad gate

**Public basis:** In the supplied post, Asshauer used his LinkedIn Thought Leader Guide PDF as a Document Ad. Five pages were freely readable, then registration was required. He targeted managing directors and marketing leaders in the German-speaking Mittelstand and tested five ads. He explains the document itself serves as creative and the preview shows people what they will receive. [S2]

**Application guidance:** For an initial five-page design, assign these page jobs:

1. Identify the buyer problem and a concrete learning promise.
2. Explain a recognizable situation and its consequence.
3. Teach a decision rule or diagnostic.
4. Show a usable worked application.
5. Give a small checklist and describe the additional practical material after registration.

This page sequence is a proposed design, not Asshauer's disclosed layout. Provide genuine usable value before the gate and a meaningful continuation afterward. A cover and four sales pages do not meet that requirement. The total document must exceed the preview if gating is to unlock additional pages. Choose another preview length when the content warrants it and current platform controls permit it.

Keep the visible promise, form promise, and delivered asset consistent. Verify mobile readability and preview behavior in the actual ad setup. A short complete checklist may be better ungated with a separate deeper asset for capture.

## R6. Match format to funnel job

**Public basis:** The public Content Recycling talk distinguishes expert text posts amplified through Thought Leader Ads, slides through Document Ads, and short videos for awareness or later conversion. The transcript describes one weekly 15 to 20 minute video adapted into shorter clips, slides, text, audio, and an article. [S4]

**Application guidance:** Thought leadership is a strategic content purpose; Thought Leader Ad is a specific LinkedIn format. A gated Document Ad is not automatically a personal-profile Thought Leader Ad. Do not assume the two share setup requirements or form options. Choose personal expert post amplification when credibility and learning are the objective; choose a document when sequential explanation and, if appropriate, contact capture are the job. Verify eligible post types and required permissions before implementation. Keep paid distribution focused on the requested buyer, rather than chasing inexpensive unrelated impressions.

## R7. Nurture interest into sales readiness

**Public basis:** The MQL to SQL article distinguishes marketing interest from clear buying readiness. Its five levers are helpful expert emails, webinars, performance campaigns, personal LinkedIn conversations, and later telephone follow-up. It favors simple, personal text emails with roughly 90 to 95 percent useful content over pitch-heavy branded newsletters. LinkedIn provides targeting; Google display remarketing can complement conversion-stage campaigns. [S8]

**Application guidance:** Deliver the requested asset immediately, then use a finite, reusable sequence that teaches related decisions and helps apply the material. Each message needs one useful lesson and a proportionate next action. Specify cadence for this audience rather than inventing a universal schedule. Define local MQL and SQL criteria with sales; use observable intent and fit rather than merely counting downloads. Plan a handoff owner and next step for explicit requests.

The Hacksletter landing page demonstrates a weekly practical-content promise and an unsubscribe option; it does not reveal a complete nurture sequence. [S14] Direct messages, calls, ads, and emails are planning options. Send or launch only within the user's authorization and applicable permissions. Asset delivery and ongoing marketing subscription should be clear to the recipient.

## R8. Reuse expert thinking without losing its substance

**Public basis:** The talk demonstrates one source contribution adapted into multiple channels and paid distribution, countering the pattern of publishing once and relying on organic discovery. [S4] A 2022 interview summary also discusses reuse and channels working together, but does not establish a detailed audio workflow. [S12]

**Application guidance:** Preserve the original claim and evidence while changing packaging for each format. A document needs a coherent sequence, a short post needs a focused lesson, and an email needs a useful continuation. Do not require every channel or daily publishing for every client. Select formats the team can maintain and assign an owner. Update reusable assets when the advice becomes obsolete. Repetition should reinforce a buyer decision rather than duplicate the same advertisement endlessly.

## R9. Diagnose Audience, Messaging, and Offer

**Public basis:** Asshauer uses AMO and explicitly credits AJ Wilcox. Audience addresses who sees the ad; Messaging covers copy and creative, including documents; Offer covers the requested next step, which can be a guide, webinar, or conversation. The article recommends consistent continuation between ad and destination. [S9]

**Application guidance:** Treat AMO as a diagnostic heuristic, not proof that tracking, delivery, or integration cannot fail.

| Observation | First diagnostic question | Narrow next change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Few relevant views or weak engagement | Are the audience and symptom aligned? | Check targeting and one hook |
| Relevant interaction but few opt-ins | Is the continuation worth requesting and clearly described? | Improve offer or form clarity |
| Many downloads but few good conversations | Are fit, nurturing, and readiness criteria sound? | Inspect contact quality and follow-up |
| Platform leads missing from CRM | Does the integration deliver records and assets? | Repair delivery before creative testing |

Compare one variable at a time when causal learning matters. Preserve the audience, outcome definition, and attribution window when comparing formats. CTR can suggest resonance; it cannot establish sales quality.

## R10. Treat reported wins as hypotheses

**Public basis:** The Document Ad post reports 14 leads after three days at EUR 35.40 average CPL and EUR 23.15 for the strongest of five ads. It compares them with an asserted DACH average around EUR 140 and predicts further improvement after roughly two weeks. These are the author's reported figures and expectations, not independently verified benchmarks or a guaranteed optimization schedule. [S2]

Case summaries cover different windows and outcomes: PikeTec reports leads and a demo in the first 14 days, while Public Cloud Group reports revenue over four months. They illustrate why cost, progression, and business results must be distinguished. [S11] Public 2026 podcast notes connect thought leadership, lead and demand generation, and webinars, without supplying a universal budget allocation. [S13]

**Application guidance:** Predefine budget, test question, review date, and the maximum loss the user accepts. Five variants are an example, not a mandatory testing count. Do not select a winner from a tiny sample merely because its CPL is lowest. Report counts, period, spend, contact validity, fit, and downstream outcomes. Calculate CPL as spend divided by leads under the stated definition, and cost per qualified lead using the qualifying subset. Mark zero-denominator metrics undefined. Scale only when quality and delivery support the result; pause when the agreed limit is reached or delivery fails.

## R11. Handle edge cases proportionately

- **Too little budget for many cells:** Reduce variants and stages while preserving capture, delivery, and one useful nurture path. This is application guidance.
- **Insufficient retargeting audience:** Keep owned-contact nurturing working; record retargeting as a dependency rather than guaranteeing delivery. This is application guidance.
- **No credible expert material:** Conduct a focused interview or use verified process knowledge. Do not generate fictional authority. This is application guidance derived from R2 and R4.
- **Cheap but unsuitable contacts:** Repair buyer fit and asset specificity before increasing spend. This follows the audience and offer diagnosis. [S9]
- **Long buying cycle:** Review learning and opportunity progression over the stated period. The agency presents a multi-month system, not a promise that every asset produces immediate sales. [S1]
- **An existing nurture system:** Improve the weak junction; avoid rebuilding useful infrastructure solely to match these files. This is application guidance.

## R12. Preserve the method without inventing private rules

Do not reduce the strategy to five preview pages, impose an unsupported posting frequency, claim an ad format always beats another, or equate form completion with an SQL. Keep the named concepts and source credits, but paraphrase their explanation. The reusable deliverable should make the next experiment and buyer experience concrete, while exposing assumptions and present-day platform dependencies. Never reproduce gated course instructions or claim access to XHAUER's private templates.
