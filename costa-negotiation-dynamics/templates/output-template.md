Fill with the user's facts. The finished output is the prep sheet; keep the setting diagnosis, relationship state and package analysis in your head. The preparation questions (limit, target, opening, concession pattern, response to offers) follow Costa's public syllabus; the layout is a package convention.

**Your numbers**

- Target: [ambitious, feasible, and why]
- Opening: [first package and the one-line justification you will give]

**Ask these first** (before proposing anything)
1. "[question]" -> if [answer], then [what you do]
2. ...

**What to trade**
- Give [cheap for you] for [valuable to you], because [why they might prefer it].
- ...

**At the hard moments**
- If they say "[likely push]": "[your line]"
- If it gets tense: "[a question or agenda reset]"

**Don't**
- [two to four specific traps for this situation]

**After the meeting** (optional, one or two lines): what to write down and what to decide next.
