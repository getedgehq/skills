# Sources

All seven sources were fetched with curl and inspected on 2 October 2026. Only public page content and the public course syllabus were used. No book passages, paywalled text or private simulator materials are included. The Observador article was served publicly without authentication and its page metadata identifies it as freely accessible. The package paraphrases the method and uses no direct quotations from source prose.

[S1] Nova SBE. **Professor Luís Almeida Costa won the CEMS Course of the Year Award**. 6 December 2017. https://www.novasbe.unl.pt/en/whats-happening/news/news-detail/id/30/professor-luis-almeida-costa-won-the-cems-course-of-the-year-award

Contribution: Institutional account of the integrated analytical approach and joint development of Negotiation Dynamics: The Game with Ingemar Dierickx.

[S2] CEMS. **Nova wins Course of the Year Award**. Interview with Luís Almeida Costa, 29 November 2015. https://www.cems.org/news-events/news/nova-wins-course-year-award

Contribution: Costa explains evolving relationships, path-dependent information, creative agendas, interpersonal dynamics and feedback in repeated negotiations.

[S3] Luís Almeida Costa. **Negotiation Strategy, CEMS MIM Programme, 1st Semester 2013/2014**. Public course syllabus. https://bo.cems.org/upload/courses/8242.pdf

Contribution: Defines the situation-first analytical scope, distinct preparation questions, auction context and issues in complex agreements; it names topics without supplying universal tactical formulas.

[S4] Nova SBE Executive Education. **What does it take to be an effective negotiator?** 23 January 2017. https://en.blog.exed.novasbe.pt/articles/o-que-e-preciso-para-sermos-negociadores-eficazes

Contribution: Editorial teaching account emphasizing inquiry, pattern recognition, realistic judgment, avoidance of major mistakes and feedback on recurring habits.

[S5] Nova SBE Executive Education. **How to avoid escalating conflicts in a negotiation?** 23 February 2022. https://en.blog.exed.novasbe.pt/articles/como-evitar-o-agravamento-de-conflitos-numa-negociacao

Contribution: Editorial adaptation inspired by Costa's conflict article; explains destructive retaliation, linked rather than isolated issues, respectful communication and specific credibility warnings.

[S6] Luís Almeida Costa. **Negociação e criação de valor**. Observador, 24 May 2021. https://observador.pt/opiniao/negociacao-e-criacao-de-valor/

Contribution: Authored article explaining how different interests and priorities permit value creation and why convincing the counterpart to share one's preferences can miss that opportunity.

[S7] Nova SBE. **Advanced Negotiation: a hands-on and effective approach**. Public program description, accessed 2 October 2026. https://www.novasbe.unl.pt/en/programs/executive-education/open-programs/management-strategy/advanced-negotiation-a-hands-on-and-effective-approach

Contribution: Specifies preparation, negotiation, recording, process evaluation and debrief; the current page describes Deal Maker, jointly developed with Ingemar Dierickx and Galo IT.

## Attribution boundaries

S2 and S6 convey Costa's own interview answers or authored prose. S3 is his public syllabus. S1, S4, S5 and S7 are institutional descriptions or editorial accounts, not transcripts of all his teaching. Cite the editorial warnings as reported guidance rather than presenting them as verbatim statements by Costa. The numbered procedure, brief fields, package labels, severity gates and invented examples are implementation aids derived from these sources.
