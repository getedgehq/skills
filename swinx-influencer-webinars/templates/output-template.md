# Campaign deliverable template

Use the fields below to produce a client-ready plan or retrospective. Replace bracketed values. Mark unknowns explicitly. Label sourced method principles, implementation recommendations, forecast assumptions, and observed results separately. References to [S#] resolve in [sources.md](../references/sources.md).

## 1. Decision and outcome

- **Client and campaign:** [Name, planning or review status, version date]. Guidance: establish the event and document scope.
- **Recommendation:** [Proceed, investigate, revise, or choose another route]. Guidance: state the evidence and dependency behind the decision.
- **Business outcome:** [Qualified registrations, new buyers, requested meetings, or other agreed outcome]. Guidance: do not collapse registration and revenue into one claim.
- **Success criteria:** [Metric, target, time window, owner]. Guidance: distinguish agreed targets from forecast outputs.
- **Open assumptions:** [Unknown input, working assumption, validation owner and date]. Guidance: avoid silently inventing client facts.

## 2. Audience and channel fit

- **Ideal participant:** [Role, seniority, industry, size, geography]. Guidance: describe who should attend and who is outside scope.
- **Buyer problem:** [Concrete decision or difficulty]. Guidance: explain why this audience would spend time attending.
- **LinkedIn fit evidence:** [Observed community activity and sources]. Guidance: verify current relevance rather than treating an old sector list as definitive.
- **Alternative route:** [Fallback and trigger]. Guidance: specify what happens if expert access cannot be established.

## 3. Expert shortlist and booking brief

Repeat for each candidate:

- **Identity and evidence:** [Name, public links, checked date]. Guidance: real candidates require evidence; invented candidates must be labeled.
- **Werdegang and Standing:** [Relevant career and authority evidence]. Guidance: connect experience to the webinar topic.
- **Community-Power and audience fit:** [Buyer interactions, aggregate audience evidence, gaps]. Guidance: distinguish observations from unverified estimates.
- **Role and rationale:** [Speaker, promoter, recorded contributor, or combination]. Guidance: explain the contribution and audience access.
- **Proposed scope:** [Posts, invitation responsibilities, messages, preparation, live or recorded segment]. Guidance: assign clear deliverables without copying a case's volume as a quota.
- **Commercial terms:** [Fee, cost basis, payment, cancellation, rights, conflicts]. Guidance: distinguish proposed from agreed terms and record missing terms.
- **Confirmation:** [Availability, agreement status, approval owner, deadline]. Guidance: provisional names cannot be advertised as confirmed.

## 4. Webinar offer

- **Title:** [Specific buyer problem and learning payoff]. Guidance: avoid unsupported claims or an unrelated trend hook.
- **Learning outcomes:** [Practical decisions or skills]. Guidance: match the promise to the session content.
- **Expert input:** [Actual experience or framework to be supplied]. Guidance: never invent personal anecdotes.
- **Agenda and format:** [Segments, contributors, durations, Q&A]. Guidance: disclose prerecorded contributions accurately.
- **Resource and next step:** [Useful takeaway and optional relevant sales action]. Guidance: preserve educational value.

## 5. Funnel and promotion

- **Registration endpoint:** [URL, platform, required fields, registration definition]. Guidance: specify the verified endpoint separately from LinkedIn event responses.
- **Event-page bridge:** [How interested users complete webinar registration]. Guidance: define the conversion step and owner.
- **Draft assets:** [Post, contextual invitation, personalized message, reminder]. Guidance: keep creator voice credible and one clear action per message.
- **Execution schedule:** [Date, action, owner, approval dependency]. Guidance: fit cadence to available lead time and creator capacity.
- **Tracking and contact handling:** [Source identifiers, deduplication, approved access, retention owner]. Guidance: choose a feasible approach within authorized scope.
- **Confirmation and reminders:** [Event access, calendar details, reminder owner]. Guidance: include date and time zone.

## 6. Budget and scenarios

- **Historical context:** [EUR 41 HR and EUR 47 IT average registration costs reported in S1]. Guidance: identify these as historical agency claims with unspecified methodology.

## 7. Measurement and handoff

- **Actual funnel:** [Event responses, verified registrations, qualified registrations, new qualified contacts, live attendees]. Guidance: preserve separate counts and reporting periods.
- **Quality and novelty:** [Buyer fit, existing-contact comparison, unknowns]. Guidance: newness and qualification are distinct attributes.
- **Sales handoff:** [Resource delivery, participant context, client owner, deadline]. Guidance: respond to actual questions or requests without inventing intent.
- **Sales outcomes:** [Meetings, opportunities, revenue, observation window]. Guidance: separate attributable from influenced outcomes.
- **Limitations:** [Tracking gaps, sample limits, inconsistent source rates]. Guidance: disclose gaps without manufacturing corrections.
- **Next decision:** [Specific change, evidence needed, owner, deadline]. Guidance: explain whether to iterate, scale, or stop.

## 8. Review and execution status

- **Checklist outcome:** [Pass, fail, or unresolved for each relevant gate]. Guidance: use [review-checklist.md](../checklists/review-checklist.md).
- **Dependencies:** [Outstanding confirmation, access, terms, content]. Guidance: distinguish a reviewable plan from a launch-ready campaign.
- **Authorized actions:** [Preparation only or explicitly authorized execution scope]. Guidance: follow the user's actual instructions; a completed template is not permission to publish or spend.
