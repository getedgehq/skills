# Pass/fail review checklist

Apply to a plan, drafts, or a retrospective. Record Pass, Fail, or Unresolved with evidence. Unresolved must not be treated as Pass. A plan can be ready for review with openly documented dependencies; execution requires the dependencies relevant to that action to be resolved.

| Gate | Pass condition | Fail or unresolved condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit and evidence | Attribution is present and [S#] claims match fetched sources | Unsupported tactic or performance claim is attributed to Ruß |
| Evidence boundary | Operating recommendations and assumptions are labeled | Suggested controls are presented as an internal swinx playbook |
| Buyer definition | Role, sector, size, geography, problem, and intended outcome are usable | Audience is simply everyone in B2B |
| Channel fit | Relevant communities and expert access are evidenced | Old sector observations or follower counts substitute for current fit |
| Expert authority | Werdegang, Standing, and topic competence are supported | Celebrity visibility substitutes for expertise |
| Audience quality | Buyer overlap and Community-Power have evidence or explicit gaps | Likes and reach are the only selection evidence |
| Booking status | Role, capacity, timing, deliverables, terms, and confirmation are clear | Availability, fees, or endorsements are invented |
| Educational offer | Title, outcomes, expert input, and agenda address a real buyer problem | A product pitch is disguised as a useful webinar |
| Authenticity | Experience and claims are supplied or confirmed by the expert | Drafts manufacture first-person stories or unsupported superlatives |
| Activation | Posts, relevant invitations, and messages form a coherent path | Indiscriminate contact or unrelated mass tagging is proposed |
| Registration bridge | LinkedIn event response and verified webinar registration are distinct | Event responses are counted automatically as webinar registrations |
| Forecast | Scenario assumptions are visible and defensible | EUR 41 or EUR 47 is used to promise a fixed number of leads |
| Cost basis | Budget inclusions, currency, tax treatment, and denominators are declared | All-in cost is compared silently with advertising-only spend |
| Funnel arithmetic | Aligned cohorts, deduplication, correct ratios, and zero handling | Counts or cohorts are mixed; undefined ratios become zero |
| Quality and novelty | Qualified, new, and live counts are tracked separately | Every registration is called a new sales-qualified lead |
| Delivery and handoff | Owners, reminders, expert contribution, and follow-up are assigned | Registrations are generated without an attendance or sales handoff plan |
| Attribution | Sales outcomes and uncertainty are reported separately | Every later sale is credited to the campaign |
| Execution scope | Proposed actions fit actual authorization and approved access | Drafting is taken as authority to publish, contact, or spend |

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure mode | Practical fix |
| --- | --- |
| Largest creator selected automatically | Re-rank by relevant authority and buyer access; document missing evidence |
| Older industry assessment treated as a permanent ban | Check current communities and candidates; explain whether fit is verified |
| Expert agrees to a post but not invitations or speaking | Clarify the role and feasible deliverables before forecasting activation |
| Creator cannot attend live | Confirm a disclosed recorded role, revise the date, or choose another expert |
| Client demands scripted endorsement copy | Build an educational promise and seek actual expert input |
| Mass tags replace targeting | Remove unrelated tags; address relevant network members contextually |
| Hundreds of event responses, few platform registrations | Inspect the registration bridge and report both stages |
| Cost per registration looks good but audience is wrong | Evaluate qualified-registration cost and actual buyer fit |
| Repeated contacts inflate apparent acquisition | Deduplicate and compare against the client's existing-contact definition |
| Published figures do not reconcile | Preserve reported values with a limitation; calculate local metrics from verified local counts |
| Strong attendance but little sales progression | Inspect topic relevance, participant requests, and handoff before adding promotion volume |
| Prospecting plugin or private network export assumed available | Use approved evidence and access; record the unavailable capability as a dependency |
