# Pass/fail review

Use this before handing off a decision brief. The checklist is a package adaptation, with relevant inspiration cited through [the source register](../references/sources.md). Mark each applicable row PASS or FAIL and attach a short reason. Mark a row N/A only when the assignment makes it irrelevant, with that reason recorded. A field that merely exists does not pass if its content is unsupported.

## Quality gates

## Failure modes and fixes

- **Deck without a decision:** Name the choice, today's approver, and the smallest output needed. If approval is unavailable, hand off a recommendation with an explicit dependency.
- **Communication blamed for positioning:** Test the underlying audience, promise, and proof. Repair the position before spending time on the slogan.
- **One sentence used as a compression trick:** Simplify the strategic choice itself. A short sentence full of vague claims is still unclear.
- **Audience drift disguised as premiumization:** Compare current and desired customers, then identify the offer changes and transition costs. Visual ambition alone does not prove demand.
- **Taste becomes personal preference:** Define creative criteria and compare plausible directions against the same promise. Explain why the selected details fit.
- **A copied wellness or heritage look:** Remove borrowed codes and recover details from the organization's own offer, people, and customer context.
- **Quiet applied universally:** Check whether the chosen audience wants rest, social energy, or something else. Remove stimulation only when it advances the promise.
- **Brand stops at the identity:** Add relevant service, product, channel, and team requirements. Mark capability gaps before making a public promise.
- **Ethical tension disappears behind attractive work:** Separate useful creative observations from endorsement and assess implications material to the brand's claims.
- **Source overreach:** Replace an unsupported attribution with an explicit adaptation label. Project credits establish roles, not private methods or measured effectiveness.

## Handoff decision

If authority, evidence honesty, attribution, or an essential delivery requirement fails, do not describe the strategy as approved or ready for execution. Continue the authorized work, mark the brief provisional, and name the input or decision needed. This is a document-quality rule, not an extra permission requirement for drafting or analysis.

If other applicable gates fail, repair the brief or record the unresolved tradeoff with a proposed test. A pass means the brief is coherent and usable for its stated scope. It does not establish market success, predict revenue, or imply Sophia Marie Fehr's endorsement.
