# Brand and creative strategy decision brief

This is a package adaptation inspired by Sophia Marie Fehr's public principles and meeting diagnostics. It is not an official FEHR document. Replace bracketed fields with evidence-backed content. Keep untested items visibly provisional. For a narrow assignment, mark irrelevant fields as not applicable with a reason instead of expanding scope.

## 1. Recommendation and decision status

**Recommended choice:** [Specific action or strategic direction.]

**Reason:** [The business tension and why this choice addresses it.]

**Status:** [Approved / recommendation pending approval / exploratory.]

**Decision owner and remit:** [Named person or role, what they can decide, and remaining dependencies.]

Guidance: Lead with the actionable choice. Do not use approved unless actual authorization exists. A meeting participant's job title does not prove their mandate. Distinguish choosing a direction from approving expenditure or publication. See [method: Three meeting questions](../references/method.md#three-meeting-questions).

## 3. Three diagnostic answers

These are paraphrases of the verified questions in [S2], not exact quotations.

**Authority:** [Who can decide this today? What decision becomes possible or remains pending?]

**Newcomer clarity:** [Could a new employee summarize the position in one sentence? Proposed sentence, actual test result if conducted, and required repair.]

**Customer horizon:** [Does this decision serve existing customers or desired future customers? Name the group, evidence, and consequences.]

Guidance: Each answer must change or confirm a choice. If no newcomer test was run, label the assessment provisional. If audiences differ, describe a transition rather than blending them into an idealized persona.
