# Bescheide, deadlines, appeals, surcharges, paying

## Read the Bescheid first

Photographed letters lose pages ("Fortsetzung siehe Seite 3"). Get every page before acting. Extract:
date of the Bescheid, addressee and "für" (person or company), Steuernummer/Ordnungskriterium,
Steuerart and period, assessed amount, Vorauszahlungen credited, amount due and due date,
Vorbehalt der Nachprüfung (§ 164 AO) or vorläufig (§ 165 AO), Verspätungszuschlag, and the
Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung. Distinguish an internal processing date printed on the letter from a
filing deadline: misreading one for the other nearly put a false statement into an Einspruch.

A "Bestätigung der Festsetzung" from ELSTER is not a Bescheid; it has no numbers and means the
Finanzamt accepted your declared figures (§ 168 AO).

## Einspruch deadline

1. **Bekanntgabe**: a letter posted in Germany counts as received on the **4th day after posting**
   (§ 122 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 AO), and an electronically provided Bescheid on the 4th day after the
   notification (§ 122a Abs. 4 AO). This is the rule for documents posted from 01.01.2025; before
   that it was 3 days. If the 4th day is a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, it moves to the next
   working day (§ 108 Abs. 3 AO).
2. **Einspruchsfrist**: one month from Bekanntgabe (§ 355 Abs. 1 AO), ending on the same-numbered
   day of the next month; if that day is a weekend or holiday, the next working day.
3. **Exception for your own Steueranmeldung** (UStVA, annual USt return): the month runs from the
   day the filing reaches the Finanzamt, or, if it leads to a refund and needs the Finanzamt's
   consent, from Bekanntgabe of that consent (§ 355 Abs. 1 Satz 2 AO). Do not add 4 days here.
4. If the letter actually arrived later than the fiction, the later date counts, and the Finanzamt
   carries the burden of proof when receipt is disputed. Keep the envelope.

Worked example (dates illustrative): Bescheid dated Tuesday 09.06.2026 → 4th day Saturday
13.06. → Bekanntgabe Monday 15.06. → deadline Wednesday 15.07.2026. With the old 3-day rule you
would have computed Friday 12.06. → Sunday 12.07. → Monday 13.07. and filed against the wrong date.
Always show the computation in the working file.

Missed it? Wiedereinsetzung (§ 110 AO) only without fault, within one month after the obstacle
ends. A Bescheid under Vorbehalt der Nachprüfung can still be changed on application (§ 164 Abs. 2
AO) after the deadline.

## Einspruch mechanics

- File via Mein ELSTER "Einspruch" (has an AdV option) or "Sonstige Nachricht", in writing, never
  only by phone.
- Bescheid addressed to the managing director "für" the company: write expressly "namens und in
  Vertretung der [Gesellschaft] lege ich als Geschäftsführer Einspruch ein".
- Name each Bescheid separately (KSt and Soli, GewSt-**Messbescheid**, Zinsen, Verspätungszuschlag).
- **Einspruch zur Fristwahrung**: if evidence is not ready, file before the deadline with
  "Begründung folgt" and a date. A short Einspruch that only states facts you can prove beats a
  detailed one that asserts things you cannot.
- In a case where the Finanzamt mentions § 371 AO (Selbstanzeige) or possible Steuerstraftat, keep
  the Einspruch minimal and get a Steuerberater before writing anything substantive.
- A change Bescheid issued during the Einspruch becomes part of it (§ 365 Abs. 3 AO).

## Verböserung

An Einspruch reopens the whole assessment (§ 367 Abs. 2 Satz 1 AO). The Finanzamt may change it to
your disadvantage only after warning you and giving you the chance to withdraw (§ 367 Abs. 2
Satz 2 AO). Before filing, list what the Finanzamt could pick up if it looks again (questionable
expenses, undeclared related revenue, inventory) and weigh it against the claimed saving. If a
warning arrives and the downside is larger, withdraw the Einspruch (Rücknahme, § 362 AO).
Note: a claim worth a few euros is not worth reopening a file with known soft spots.

## Aussetzung der Vollziehung (AdV)

- An Einspruch **does not suspend payment** (§ 361 Abs. 1 AO). Apply for AdV (§ 361 Abs. 2 AO)
  with the disputed amount per tax, citing "ernstliche Zweifel an der Rechtmäßigkeit".
- For GewSt: apply for AdV of the Messbescheid; the GewSt-Bescheid follows (§ 361 Abs. 3 AO).
- Cost if you lose: Aussetzungszinsen 0.5 % per full month (§ 237, § 238 AO) on the amount rounded
  down to 50 EUR; interest under 10 EUR is not charged (§ 239 Abs. 2 AO) *(verify rate)*. Do not
  confuse with the 0.15 %/month rate of § 233a.
- For small amounts AdV is close to free, and it stops Säumniszuschläge while it runs.

## Schätzungsbescheid (missing return)

- Issued when a Voranmeldung or return was not filed. Usually under Vorbehalt der Nachprüfung, with
  an estimate set high on purpose.
- Fix: file the real Voranmeldung/return; it replaces the estimate. If the Einspruch deadline is
  close and the real filing is not ready, file an Einspruch + AdV to keep the case open.
- Check first whether something was filed under a different Steuernummer; cite the Transferticket
  and ask for reassignment rather than asserting it was "filed on time" unless that is proven.
- Verspätungszuschlag in the estimate: if the underlying assessment changes, the surcharge must be
  adjusted (§ 152 Abs. 12 AO); ask for it explicitly.

## Herabsetzung der Vorauszahlungen

- Prepayments for ESt/KSt (§ 37 Abs. 3 Satz 3 EStG, for KSt via § 31 KStG) and GewSt (§ 19 Abs. 3
  GewStG) can be adjusted on application during the year and up to the end of the 15th month after
  the year (§ 37 Abs. 3 Satz 3 EStG); there is no Einspruch deadline. The Finanzamt wants a plausible
  forecast: revenue to date, costs to date, expected rest of year.
- In the city states one letter to the Finanzamt covers KSt and GewSt; elsewhere the municipality
  sets GewSt prepayments from the Finanzamt's Messbetrag.
- Send before the next due date (typically 10.03./10.06./10.09./10.12. for ESt/KSt, 15.02./15.05./
  15.08./15.11. for GewSt). Template in `templates.md`.

## Annual return deadlines

- Without a tax adviser: 31 July of the following year (§ 149 Abs. 2 AO). With an adviser: the
  end of February of the second following year (§ 149 Abs. 3 AO) *(verify per year; the Corona
  extensions have ended)*.
- Fristverlängerung on application before the deadline, with a reason.
- Personal ESt without filing obligation (Antragsveranlagung): 4 years, see
  `personal-income-tax.md`.

## Verspätungszuschlag (§ 152 AO)

- Discretionary in general (§ 152 Abs. 1). **Mandatory** if an annual return is filed later than
  14 months after the end of the tax year (§ 152 Abs. 2 Nr. 1; e.g. 2025 returns after
  28.02.2027) *(verify)*.
- Mandatory surcharge does not apply, among others, if the assessed tax is 0 or a refund, or does
  not exceed the prepayments and withholding taxes (§ 152 Abs. 3).
- Amount: 0.25 % of the assessed tax minus prepayments and withheld taxes per started month, at
  least 10 EUR per month, for annual returns at least 25 EUR per month (§ 152 Abs. 5) *(verify)*.
- For a dormant company, filing a zero return on time costs nothing and avoids all of this.

## Säumniszuschlag (§ 240 AO)

- 1 % per **started** month of late payment, on the overdue tax rounded down to a multiple of
  50 EUR. Amounts under 50 EUR cost nothing.
- Three-day grace period for bank transfers (§ 240 Abs. 3); none for cash or cheque.
- Applies to taxes, not to the surcharges themselves.
- Many Finanzämter state that tiny balances (for example under 3 EUR, or Säumniszuschläge under
  5 EUR) can be paid with the next payment. Follow what the letter says.

## Paying

- Pay to the bank account printed on the Bescheid (in some cities the "Steuerkasse").
- **Verwendungszweck**: Steuernummer, Steuerart, period, for example
  `12/345/67890 USt 3VJ 2026` or the Kassenzeichen if the letter gives one. Wrong or missing
  references produce Mahnungen while the money sits unassigned.
- Refunds arrive referencing the Steuernummer that **carries** that tax, which reveals which number
  the Finanzamt uses for it.
- Request a Kontoauszug (Steuerkonto statement) from the Finanzamt when you cannot tell what was
  paid; a personal ELSTER certificate may not show the company's Steuerkonto.
