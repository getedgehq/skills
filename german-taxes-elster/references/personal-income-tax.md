# The founder's own Einkommensteuer

## Must I file, and by when?

Pflichtveranlagung for employees (§ 46 Abs. 2 EStG) is triggered among others by: two employers at
the same time (one on Steuerklasse VI), other income over 410 EUR, wage-replacement benefits over
410 EUR (Progressionsvorbehalt), Steuerklassen III/V or IV with factor *(verify the full list)*.
Self-employed people generally have to file (§ 25 Abs. 3 EStG with the thresholds of § 56 EStDV);
an employee's side income of 410 EUR or less does not by itself create a duty.

- Pflicht, no adviser: 31 July of the following year. With adviser: end of February of the second
  following year *(verify per year)*.
- No obligation: **freiwillige Antragsveranlagung**, possible for 4 years (Festsetzungsfrist,
  § 169 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 AO, no Anlaufhemmung): the 2025 return can be filed until 31.12.2029. Usually
  worth it when someone was employed only part of the year (refund of over-withheld Lohnsteuer).
- Do not guess the trigger. Check the Lohnsteuerbescheinigungen (ELSTER "Belegabruf" prefills them)
  before telling someone they are late.

Company money is not personal income: revenue of the founder's UG is the UG's. Personal tax only
sees salary, distributions (Abgeltungsteuer, or Teileinkünfteverfahren on application), interest
on shareholder loans, and disposals of shares.

## Abfindung and the Fünftelregelung (§ 34 EStG)

- The reduced rate needs **Zusammenballung**: the whole compensation flows in one calendar year
  (several instalments within the same year are fine). A tail in the next year breaks it unless it
  is a minor part; the BFH accepted a tail just under 10 % of the main payment and rejected one of
  about 12.5 % *(verify current case law)*.
- **Since 01.01.2025 the employer may no longer apply the Fünftelregelung in the Lohnsteuer
  deduction** (Wachstumschancengesetz). Lohnsteuer is withheld at the normal rate; the benefit only
  comes through the **Einkommensteuererklärung** for that year. So the return is not optional
  paperwork; it is the only way to get the money.
- Read the signed agreement, not a planning document: the payment schedule in the contract decides.
  Then check actual receipts against it. If instalments slip into January, the benefit is at risk;
  pull the last payment forward.
- If the employer withheld Lohnsteuer on the full amount at signing while only part was paid out,
  find out why (inflow by offset against a debt? error?) before assuming either outcome.

## Founder moving abroad

Shares of at least 1 % in a corporation trigger exit taxation on unrealised gains when German tax
residence ends (§ 6 AStG), with deferral rules that changed in 2022 *(verify)*. Where the company
is managed from also decides where the company is taxed (§ 10 AO place of management). Flag both
to a Steuerberater before the move, not after.

## Shareholder in more than one company

Keep one working folder per taxpayer. Expenses paid by the founder for company A must not end up in
company B's books; check the addressee of every invoice. In the source run, invoices addressed to a
separate foreign company of the founder were found in another company's folder and had to be
excluded.
