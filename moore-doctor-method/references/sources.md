# Public sources

All nine pages below were fetched in full with curl on 2026-10-02 from Richard Moore's public website. No paywalled material or book text was used. Source titles are bibliographic metadata; method explanations are paraphrases. Citations support published guidance, not invented examples or independently verified performance claims.

1. [S1] Richard Moore, **The Doctor Method: A New Approach to Sales**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/the-doctor-method-a-new-approach-to-sales
   Contributes the core expert positioning, diagnosis, tailored prescription, warning against excessive choices, and post-sale follow-up.
2. [S2] Richard Moore, **Choosing the Third Way: The Doctor Method for LinkedIn Success**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/choosing-the-third-way-the-doctor-method-for-linkedin-success
   Contributes the balance of helpful problem-focused content and a clear next action, with conversion prioritized over attention alone.
3. [S3] Richard Moore, **How to Write Content That Attracts Buyers, Not Just Peers**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/how-to-write-content-that-attracts-buyers-not-just-peers
   Contributes buyer language, needs, and relevance, plus warnings about peer applause and seller revenue boasting.
4. [S4] Richard Moore, **Revolutionizing Sales with a Human-Centric Approach for Better Results**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/revolutionizing-sales-with-a-human-centric-approach-for-better-results
   Contributes connection and trust before an offer, with warnings about pressure and volume-first outreach.
5. [S5] Richard Moore, **How to Convert LinkedIn Connections into Meaningful Interactions (Without Being Salesy)**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/how-to-convert-linkedin-connections-into-meaningful-interactions-without-being-salesy
   Contributes contextual conversation openings, thoughtful use of quiet engagement signals, and a short call after chat momentum develops.
6. [S6] Richard Moore, **Selling Outcomes, Not Products: The Secret to Sales That Convert**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/selling-outcomes-not-products-the-secret-to-sales-that-convert
   Contributes translating features into buyer outcomes and explaining a supported before-and-after; its unsupported purchase percentage is not adopted.
7. [S7] Richard Moore, **Reframing Objections in Sales: A New Perspective**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/reframing-objections-in-sales-a-new-perspective
   Contributes objections as feedback, active listening, acknowledgment, and a relationship-focused response instead of argument.
8. [S8] Richard Moore, **Why Automation Might Be Killing Your High-Ticket Sales**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/why-automation-might-be-killing-your-high-ticket-sales
   Contributes personal interaction and reduced scheduling friction for substantial services, while allowing automation for lower-priced offers and greater volume.
9. [S9] Richard Moore, **Why Your Clients Aren't Buying and Why It's Not About Price**. https://www.therichardmoore.com/blog/why-your-clients-arent-buying--and-why-its-not-about-price
   Contributes trust, clear understanding, relevant evidence, and warnings against reflexive discounting; its broad claims about price are treated as advice rather than universal facts.
