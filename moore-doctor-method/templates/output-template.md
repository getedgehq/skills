# Sales asset or consultation deliverable

Use this working template to produce the asset requested. Replace bracketed fields, remove irrelevant sections, and do not deliver empty placeholders. Keep preparation notes separate from buyer-facing copy. The field labels are agent adaptations of diagnosis, prescription, outcome-focused communication, and follow-through. [S1][S2][S6]

## 1. Assignment and buyer picture

- **Requested asset:** [Post / outreach message / discovery plan / recommendation / objection response / follow-up.]
  Guidance: Choose the actual user request; do not automatically produce every asset.
- **Audience and situation:** [Specific buyer role and recognizable context.]
  Guidance: Use the buyer's language rather than a broad industry label. [S3]
- **Relationship stage and channel:** [Public reader / new contact / engaged conversation / consultation / existing client; channel.]
  Guidance: This determines how much commitment the next action can reasonably request. [S5]
- **Confirmed facts:** [Facts supplied by the user or buyer.]
  Guidance: Identify whose observation each fact represents when that distinction matters.
- **Working assumptions:** [Assumptions needed to draft.]
  Guidance: Label assumptions visibly; do not turn them into established buyer facts.
- **Material unknowns:** [Missing information that could change the recommendation.]
  Guidance: Ask for it, make the recommendation conditional, or propose a bounded diagnostic step.

## 2. Problem, outcome, and credibility

- **Observable symptom:** [What the buyer sees happening.]
  Guidance: Write the observation before naming a cause. [S1]
- **Desired outcome:** [Change that matters to the buyer and how they would recognize it.]
  Guidance: Explain the significance of the change; do not substitute offer components for outcomes. [S6]
- **Leading explanation:** [Possible cause and evidence supporting it.]
  Guidance: In public content this is a hypothesis. In a recommendation, indicate how discovery supports it.
- **Competing explanation or uncertainty:** [What else could produce the symptom; what is not yet known.]
  Guidance: Include the uncertainty that could alter the proposed course, not an exhaustive list.
- **Relevant proof:** [Approved case / verified experience / transparent diagnostic reasoning.]
  Guidance: Include context and limits. If none exists, state that internally and avoid invented buyer-facing proof. [S1][S9]

## 4. Next step and results check

- **Next action:** [One action and its purpose.]
  Guidance: Match it to buyer readiness and the actual task.
- **Owner and timing:** [Who acts; confirmed timing or proposed timing.]
  Guidance: Distinguish an agreement from a proposal. Do not invent appointments.
- **Buying path:** [Reply / assisted scheduling / booking link / self-service purchase.]
  Guidance: Reduce buyer effort while respecting preference, offer complexity, and capacity. [S8]
- **Progress indicator:** [Relevant conversation, decision, or service outcome.]
  Guidance: Use a baseline and review point when appropriate. Views alone do not establish conversion. [S1][S2][S3]
- **Revision condition:** [Evidence that would change the diagnosis or plan.]
  Guidance: Make a useful stopping or adjustment condition explicit when recommending work.
