# Deliverable skeletons

Pick the skeleton for what the user asked for. Fill it with their facts; leave out any section the request does not need. Headings are suggestions, not required labels. Never add sections for evidence status, assumptions, validation or review: those stay in your head. These layouts are package conventions; the logic inside them follows [S2] and [S3].

## 90-day (or 30/60/90) sales plan

**Goal for the 90 days:** [one line: the proof this period should produce for this company, e.g. first paid customers outside the founder's network who reach a confirmed result.]

**Who to sell to:** [observable conditions that make an account a fit, the user / champion / budget owner, the trigger that makes it urgent, who to skip.]

**The message:** [two or three sentences: today's workflow, its cost, why current options fall short, what changes with the product. Only claims the input supports.]

**Days 1-30:** [specific actions and weekly volumes the founder can sustain alongside everything else; exit signal for the phase.]
**Days 31-60:** [...]
**Days 61-90:** [...]

**Discovery questions:** [5 to 8, starting with how the work happens today; one line on what to do if there is no pain.]

**What to track:** [a few fields per deal and the 3 to 4 numbers to review weekly.]

**Day-90 decision:** [what result means keep going, change the target, or start testing a hire.]

## Playbook

Same building blocks as the plan, arranged by the sales motion instead of by time: target and disqualifiers, the argument, outreach, discovery with branches, demo flow, commitment ask per stage, handoff to implementation, deal record, when to hire.

## Outreach email

- Subject: [specific to their situation, under 7 words]
- Body (under 120 words): [a verified fact or a plainly stated hunch about their workflow] -> [the problem it likely causes] -> [what the product changes, only supported claims] -> [ask for a short conversation about how they handle it today].

## Discovery call plan

Objective of the call; opening question about the current process; 3 to 5 branches (current tool works / manual workaround / pain but low priority / champion without budget); problem summary to confirm; which workflow to demo; the specific next step to ask for.

## Demo outline

3 to 5 beats, each tied to a problem the buyer confirmed, ending with the commercial next step.
