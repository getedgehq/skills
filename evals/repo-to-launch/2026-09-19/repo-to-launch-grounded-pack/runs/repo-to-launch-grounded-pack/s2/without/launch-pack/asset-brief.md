# QuietQueue launch asset brief

## Objective

Create a restrained launch visual that communicates a local-first macOS menu bar reading queue without implying features or distribution details that the repository does not establish.

## Required copy

- Product name: QuietQueue
- Descriptor: Local-first reading queue for macOS
- Version/status: 0.3.0 · unsigned beta
- Feature line: Save from clipboard · Tag locally · Export as JSON

## Visual direction

Use a clean, quiet composition suited to a focused utility. A simple wordmark, link/bookmark motif, and subtle menu-bar framing are appropriate. Keep typography readable at social-card size and leave generous negative space.

## Factual guardrails

- Refer to QuietQueue as a macOS menu bar app.
- Show only the three verified capabilities: clipboard URL saving, local tags, and JSON export.
- If limitations appear, use: no accounts, no cloud sync, and no browser extension.
- Do not fabricate product UI or present a conceptual mockup as the real interface.
- Do not add numerical performance, usage, adoption, ranking, review, or commercial claims.
- Do not imply a shipping channel, installation method, integration, device support, or availability beyond what the repository states.
- Do not use third-party logos.

## Deliverable suggestion

One text-led 1600×900 launch card. If a real product capture is supplied later, it may be placed in the composition unchanged and labeled with the matching version. Until then, keep the asset illustrative and non-UI.

## Source basis

All copy comes from the repository README, package metadata, and the feature identifiers in `src/app.js`.
