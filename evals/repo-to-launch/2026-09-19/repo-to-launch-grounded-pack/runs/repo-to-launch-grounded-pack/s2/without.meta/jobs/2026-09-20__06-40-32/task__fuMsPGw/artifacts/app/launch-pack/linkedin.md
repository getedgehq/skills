I’m sharing QuietQueue 0.3.0, a small, local-first reading queue for macOS.

QuietQueue lives in the menu bar and keeps its current scope focused:

- Save a URL from the clipboard
- Tag saved links locally
- Export the queue as JSON

This is an unsigned beta, not a finished release. It currently has no accounts, cloud sync, or browser extension.

The goal of this version is straightforward: provide a focused place to keep links for later without turning the workflow into a larger service.
