# Show HN: QuietQueue – a local-first reading queue for the macOS menu bar

QuietQueue 0.3.0 is a macOS menu bar app that stores links for later and opens them in a focused reading queue.

The implementation currently exposes three verified capabilities: saving a URL from the clipboard, tagging saved links locally, and exporting the queue as JSON.

It is an unsigned beta. It has no account system, cloud sync, or browser extension, and I don’t have public benchmark results to share. The scope at this stage is a small, local-first link queue rather than a hosted reading service.
