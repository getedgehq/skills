I’m sharing QuietQueue, a local-first reading queue for macOS.

It lives in the menu bar and keeps the workflow deliberately small:

- Save a URL from the clipboard
- Tag saved links locally
- Export the queue as JSON

QuietQueue is currently an unsigned beta. It does not have accounts, cloud sync, or a browser extension.

The goal is a focused place to hold links for later reading, with local organization and a portable JSON export.
