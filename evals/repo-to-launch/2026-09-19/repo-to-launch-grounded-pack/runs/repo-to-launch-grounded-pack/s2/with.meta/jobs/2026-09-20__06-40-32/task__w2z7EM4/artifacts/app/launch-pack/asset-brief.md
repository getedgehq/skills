# QuietQueue launch asset brief

## Objective

Create a restrained launch image that communicates one concrete workflow: save a clipboard URL, organize it with local tags, and retain the option to export the queue as JSON.

## Required facts

- Product name: QuietQueue
- Product type: local-first macOS menu bar reading queue
- Release status: unsigned beta
- Supported actions: save a URL from the clipboard, tag saved links locally, and export the queue as JSON

## Recommended composition

Use a clean macOS-oriented layout with “QuietQueue” as the primary text and “A local-first reading queue for your menu bar” as the supporting line. A simple three-step text treatment may read: “Clipboard URL → Local tags → JSON export.” Keep any window or menu-bar treatment abstract unless it is captured from the real running product.

## Visual direction

Quiet, focused, and utility-led. Use generous space, strong legibility, and a compact queue motif. Avoid service logos or imagery that implies online syncing, account profiles, or browser integration.

## Asset integrity

No screenshots are present in the repository. Do not fabricate a product UI or present a mock interface as a real screenshot. If a real build becomes available, capture its actual menu-bar and reading-queue states before adding interface imagery. Until then, use typography and abstract shapes only.

## Suggested deliverables

- Social card: 1200 × 630 px
- Square social image: 1080 × 1080 px
- Product Hunt gallery image: 1270 × 760 px

The size list is a production request, not evidence that any asset or listing already exists.
