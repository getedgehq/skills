I’m sharing QuietQueue 0.3.0, a local-first macOS menu bar app for keeping links to read later.

The current beta has a deliberately small workflow:

- Save a URL from the clipboard
- Organize saved links with local tags
- Export the queue as JSON

QuietQueue is currently an unsigned beta. It does not have accounts, cloud sync, or a browser extension, and there are no public benchmark results.

The focus at this stage is a straightforward, local-first reading queue—not a promise of a larger platform.
