I’m sharing QuietQueue 0.3.0, a local-first reading queue for macOS.

QuietQueue lives in the menu bar and keeps its current scope deliberately narrow: save a URL from the clipboard, organize saved links with local tags, and export the queue as JSON.

This release is an unsigned beta. It does not include accounts, cloud sync, or a browser extension.

If a small, local-first queue fits the way you set links aside for later, QuietQueue is built around that workflow.
