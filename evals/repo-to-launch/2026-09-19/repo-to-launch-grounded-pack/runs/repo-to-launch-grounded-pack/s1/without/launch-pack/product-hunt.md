# QuietQueue

## Tagline

A local-first reading queue in your macOS menu bar

## Short description

Save a clipboard URL, tag links locally, and export your queue as JSON.

## Maker comment

QuietQueue 0.3.0 is a focused macOS menu bar app for keeping links to read later.

The current beta does three things: it saves a URL from the clipboard, lets you tag saved links locally, and exports the queue as JSON. The product is local-first and requires no account.

This is an unsigned beta, with no cloud sync or browser extension. Those boundaries are part of the current release, so evaluate it as a small local reading queue rather than a cross-device service.
