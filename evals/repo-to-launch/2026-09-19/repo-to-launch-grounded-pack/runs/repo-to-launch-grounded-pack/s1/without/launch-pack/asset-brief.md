# QuietQueue launch asset brief

## Objective

Create a factual visual companion for QuietQueue 0.3.0 that communicates a small, local-first macOS reading queue without implying unverified product UI or functionality.

## Recommended deliverable

One clean launch card in a landscape social format, plus crops suited to each publishing channel. Use typography and simple abstract shapes only. Do not present a fabricated interface, device mockup, or screenshot.

## Required copy

- Product name: QuietQueue
- Descriptor: Local-first reading queue for the macOS menu bar
- Capability line: Save from clipboard · Tag locally · Export as JSON
- Status label: Unsigned beta

## Visual direction

Keep the layout quiet, compact, and reading-oriented. A restrained neutral palette with one accent color can suggest focus without making a product claim. Use a simple queue or bookmark motif only as decorative abstraction, not as a representation of the actual interface.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Do not invent or reconstruct a product screenshot.
- Do not imply mobile, web, Windows, cross-device, or browser-based availability.
- Do not depict account profiles, sync states, extension controls, collaboration, analytics, or other unsupported features.
- Do not add ratings, usage figures, performance comparisons, publication logos, or endorsement badges.
- Do not imply signed or store-based distribution; label the release as an unsigned beta wherever release status appears.

## Source basis

The copy is limited to repository-verified statements in `README.md`, `package.json`, and `src/app.js`: macOS menu bar format, local-first positioning, version 0.3.0, clipboard URL capture, local tags, JSON export, unsigned beta status, and the stated missing account, sync, and extension features.
