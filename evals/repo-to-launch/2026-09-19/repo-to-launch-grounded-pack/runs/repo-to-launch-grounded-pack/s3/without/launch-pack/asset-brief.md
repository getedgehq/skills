# QuietQueue launch asset brief

## Objective

Create a restrained launch visual that explains QuietQueue as a local-first macOS menu bar reading queue without implying unverified product behavior or release availability.

## Recommended asset

A 1600 × 900 editorial graphic suitable for social posts and listing headers. Use a clean macOS-adjacent visual language, but do not reproduce an interface that is not present in the repository.

## Content hierarchy

1. Product name: “QuietQueue”
2. Headline: “A local-first reading queue for your Mac”
3. Three factual capability labels:
   - “Save from clipboard”
   - “Tag locally”
   - “Export as JSON”
4. Status label: “0.3.0 · unsigned beta”

## Visual direction

- Calm, minimal composition with ample negative space.
- Represent the workflow abstractly with a clipboard symbol, a tag symbol, and a JSON document symbol feeding into a simple queue.
- Use original vector-style shapes and typography rather than fabricated product UI.
- Keep the platform reference textual (“macOS” or “Mac”); do not add third-party service logos.
- Ensure the status label remains legible at social-feed size.

## Required caveats

If the asset includes supporting copy, use only: “No accounts. No cloud sync. No browser extension.” Do not imply signing, a documented distribution channel, performance results, or features beyond the three verified capabilities.

## Source-material constraint

No screenshots or other visual assets are supplied in the repository. This brief therefore calls for an illustrative launch graphic, not a screenshot, device mockup, or reconstructed interface.

## Deliverables

- 1600 × 900 master graphic
- 1200 × 630 social crop
- Editable source with text layers preserved
- Alt text: “QuietQueue launch graphic showing clipboard saving, local tags, and JSON export; version 0.3.0 unsigned beta.”
