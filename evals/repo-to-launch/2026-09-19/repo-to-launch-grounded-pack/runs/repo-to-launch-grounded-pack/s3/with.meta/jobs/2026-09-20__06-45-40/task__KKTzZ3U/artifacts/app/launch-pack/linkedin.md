I’m sharing QuietQueue 0.3.0, a small, local-first reading queue for the macOS menu bar.

Its workflow is deliberately focused: save a URL from the clipboard, organize saved links with local tags, and export the queue as JSON.

QuietQueue is currently an unsigned beta. It does not include accounts, cloud synchronization, or a browser add-on. That makes the present scope narrow and transparent—and keeps the queue local.

If your “read later” habit starts with copying links, I’d be interested to hear how this workflow fits the way you collect them.
