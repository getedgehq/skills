# QuietQueue

## Tagline

A local-first reading queue in your macOS menu bar

## Short description

Save clipboard URLs, tag links locally, and export your queue as JSON.

## First comment

QuietQueue 0.3.0 explores a focused way to keep links for later without adding an account to the workflow.

The current beta does three things: it saves a URL from the clipboard, lets you tag saved links locally, and exports the queue as JSON.

This is an unsigned beta, not a finished distribution release. There is no account system, cloud synchronization, or browser add-on. Feedback on the core clipboard-to-queue workflow would be especially useful.
