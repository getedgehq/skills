Privacy

Last updated September 20, 2026

Signal Weekly only asks for your email address so it can send you the newsletter you requested.

What we collect

When subscriptions are enabled, we collect the email address you submit, the date you subscribed, and the consent or delivery records needed to operate the mailing list.

How we use it

We use this information to deliver Signal Weekly, maintain the subscription list, prevent abuse, and honor unsubscribe requests. We do not sell subscriber information.

Retention and your choices

We keep subscription records while you are subscribed and as needed to document an unsubscribe request. Every newsletter will include an unsubscribe link. You may request access, correction, or deletion using the contact details published by the site operator.

Service providers

The email delivery provider, once selected, may process subscriber information on our behalf. This notice must be updated with that provider’s name, location, and policy before subscriptions are enabled.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or non-essential cookies.
