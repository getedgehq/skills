Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

When you reserve a workshop seat, we collect the name and email address you provide. Our payment provider separately processes your payment details; this website does not receive or store full card details.

How we use information

We use your information to process your order, send receipts and workshop details, provide support, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

We share information only with service providers needed to operate checkout and deliver the workshop, or when required by law. We retain order records only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable accounting obligations.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, subject to legal retention requirements. Contact details for the workshop operator must be added here before launch.

Cookies
