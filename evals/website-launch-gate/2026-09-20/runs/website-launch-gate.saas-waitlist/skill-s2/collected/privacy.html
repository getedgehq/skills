Privacy Policy

Effective: September 20, 2026

This policy explains how AcmeFlow handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you submit and basic technical information needed to operate and protect the form.

How we use it

We use this information to manage early access, send product updates, prevent abuse, and maintain the security of the service. We do not sell personal information.

We may use service providers to operate the website and email list. They may process information only on our instructions. We keep waitlist information while it is relevant to early access or until you ask us to delete it, subject to legal obligations.

Your choices

You may opt out of product email using the unsubscribe link in a message. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by replying to any AcmeFlow email.

Updates

We may update this policy as the product develops. We will post the revised effective date here.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, reply to an email you have received from AcmeFlow. A dedicated contact address will be published before public data collection begins.
