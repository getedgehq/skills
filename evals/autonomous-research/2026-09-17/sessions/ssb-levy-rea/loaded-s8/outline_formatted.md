## Voice and register

**Target register:** Professional policy brief for civil service audience
- Evidence-focused, not advocacy
- Precise language ("1.6 percentage point reduction" not "significant drop")
- Acknowledge limitations openly (credibility with Treasury)
- Avoid academic hedging ("may", "could", "might" - state what evidence shows)
- Avoid campaigning language (no "vital", "crucial", "essential")

**Key phrases to use:**
- "Evidence demonstrates..."
- "International comparators show..."
- "Mechanism transferability suggests..."
- "Acknowledged limitation..."
- "Empirical evidence indicates..."

**Key phrases to avoid:**
- "Clearly...", "Obviously..." (patronizing)
- "Experts agree..." (appeal to authority without citation)
- "It is vital that..." (advocacy not evidence)
- "We believe..." (this is evidence assessment, not opinion)
