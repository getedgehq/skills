# Tallow CRM Renewal Review

## Background

Brightfield Housing Trust has used the Tallow Housing Edition since November
2025 under agreement TSW-BHT-2025-114. The initial term runs from 1 November
2025 to 31 October 2026 and covers 60 named user seats at EUR 800 per seat,
EUR 48,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. The system supports the Case,
Repairs and Tenancy modules.

## Current usage

The August 2026 usage export shows the following picture:

| Team | Seats assigned | Active in last 30 days |
|---|---|---|
| Housing Services | 22 | 17 |
| Tenant Repairs | 18 | 15 |
| Finance | 8 | 3 |
| Fundraising | 7 | 2 |
| Leadership | 5 | 1 |

38 of 60 assigned seats were active in the period. Utilisation is therefore
around 63%, with Fundraising and Leadership the least active teams.

## Stakeholder views

Aisha in Housing Services has noted that the tenant repairs team rely heavily on
the case module and that a switch mid-winter would be disruptive. Marek in
Finance observes that Tallow pricing has historically increased by around 10%
per year. Dev in IT raised a question about the notice period in the agreement.

## Cost for next year

Tallow have advised that new list prices will be published in early October and
that a renewal quote will follow. Based on the historical trend, we expect the
renewal to come in at approximately EUR 52,800 for the coming year. This is the
figure currently carried in the FY27 budget draft going to the board on 8
October, alongside EUR 4,100 for case management add-ons and EUR 9,600 for
telephony.

## Timing

The contract runs to 31 October 2026, so we have until the end of October to
decide whether to renew. Dev has asked whether a 60 day notice period applies;
the agreement does refer to sixty days, so we should allow for that.

## Recommendation

On balance we recommend renewing. The repairs team depend on the case module,
the alternative (Cobbleworth) was quoted at around 31k two years ago and would
require migration, and the expected cost of EUR 52,800 is affordable within the
FY27 envelope. We suggest waiting for Tallow's quote in early October and
reviewing the seat count with Lukas at that point, since he has confirmed seat
changes are applied at the renewal date.
