## 01. We are paying for 60 seats and using 38

August 2026 usage shows 60 assigned seats, 38 active in the last 30 days. Finance (3 of 8 active), Fundraising (2 of 7), and Leadership (1 of 5) account for most waste. Housing Services and Tenant Repairs are the genuine users: 32 of the 38 active seats.

**Implication:** Cutting to 40 seats matches demand with a 5% buffer and eliminates EUR 16,000 of list-price capacity we do not use.

## 02. The arithmetic on 40 seats

Current spend: 60 seats × EUR 800 = EUR 48,000/year. At current list price, 40 seats = EUR 32,000. Vendor is launching new packaging in early October with updated pricing; account manager "would not expect anything dramatic" but gave no number. 

**Conservative estimate for FY27 budget:** EUR 38,000 to EUR 42,000 (assumes 15–20% list increase, nonprofit discount applied, 40 seats). This replaces the EUR 52,800 placeholder in Marek's board pack.

## 03. Switching risk is real and winter timing makes it worse

Aisha's team (Tenant Repairs) runs the Case module daily and the repairs export bug was only just fixed in release 9.4. A mid-winter CRM migration coincides with peak repairs demand. The system works now, and no alternative has been scoped or tested.

**If we do not renew:** 90-day data export window begins 1 November, deletion follows. Migration, training, and configuration would fall in Q4 2026 and Q1 2027, the worst possible window for housing operations.

## 04. The notice deadline is 1 October, not "plenty of time"

Contract clause 9.2 requires 30 days' written notice to prevent auto-renewal. Current term ends 31 October 2026. **Notice deadline: 1 October 2026** (16 days from now). Seat reduction requests go to the account manager (Lukas Brenner, back 28 September) and must be submitted before the same deadline. 

Vendor pricing will not be published until "early October," after the decision window closes. We are deciding without a firm quote.
