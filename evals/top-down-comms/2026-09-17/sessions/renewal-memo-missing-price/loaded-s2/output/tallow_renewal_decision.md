# Tallow CRM renewal: renew at 40 seats, not 60

**The recommendation:** Give notice by 1 October to terminate the current agreement, then negotiate a new 40-seat contract for 1 November at known pricing. Do not let it auto-renew.

---

**AT A GLANCE**

| Decision deadline | Current cost | Seats paid vs used | Estimated next-year cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Oct (14 days) | €48k for 60 seats | 60 assigned, 38 active | €52.8k if auto-renewed at 60 |

---

## 01. We are paying for 22 seats nobody uses

August usage data:
- 60 seats assigned
- 38 active in the last 30 days
- 22 inactive (37% waste)

Finance has 8 seats, 3 active. Fundraising has 7 seats, 2 active. Even allowing headroom for peaks, 40 seats covers current load with 5% spare.

At an assumed €880/seat (10% increase), dropping to 40 seats saves **€17.6k annually** compared to auto-renewing at 60.

---

## 02. Auto-renewal locks us in at unknown pricing

The contract auto-renews on 1 November unless we give 30 days' notice (deadline: 1 October). Renewal pricing is "Vendor's then-current list price" — no cap, no negotiation lever once renewed.

Tallow's account manager will not provide pricing until early October, after the notice window closes. If we stay silent, we are committed to whatever number arrives in the invoice.

---

## 03. Giving notice does not mean switching vendor

Termination notice prevents auto-renewal. It does not terminate support or require a migration. The contract allows a new agreement to start immediately after the old one expires.

The sequence:
1. Notice sent by 1 Oct → current term ends 31 Oct as planned
2. Pricing published early Oct → we negotiate a 40-seat deal
3. New contract starts 1 Nov → no service interruption

If Tallow will not negotiate, we have four weeks to evaluate alternatives before the current term expires. Right now we have no leverage and no number.

---

## 04. Operational risk is low

Aisha's team depends on the Repairs module, especially in winter. This proposal does not disrupt that:
- Same vendor, same modules, same data
- Seat reduction affects unused licences (Finance, Fundraising, Leadership)
- Housing Services (22 assigned, 17 active) and Tenant Repairs (18 assigned, 15 active) are untouched

The alternative — paying €17k/year for seats nobody logs into — does not make the system more reliable.

---

## 05. The board pack placeholder is too high

Marek's FY27 draft (due 8 Oct) assumes €52.8k at 60 seats. If we renew at 40 seats and pricing rises 10%, the actual cost is **€35.2k**, freeing €17.6k for reallocation or saving.

Letting an estimate become a commitment wastes budget we do not need to waste.
