## 01. We are paying for 60 seats and using 38

Usage in August 2026:
- **60 seats assigned**, **38 active** (63% utilization)
- Housing Services and Tenant Repairs teams drive 84% of actual usage (32 of 38 active users)
- Finance, Fundraising and Leadership account for 6 active users across 20 assigned seats

The low-use teams could share a smaller pool. Tenant Repairs (the team Aisha flagged as critical) has 15 active users in 18 seats; they are not at risk.

## 02. Cutting to 40 seats saves money even if the price goes up

Current cost: €48,000/year (60 seats @ €800)

Likely scenarios for FY27:

| Scenario | Seats | Price per seat | Annual cost | vs FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Roll forward, no change** | 60 | €800 | €48,000 | — |
| **10% price rise, keep 60** | 60 | €880 | €52,800 | +€4,800 |
| **10% price rise, cut to 40** | 40 | €880 | €35,200 | **−€12,800** |

Tallow's account manager would not quote before "early October" but said increases would not be "dramatic". Marek saw 10% last time. At that rate, 40 seats still cost less than we pay now.

## 04. The operational risk of staying is low

- Aisha's Tenant Repairs team (the most dependent user group) has 15 active users; 40 seats covers them and the rest of Housing Services comfortably

The contract auto-renews unless we act. Not renewing is the disruptive option.
