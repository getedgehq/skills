# Pass/fail review

Credit: Review criteria for this independent adaptation of Marcus Sherwin's public method. Public-method rules are detailed in [method.md](../references/method.md); observability, evidence labelling, and deliverable checks are package additions.

Mark each item PASS or FAIL with one line of evidence or a repair. A PASS requires an explicit answer or an honest unknown with a concrete next action. A fabricated answer fails. For a strategy-only deliverable, unconfirmed rights or platform access may remain clearly stated execution dependencies; do not mark activation ready until they are resolved.

## Method and attribution

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The plan distinguishes initial shortlist presence from confidence once considered.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The diagnosis follows buyer or competitive evidence, or is clearly provisional.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The five confidence themes are considered and decisive priorities selected.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Bain research, Sherwin interpretation, guest practices, and package adaptations remain distinguishable.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Substantive method claims use traceable [S#] citations to fetched public sources.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Approximately ninety-percent purchase-from-list findings are not relabelled as a different statistic or a guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Research limits and service-mark ownership are respected; no custom score is sold as official LTB.

## Committee, voices, and proof

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Champions and relevant hidden buyers appear with distinct risks, responsibilities, and channels.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Each proposed voice has a defensible expertise and audience-fit rationale.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: External creator names, audience data, and product experience are verified or labelled unknown.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Proof addresses a buyer concern rather than restating a feature or slogan.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Customer outcomes, references, financial assumptions, and security claims are supported and usable.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Genuine delivery gaps have an improvement owner rather than a content-only solution.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Creator freedom and willing SME participation are preserved within factual boundaries.

## Activation and handoff

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The plan includes organic learning, deliberate amplification, and continued consideration.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Distribution can reach the intended roles and has a relevant next destination.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Current platform capabilities are verified for implementation or named as a dependency.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: External partnerships distinguish ownership, usage rights, platform permission, duration, disclosure, and removal handling.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Compensation fits an affiliate relationship or a paid-content flight as appropriate.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Signals are labelled by granularity; aggregates are not attributed to named viewers.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The proposed threshold is observable and locally justified; Sherwin's example is not treated as a universal constant.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The sales handoff has an owner, a useful action, and truthful outreach context.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Execution remains within the user's authorized scope.

## Measurement and deliverable readiness

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Consideration and confidence have separate evidence or research plans.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Metrics define relevant cohorts, denominators, baselines, and observation windows.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Sourced, influenced, associated, and causal outcomes are not conflated.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Continue, revise, and stop decisions respond to meaningful evidence.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Budgets, timing, owners, assumptions, and unresolved dependencies are visible.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Illustrations and operating targets are labelled as invented or proposed.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A creator campaign is prescribed before diagnosis | Visibility and confidence require different responses | Establish the two dimensions first; use a provisional diagnosis if needed |
| Only the CRM champion is targeted | Approval guardians remain unconvinced | Map actual Finance, IT, Procurement, and other relevant roles |
| Biggest follower count wins | Reach does not establish relevant trust | Inspect genuine expertise, audience composition, and buyer response |
| One author is asked to answer every concern | Domain credibility becomes implausible | Match specialised voices and proof to roles |
| Paid reach rescues unsupported content | Budget cannot supply missing evidence | Repair the claim, message, or delivery problem before amplification |
| Every post sounds like brand copy | The creator's reason to be trusted disappears | Brief the belief and proof, allow the author to shape the story |
| One post is declared a complete test | Long buying cycles and consideration are ignored | Define a sustained learning window and modest inference limits |
| Aggregate engagement becomes a contact alert | The data cannot support the claimed identity | Keep account review separate from identified contact events |
| Three roles in thirty days is mandatory | The rule may be unobservable or unsuitable | Calibrate it to real data; respond to explicit requests directly |
| Likes become an LTB score | Activity is confused with buyer sentiment and a proprietary instrument | Use a labelled activity metric and separate adapted buyer research |
| All influenced pipeline is called new revenue | Attribution exceeds the evidence | Separate sourced and influenced cohorts; state comparison limits |
| Rights are assumed after a permission click | Platform access does not establish commercial terms | Resolve usage, duration, continuity, disclosure, and takedown |
| Confidence claims exceed service capability | Buyer trust cannot be sustained by a promise alone | Assign and verify the operational improvement |
| Day 1 success is claimed after an RFP opens | The initial shortlist has already formed | Describe confidence support now and early presence for future buyers |
| A case interpretation becomes a guaranteed multiplier | Reported examples do not prove universal causation | Attribute the interpretation and remove the promised lift |

## Final review disposition

**Ready as a strategy:** The diagnosis, recommendations, limits, and next decisions are clear; execution dependencies are explicit.

**Ready for activation:** Relevant rights, evidence, platform feasibility, data access, owners, and authorization are resolved for the proposed action.

**Needs revision:** Any unsupported claim, missing decisive role, false identity inference, or misleading measurement remains. Record the fix and owner, then review the affected items again.
