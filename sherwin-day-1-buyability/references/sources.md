## Access and attribution limits

The LinkedIn starting post returned both its post text and a short embedded transcript excerpt. That excerpt is evidence for the limited point attributed to it, not a transcript of the complete interview. The linked YouTube page was attempted with curl but returned navigation without usable episode content. The podcast landing page was also fetched, but supplied general show positioning rather than the full Bain conversation. Neither is counted as an additional substantive method source.

The agency's public sitemap was inspected for related articles. This research found sufficient public articles, case interpretations, and podcast recaps to build the package; it does not claim to exhaust every newsletter or talk. Guest statements in recaps remain guest practice shared through Sherwin's show. Clay and Apollo commercial claims remain Sherwin's reported case interpretation, not independently audited outcomes. No existing third-party skill supplied the method or wording.
