# B2B Day 1 List and confidence plan

Credit: Adapted from Marcus Sherwin's public method and the cited research. This is a package deliverable template, not an official Kleos or Bain instrument.

Replace bracketed fields with evidence or an explicit unknown. Keep recommendations distinct from actions already completed. Cite sourced method claims as [S#] using [the source register](../references/sources.md); identify client facts by their supplied source. Do not manufacture missing inputs.

## 1. Decision brief

- **Brand and category:** [Vendor, buyer-defined category, use case]. Use the buyer's category language, not only the vendor's positioning.
- **Scope:** [Market, segment, account cohort, named competitors]. Make the unit of analysis clear.
- **Buying trigger and horizon:** [Why an evaluation might begin, anticipated timing]. State whether opportunities are pre-evaluation or already active.
- **Objective:** [Consideration or confidence change linked to a commercial decision]. Avoid an impression target as the sole objective.
- **Diagnosis:** [Low presence, weak confidence, both, or leadership preservation]. Name the evidence and uncertainty.
- **Immediate recommendation:** [Specific next move and why it fits the diagnosis]. Include dependencies that could change it.
- **Budget and execution scope:** [Proposed cap, allocation, existing authorizations]. A strategy document does not imply that spending has occurred.

## 3. Committee and proof matrix

| Role and influence | Buyer question or risk | Confidence theme | Evidence and status | Voice and fit | Channel and next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Actual role, responsibility] | [Specific approval concern] | [One or more of five themes] | [Verified proof, missing proof, or real delivery gap] | [SME or practitioner and why credible] | [Reachable channel and useful action] |

Cover the champion, economic buyer, and relevant guardians. Evaluate all five themes: current and future needs, value against total cost, internal defensibility, implementation confidence, and ease of working with the vendor. Prioritise a few decisive gaps; do not force equal spend across factors or roles.

## 5. Content briefs and sequence

For each proposed asset, fill:

- **Stage and buyer:** [Early category familiarity or continued confidence; role].
- **Question:** [Concern in the buyer's language].
- **Desired belief:** [Specific conclusion the evidence should justify].
- **Proof:** [Approved source, demonstration, assumptions, and limits].
- **Voice and format:** [Credible author, useful practitioner treatment].
- **Creative freedom:** [What the author controls; factual boundaries].
- **Next step:** [Resource, review, conversation, or explicit request appropriate to readiness].
- **Learning signal:** [Relevant response that would earn amplification].

Sequence: [Problem recognition, risk explanation, demonstration, implementation or reliability proof, useful next action]. Adapt the order to the diagnosis and actual buyer journey.

## 6. Relationship and distribution plan

- **Partner model:** [Internal advocacy, external TLA fee, affiliate/community programme, or combination]. Explain why compensation matches the relationship.
- **Rights:** [Ownership, paid usage duration, renewal, sponsorship permission, disclosure, takedown, fallback owner]. Mark unconfirmed terms as dependencies.
- **Organic learning:** [Post sample and relevant response criteria]. Sherwin suggests reviewing three to five posts and selecting two; adjust deliberately where evidence warrants.
- **Paid audience:** [Account cohort, roles, geography, targeting rationale]. Document feasibility and relevant platform checks.
- **Control preferences:** [Objective, setup, expansion settings, bidding approach]. Verify current features before implementation.
- **Follow-on route:** [Retargeting feasibility, proof destination, continued consideration content]. Do not assume every engagement type supports a retargeting pool.
- **Timing and cap:** [Flight, review dates, spend allocation]. Identify local choices instead of presenting them as validated method constants.
- **Channel resilience:** [Owned or community route if platform access or permission changes].
