## How to interpret the evidence

[S3], [S4], [S8], and [S9] are Meier's descriptions of her own work and commercial perspective, not independent evaluations of effectiveness. [S2] is an editorial episode summary, [S6] a public interview, and [S7] a host's recap. [S5] explicitly labels its transcript AI-assisted; use the clear conceptual statements, not transcription artifacts as exact wording or verified biographical detail.

[S2] contains dated platform advice and broad generational claims. [S7] also makes sweeping media statements. Neither establishes current behavior for every young person. This package uses their decision-relevant principles and requires local evidence for channel selection. The financial and performance claims in promotional posts are not adopted as promises, benchmarks, or universal budget prescriptions. Relative LinkedIn timestamps are not treated as exact publication dates.
