# Review checklist

Internal review: run it silently on your draft and fix what fails. Never put this checklist, PASS/FAIL marks, a review note or a list of limitations in the deliverable; a gap that matters to the user becomes one short open question at the end (three at most). The gates are package adaptations of the public method, not a scoring system published by the expert.

## Pass/fail gates

- [ ] **Decision defined:** The page, primary buyer, desired next action, hypothesis, and success condition are explicit.
- [ ] **Buyer context usable:** Priorities, triggers, buying involvement, and alternatives have recent evidence, or research gaps are clearly identified before making a strategic promise. [S2][S4]
- [ ] **Correct study:** The selected study answers the uncertainty; a routine message test is focused on the persuasion path. [S4]
- [ ] **Stimulus traceable:** The page version, scope, key sections, and campaign context are recorded. [S5][S6]
- [ ] **Audience traceable:** Recruitment criteria and actual participants match the intended market; segment and customer/prospect differences remain visible. [S2][S6]
- [ ] **Questions diagnostic:** Open-ended interpretations and explanations accompany any scores; prompts do not supply the answer. [S1][S6]
- [ ] **Sample claims restrained:** Sample size has a rationale; no qualitative panel is presented as a precise population estimate or statistically significant conversion experiment. [S4][S6]
- [ ] **Evidence authentic:** Findings and scores come from actual records; invented examples, inference, and proposals are labelled. No missing responses are reconstructed.
- [ ] **Order respected:** Clarity, relevance, value, and differentiation are assessed in sequence; the earliest failed layer has a causal hypothesis. [S1]
- [ ] **Doubts addressed:** Consequential objections and proof gaps are linked to the claims they affect. Brand findings remain separate from friction. [S1][S4]
- [ ] **Difference defensible:** The alternative is one buyers actually consider; the reason to choose the offer has support beyond novel wording. [S3]
- [ ] **Claims supported:** Proposed copy uses verified claims or visibly provisional placeholders; numbers, testimonials, integrations, and guarantees are not fabricated. [S1][S6]
- [ ] **Revision focused:** Priority changes answer the primary failed question, with unnecessary repeated explanation removed where warranted. [S1][S5]
- [ ] **Strategy retained:** Individual suggestions are evaluated by segment fit and position, rather than automatically becoming features or copy. [S3]
- [ ] **Recommendation justified:** Ship, revise, or gather-context advice matches the declared evidence status and project-specific gate. Proposed copy is not called validated. [S4]
- [ ] **Retest useful:** A substantive rewrite has a comparable retest plan; typo or design-only changes do not trigger unnecessary message tests. [S4]
- [ ] **Measurement boundary clear:** Improved message feedback is not represented as demonstrated conversion lift. [S6]
- [ ] **Maintenance scoped:** The brief names a relevant review date or change trigger, without automatically commissioning unrelated research. [S4]

Release a buyer-tested recommendation only when its applicable evidence gates pass. For a provisional review, release the hypotheses and study plan with unresolved validation items clearly stated. Checklist mechanics are package conventions; citations identify the underlying method.

## Common failures and fixes

| Failure mode | Why it matters | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rewrite before understanding the buyer | The promise may solve the wrong priority | Reuse recent context or gather focused buyer evidence. [S2][S4] |
| Leading questions produce fluent answers | Respondents repeat the prompt instead of revealing interpretation | Ask unaided paraphrases before discussing the intended message. [S6] |
| Good average score hides category confusion | Later persuasion judgments rest on misunderstanding | Inspect response-level explanations and fix clarity first. [S1] |
| Broad panel hides role disagreement | A message relevant to one role is diluted | Analyze segments separately and narrow the primary buyer. [S2][S4] |
| Feature list substitutes for value | The buyer cannot see a wanted result | Connect capability to a concrete outcome and verified evidence. [S1] |
| Superlatives substitute for difference | Competitors can claim the same advantage | Name the actual alternative and substantiate a structural difference. [S3] |
| Every buyer request becomes a change | The position loses its intentional tradeoffs | Cluster the underlying question; preserve strategy and evaluate fit. [S3] |
| Noise or repeated sections delay explanation | A polished page still leaves the offer unclear | Move the essential account earlier and consolidate repetition. [S5] |
| A plausible statistic fills a proof gap | Copy becomes more persuasive by becoming unsupported | Remove the number or request evidence; keep placeholders provisional. [S1] |
| Preference vote becomes the strategy | A popularity result displaces business judgment | Compare only viable options and interpret reasons against the chosen position. [S4] |
| Before/after scores use different audiences | Apparent improvement may be a sampling difference | Preserve criteria or disclose the change and constrain the conclusion. [S4] |
| Minor edits trigger another paid test | Research spend yields little new diagnosis | Reserve message retests for substantive changes. [S4] |
| Message test is called a conversion win | Interpretation evidence cannot prove behavioral lift | Report diagnostic improvement and specify separate behavioral measurement. [S6] |
