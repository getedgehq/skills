# Sources

All sources below were fetched successfully with curl on 2026-10-02 and inspected as public HTML. Source numbers are local to this package. No book, paywalled text, or embedded video transcript was used. Guidance is paraphrased. Laja's authored material supplies the core method; Wynter's institutional guidance and Kate Meda's article support execution details.

1. [S1] Peep Laja, **B2B Message Layers framework**. https://wynter.com/post/b2b-message-layers-framework-wynter
   Contributes the ordered clarity, relevance, value, and differentiation diagnosis, with proof and friction across layers.
2. [S2] Peep Laja, **How to use buyer intelligence to win the market**. https://wynter.com/post/buyer-intelligence
   Contributes current buyer context, priorities, objections, alternatives, and faster research feedback loops.
3. [S3] Peep Laja, **How to compete and win on messaging**. https://wynter.com/post/how-to-compete-and-win-on-messaging
   Contributes the sameness audit, defensible positioning, and warnings about imitation and blindly following customer requests.
4. [S4] Wynter, **The Wynter Research Playbook**. https://wynter.com/research-playbook
   Contributes study selection, buyer research sequencing, sample planning, retesting, maintenance triggers, and explicit exclusions.
5. [S5] Peep Laja, **Qualified**, public written notes accompanying **Do you even resonate?**. https://wynter.com/dyer-recordings/qualified
   Contributes a concrete critique of a vague hero, delayed differentiation, noisy presentation, and repeated use-case sections.
6. [S6] Kate Meda, **The definitive guide to message testing**. https://wynter.com/post/message-testing
   Contributes open-ended questions, audience and stimulus setup, qualitative interpretation, and the distinction from A/B testing.
7. [S7] Wynter, **Wynter example tests**. https://wynter.com/examples
   Contributes visible examples of full-page, campaign landing page, pricing, survey, and preference study outputs.
