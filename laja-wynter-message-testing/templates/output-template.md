# Message diagnosis and revision brief

Copy this structure into the deliverable. Replace bracketed fields with supplied facts, actual findings, or explicitly labelled hypotheses. Delete irrelevant optional fields. A missing input is a research gap, not permission to invent it. The structure implements the diagnosis and rewrite loop; it is not a native Wynter export schema. [S1][S4]

## 1. Decision and evidence status

- **Decision:** [What page/message decision must the team make?]
- **Research question:** [One sentence naming the buyer, stimulus, and uncertainty.]
- **Desired next action:** [Demo request, trial, evaluation, or another concrete action.]
- **Status:** [Buyer-tested diagnosis / provisional expert review / proposed retest.]
- **Message hypothesis:** [What the page is expected to communicate and why it should matter.]
- **Success condition:** [Observable interpretation or objection resolution; define before analysis.]

Guidance: Specify what would change the recommendation. Do not claim conversion lift from qualitative message feedback. [S6]

## 3. Test record

- **Study type and rationale:** [Why this type answers the decision.]
- **Stimulus:** [URL/image location, capture date, version, page scope, selected sections.]
- **Campaign context, if applicable:** [Source audience and ad promise.]
- **Audience:** [Recruitment criteria, verification, prospects/customers, segments.]
- **Planned and completed sample:** [Counts and reason for size; exclusions and reasons.]
- **Questions and order:** [Exact prompts administered, including neutral paraphrase prompt.]
- **Rating anchors:** [Scale and meaning; leave blank if no ratings were collected.]
- **Evidence location:** [Response IDs or accessible research artifact.]
- **Limitations:** [Coverage, freshness, missing context, stimulus differences.]

Guidance: If no test ran, write planned rather than completed. Sample defaults are planning guidance, not statistical guarantees. [S4][S6]

## 6. Proposed revision

- **Hero headline:** [Identify the offer and primary promise.]
- **Subheading:** [Buyer situation, concrete outcome, and meaningful explanation.]

- **Proof placement:** [Evidence beside the claim it supports; flag verification needs.]
- **CTA:** [Appropriate next action with clear expectation.]
- **Page outline:** [Ordered sections, their buyer question, and sections to combine/remove.]
- **Claim status:** [Verified / needs verification / omitted, for each material promise.]

Guidance: The hero need not carry every claim. Fix the primary failure first and move unnecessary detail to an appropriate supporting location. [S1][S5]

## 8. Retest and recommendation

- **Recommendation:** [Ship / revise / gather buyer context, with basis.]
- **Retest stimulus:** [Version and substantive changes.]
- **Comparable setup:** [ICP, scope, prompts, scale; disclose changes.]
- **Success evidence sought:** [Correct paraphrases, priority recognition, wanted value, explained difference, addressed doubts.]
- **Remaining uncertainty:** [What this research cannot establish.]
- **Next research or measurement:** [Message retest, preference test, focused survey, or behavioral experiment as appropriate.]
- **Maintenance trigger:** [Launch, segment shift, pricing change, competitor move, or scheduled review.]

Guidance: A proposed revision cannot earn a buyer-validated ship recommendation. Compare interpretations rather than only average scores. Skip minor-edit retests and keep behavioral measurement distinct. [S4][S6]
