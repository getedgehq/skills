# Pass/fail review

Review the completed proposal using [method.md](../references/method.md). Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL with one concrete reason. Mark genuinely irrelevant items N/A and explain why. Unresolved execution dependencies must remain visible. A draft may be delivered with dependencies, but should not be represented as ready to launch.

## Method and evidence

- [ ] Product truth: The main promise describes an experiential difference with identified proof. FAIL if humour conceals a generic or unsupported claim. P1.
- [ ] Category distinction: The chosen reversal is visible in the actual buying context and retains product clarity. P2.
- [ ] Surface value: An existing touchpoint reaches a relevant person with a useful message; incremental costs are counted. P3.
- [ ] Cultural fit: The proposal names a community, occasion and contribution beyond a fashionable reference. P4.
- [ ] Partner value: Selection is based on trust and contribution, with costs and uncertainty visible. Fame alone fails. P5.
- [ ] Originality: Copy and imagery are original or appropriately cleared; Gustavo Gusto execution is not transplanted wholesale.
- [ ] Attribution: [S#] citations resolve to fetched sources, and operational adaptations are labelled. Podcast notes are not called transcripts.
- [ ] Source boundaries: No restricted interview material, unavailable LinkedIn post or unsupported quotation is used. Historical results remain attributed reports.

## Creative and practical quality

- [ ] Humane humour: Copy is understandable, product-linked and free of customer mockery, arrogance or lectures. P8.
- [ ] Consistency: Follow-ups build the same promise and voice rather than scatter unrelated stunts. P6.
- [ ] Truthful access: Geography, timing, stock, booking capacity and partner status are accurate.
- [ ] Feasible budget: Costs include production, rights, partners, fulfilment and measurement; time is shown separately.
- [ ] Pilot ownership: Dates, tasks and owners are clear, and one bounded pilot can run with available capacity.
- [ ] Prepared opportunity: Forciertes Glück has a signal, owner, useful next action, reserve and stopping rule. P7.
- [ ] Commercial learning: Attention, demand, purchases and economics are separate; targets are not invented results.
- [ ] Continuation decision: Scale, revise and stop criteria account for quality and ability to fulfil.
- [ ] Concrete contribution: Any solidarity claim names an actual action and beneficiary. Generic virtue claims fail. P9.
- [ ] Growth fit: The organisation can execute the idea while a responsible human retains creative judgement. P9.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Famous person pasted onto the pack | Borrowed reach with no audience or product reason | Define the community and co-created benefit; compare more relevant partners. |
| Generic premium promise | The offer could belong to any competitor | Write the specific product fact and available proof before the headline. |
| Random shock tactic | Attention may not help purchase or trust | Link the surprise to an occasion and a useful customer action. |
| Copying the white box | Confuses a category-specific execution with a principle | Inspect this category and choose its own distinctive convention. |
| Misleading national availability | Turns an accidental historical episode into deception | Publish accurate coverage and use genuine unmet demand to inform expansion. |
| Almost no budget treated as zero cost | Hides production, rights and labour | Build a cash-and-time ledger and shrink the pilot to fit. |
| Forciertes Glück treated as waiting | The team cannot act while the opportunity lasts | Prepare assets, an owner and a bounded response before launch. |
| Forciertes Glück treated as unlimited escalation | Attention outruns capacity or economics | Cap the reserve and specify a stop trigger. |
| Humour at the audience's expense | Breaks the welcoming brand voice | Make the situation or brand the comic subject; keep the benefit clear. |
| Limited edition with no learning plan | Novelty masks weak repeat demand | Track product response and define a continuation decision. |
| Purpose without material action | Performance replaces contribution | Name the help and beneficiary, or remove the claim. |
| Viral reach presented as sales impact | No evidence of conversion or incrementality | Separate metrics and state baseline limitations. |
| Every approval layer rewrites the idea | Distinctiveness dissolves and response slows | Assign creative ownership and route factual or operational checks to accountable specialists. |
| AI produces generic category copy | Efficiency erases the challenger difference | Use AI for exploration or production; have a human select a specific, original voice. |
| Success case presented as a guarantee | Context and attribution are lost | Cite the report, name confounders and use locally justified targets. |

## Release decision

Ready to execute only when product claims, capacity, budget, partner permission and purchase information are resolved, and all applicable launch-critical checks pass. Otherwise deliver the proposal with the specific dependency and owner. This checklist does not grant authority to contact partners, spend money or publish work.
