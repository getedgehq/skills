# Public source register

Credit: Martin Brath and Kraftvertising. This package is an independent paraphrase of their public material, with no affiliation or endorsement.

All six sources below were successfully fetched as public HTML using `curl -L --fail` on 2026-10-02. Only readable page content was used. Titles and URLs identify sources; method prose contains no direct quotations.

[S1] **SEO, Google Ads or Paid Social? A B2B Demand Framework**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/blog/seo-google-ads-or-paid-social-b2b-demand-framework
Contribution: Core search-demand question, three situations, low-volume exception, tangential intent, product-market-fit caution and AI discovery caveat.

[S2] **B2B SaaS Marketing: How to Combine Demand Generation and Demand Capture**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/blog/b2b-saas-demand-generation-and-capture
Contribution: Connected acquisition system, category maturity, product-led versus sales-led weighting, geographic concentration, measurement and agency-described client illustrations.

[S3] **B2B LinkedIn Ads Playbook for 2026**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/b2blinkedinadsguide
Contribution: Public landing-page principles on channel fit, buying committees, attention, repeated recognition, outbound support and measurement beyond clicks.
Scope: The downloadable guide requires a contact form. No form was submitted and no PDF or private follow-up was used; citations refer only to the visible landing page.

[S4] **Why We Usually Run Google Ads Before Investing in SEO**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/blog/google-ads-before-seo
Contribution: Paid-search learning as organic prioritization, keyword estimates versus actual behavior, parallel foundations and limits of PPC validation.

[S5] **Why B2B SEO vs. PPC Attribution Is Often Misleading**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/blog/b2b-seo-vs-ppc-attribution
Contribution: Separation of demand creation from conversion credit, brand versus non-brand reporting, attribution uncertainty and fully loaded cost comparisons.

[S6] **B2B Google Ads Conversion Tracking: Why Leads Are Not Enough**
URL: https://kraftvertising.com/blog/b2b-google-ads-conversion-tracking
Contribution: Meaningful conversion hierarchy, proxy drift, actual submissions versus button clicks, and CRM or product feedback to bidding.

## Additional research and exclusions

The agency blog index (https://kraftvertising.com/blog) and homepage (https://kraftvertising.com/) were fetched for discovery. The homepage contained client references; no separate case-study page was used as method evidence. The named-client illustrations in [S2] are agency accounts, not independently verified results, and are not reproduced as performance promises.

A curl request to https://www.linkedin.com/company/kraftvertising failed with HTTP 999. LinkedIn posts were therefore not used or cited as evidence.

The public Apple Podcasts listing for **B2B Steak Sauce** (https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/b2b-steak-sauce/id1877749036) was fetched. It identifies Kraftvertising as creator and Martin Brath as host, but does not provide enough relevant episode content to substantiate framework rules. No audio, transcript, newsletter or talk was used. These discovery pages do not count toward the six method sources.

The method sources used here are English. No German method name was present in the material used, so this package does not invent a translated or German-branded label. No existing third-party skill was consulted or copied.
