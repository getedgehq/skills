# B2B Demand Framework: detailed method

Credit: Based on Martin Brath's public method, published by Kraftvertising. Independently paraphrased; not affiliated with or endorsed by Martin Brath.

## Reading map and attribution boundary

Sections 1 to 8 cover diagnosis, routing, scarce demand, sequencing, education, integration and measurement. Section 9 covers exceptions and warnings. [S#] references resolve in [sources.md](sources.md).

Source-backed principles below describe the public method. Evidence labels, experiment caps, owners, review dates and the output schema are package operating guidance added to make an agent's recommendation reviewable. They are not claimed to be Brath's named tools. No universal budget split, minimum keyword volume, fixed testing duration or guaranteed return is part of this package.

## 1. Begin with buyers, not platform prices

First establish whether relevant people already look for the product or category. Search is initiated by the buyer; paid social initiates contact with a buyer who may have no active purchase intent. The channels therefore solve different problems. Comparing CPC alone skips the strategic question. [S1]

Demand capture serves existing active interest. In an established category, demand generation often means building preference for a particular brand rather than creating the category's demand. In an emerging category, education may need to explain that the solution exists before buyers know what to look for. [S2]

Collect the offer and problem solved, category maturity, priority markets, customer value, relevant companies and stakeholders, purchase friction, sales cycle and resources. Product-led and sales-led businesses require different balances of capture, trust and education. [S2]

Package guidance: If the brief supplies none of these facts, produce a provisional diagnosis with explicit unknowns. Do not fill the gaps with fictional customer data.

## 2. Establish relevant search demand

Look for actual category and solution queries, commercially relevant competitor queries, and searches describing the underlying problem. Keyword-tool estimates are hypotheses; real searches can differ because people use long-tail phrasing and varied language. Paid-search reports can improve the evidence about intent and behavior. [S1][S4]

Use this package's ledger:

| Evidence | Record | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer language | Interview or sales evidence, date and market | Do buyers know the category or only the problem? |
| Query family | Category, competitor, brand or problem wording | What purchase intent does it express? |
| Tool estimate | Source, geography, language and range | A clue about volume, not observed buyer behavior |
| Actual search terms | Relevant terms, delivery constraints and resulting actions | Stronger evidence of accessible demand |
| Commercial progression | Activation, accepted leads, opportunities or customers | Whether traffic supports the business |

Package guidance: Label each entry observed, estimated or unknown. Absence of account traffic could reflect eligibility, geography, targeting or limited delivery. Check these before interpreting it as absence of market demand. If relevant demand remains uncertain, use a bounded evidence-gathering plan.

Branded search demonstrates existing brand awareness; it does not by itself establish a large pool of buyers discovering the category. [S5]

## 3. Select the demand branch

| Buyer state | Prioritize | Supporting role and caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knows the category and actively searches | Google Ads for immediate capture | SEO for valuable organic visibility over time |
| Experiences the problem but does not know the category | Paid social for explanation and discovery | Related educational SEO can support learning, but cannot ensure new category searches |
| Relevant searches exist and future buyers must also be reached | Combine capture with education or brand building | Maintain a coherent buyer experience across channels |

These are the three situations in the public framework. The first question routes investment; it is not a rule that the business can use only one channel forever. [S1]

Package guidance: Apply the table by segment and market when awareness differs. State confidence and the evidence that would change the selected branch. An emerging category may have an existing niche of informed searchers worth capturing while the wider audience still needs explanation.

## 4. Do not confuse scarce demand with absent demand

A small number of high-intent searches can be valuable. With high deal values, the company may reasonably pay for presence across that small opportunity set. Low traffic also limits click-based media expenditure. Visibility is an opportunity to be considered, not a guarantee of a sale. [S1]

Judge the relevance of searches, supported countries, buyer fit and likely commercial value. If conversion volume is sparse, the company may have to decide whether relevant presence is worth paying for before a dependable acquisition-cost estimate exists. [S1]

Package guidance: Show scenarios rather than a point forecast. If estimated CPC is C, qualified-opportunity rate per click is Q and win rate is W, modeled media cost per customer is C / (Q × W), when Q and W are positive. Identify unsupported rates and include management, creative and other acquisition costs separately. This arithmetic is a package aid, not Brath's formula or empirical proof.

Tangential problem searches may reveal a relevant audience without indicating a category purchase. As topic distance increases, intent becomes less certain. Treat those queries as a separate exploratory activity rather than pooling them with high-intent capture. [S1]

## 5. Use paid search to prioritize SEO

Paid search can expose which searches happen and which bring useful visitors, trials, demonstrations or qualified opportunities before the business commits heavily to ranking. Commercial behavior matters more than estimated volume. This is especially useful where organic positions are expensive to win and expert content resources are limited. [S4]

The overlap principally concerns keywords and intent. A paid conversion page is not automatically the best organic page; search ads also offer limited space for broad proposition tests. Paid social is generally more suitable for testing explanations, problems and hooks. [S1][S4]

Run paid learning alongside sensible SEO foundations when the category is strategically important. The method does not require completing or stopping Google Ads before starting SEO. [S4]

If product-market fit, category or positioning may change materially, avoid making SEO the primary large commitment on untested assumptions. Paid channels provide faster feedback. Early SEO can still be reasonable where future demand is credible and establishing visibility ahead of competition has value. [S1]

Package guidance: Rank SEO candidates by relevant demand, observed commercial behavior, strategic importance and cost of competing. Unknown outcomes should remain unknown, not become zero-value keywords after a tiny test.

## 6. Make paid social do the education job

For an unfamiliar solution, explain a recognizable problem, why the existing approach is insufficient, what another approach enables, and how the product fits. Demonstration, context, expert communication and stories can create understanding that a short search ad cannot accommodate. Established categories may instead need stronger brand recognition and preference. [S1][S2]

LinkedIn's public playbook page emphasizes professional targeting, multiple buying stakeholders, meaningful attention and recognizable creative. Accurate targeting alone does not ensure the buyer notices or remembers the advertiser. Repeated exposures can support future search, outbound response and sales conversations even without a click. [S3]

Select LinkedIn because reaching specific companies, roles or seniority adds value, rather than merely because the product is B2B. Other paid-social channels may fit the audience and task. Lead forms are one conversion path, not the definition of success. [S3]

Package guidance: Define a message hypothesis as audience + problem + explanation + proof + next action. Keep one diagnostic question per creative test. Do not promise that education can fix a product with no useful market fit.

## 7. Connect capture and recognition

Buyers encounter a company across many moments. Social messages, organic and paid results, landing pages and sales should reinforce the same proposition, visual identity and evidence. Retargeting can continue earlier communication instead of treating every visit as a fresh isolated campaign. [S2]

Product-led businesses can often lean further into capture when buyers can try and evaluate independently. Sales-led offers may need greater familiarity and trust before a sales conversation. For small enterprise account universes, paid social can support outbound and buying committees instead of producing large inbound volumes. [S2][S3]

Global capture can reach active searchers where supported demand exists. Limited recognition-building resources often work better in selected markets with enough repetition than when diluted worldwide. Audience stability and long buying cycles can increase the value of sustained familiarity. [S2]

Choose allocations from demand, category awareness, commercial value, trust requirements, market size, sales model and available resources. The public method gives no universal percentage split. [S2]

## 8. Evaluate commercial value and measurement limits

For capture, follow outcomes beyond initial leads. Product-led evidence includes returning users, meaningful activity and paying customers. Sales-led evidence includes account and role fit, sales acceptance, opportunity creation and progression. For brand building, combine target-audience reach, repeated exposure, recognition, branded interest and sales feedback rather than expecting one metric to prove everything. [S2][S3]

Conversion credit cannot establish which touchpoint created demand. Separate brand from non-brand activity in paid and organic reporting. Direct traffic may reflect missing journey information. Compare commercial outcomes and fully loaded channel costs, with attention to small samples. [S5]

Confirm events represent completed actions, not merely clicked buttons. Feed useful CRM and product outcomes into optimization where feasible. Earlier signals may be necessary when downstream events are scarce, but repeatedly check whether they still predict useful outcomes. Do not give a page visit the same meaning as an opportunity. [S6]

Package guidance: For each proposed activity, define its question, primary commercial outcome, diagnostic signals, owner, spend cap, review point and continue/change/stop criteria. Let review timing reflect the sales cycle and available evidence. A cap is a controllable limit, not a prediction of sufficient statistical power.

## 9. Edge cases and warnings

- **No category demand, but problem searches exist:** Explore problem content separately; do not assume informational intent is equivalent to buying intent. [S1]
- **Ten relevant monthly searches:** Assess their value and paid availability; a volume cutoff would discard potentially useful demand. [S1]
- **Strong brand search, weak discovery:** Separate reports before claiming category capture is successful. [S5]
- **Uncertain positioning:** Favor flexible learning and modest foundations over a large untested SEO program. [S1]
- **Very small enterprise market:** Align account familiarity with sales activity and decision-maker coverage. Advertising can support sales rather than replace it. [S2][S3]
- **Many cheap trials:** Investigate activation and downstream value before scaling. [S2][S6]
- **AI-assisted discovery:** Buyers may describe problems conversationally and receive category recommendations. Organic AI visibility can bridge discovery and search; treat it separately from bought search presence and never promise inclusion. Platform capabilities change, so verify current mechanics before implementation. [S1]
- **Low CPC or high traffic used as the deciding argument:** Return to buyer intent and each channel's role. [S1]
- **One fixed budget ratio across companies:** Rebuild allocation from the business situation. [S2]

The output should make the buyer-state diagnosis, channel roles and next learning decision explicit. Keep agency illustrations distinct from independently verified performance evidence and avoid borrowing their results as promises for a new company.
