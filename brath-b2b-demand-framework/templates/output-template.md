# B2B channel strategy brief

Use this fill-in structure for a recommendation. Bracketed fields are intentional template slots. Complete them with evidence or explicit unknowns. References to [S#] describe framework reasoning; company-specific claims need their own provenance. Budget and review fields are package operating guidance, not published universal rules.

## 1. Decision and recommendation

**Decision requested:** [The channel, sequencing or allocation question.]
**Recommendation:** [Capture first / education first / combined system.]
**Reason:** [Buyer search behavior and awareness evidence that explains the branch.]
**Confidence:** [High / medium / low, with a concrete reason.]
**What would change this recommendation:** [Specific contrary evidence.]

Guidance: Lead with the decision. Name the segment and geography to which it applies. Avoid claiming that all buyers share the same awareness state.

## 2. Business context

| Field | Fill in | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer and use case | [Product and operational problem] | Explain what the buyer is purchasing |
| Category awareness | [Known category / unfamiliar solution / mixed / unknown] | Support with evidence |
| Priority markets | [Countries and languages] | Include actual delivery and service limits |
| Ideal companies | [Industry, size, needs and exclusions] | Define plausible buying fit |
| Buying committee | [Users, influencers and budget owners] | Do not equate the first clicker with the buyer |
| Sales model | [Product-led / sales-led / hybrid] | Identify purchase friction and trust requirements |
| Economics | [Deal value, margin or lifetime value, sales cycle] | Separate supplied facts from modeling assumptions |
| Resources | [Media, creative, SEO and internal capacity] | State limits and whether proposed spend is authorized |

## 3. Search-demand evidence

| Query family and market | Intent class | Evidence and date | Status | Commercial behavior or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Buyer wording] | [Brand / category / competitor / problem] | [Interview, tool or report] | [Observed / estimated / unknown] | [Fit, activation, opportunities or missing data] |

Guidance: Include representative actual wording when available. Tool estimates are not observed traffic. Separate scarcity from absence and problem interest from purchase intent. Mention delivery constraints before inferring no demand from a quiet account.

## 4. Channel roles and sequencing

| Activity | Buyer state and job | Start now or condition | Priority and resources | Evidence needed next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Ads | [Capture which intent] | [Now / if relevant searches found] | [Proposed cap and rationale] | [Queries, fit and commercial outcomes] |
| SEO | [Organic visibility for which demand] | [Foundation / validated expansion] | [Effort and full cost] | [Useful intent and strategic confidence] |

Guidance: Omit activities without a useful role and explain why. Do not invent a mandatory split or spend to fill the table. Capture scarce valuable searches without pretending they can absorb an unlimited budget. Explain any geographic difference between capture and brand investment.

## 5. Education and connected experience

**Audience:** [Who needs to learn or remember something.]
**Current understanding:** [Problem-aware, category-aware or uncertain.]
**Message hypothesis:** [Problem + explanation + proof + useful next action.]
**Creative and format rationale:** [Why this can earn enough attention for the task.]
**Consistent elements:** [Proposition, brand cues, proof and landing-page continuation.]
**Sales or product connection:** [Outbound support, buying committee, walkthrough or trial activation.]

Guidance: Established categories may need preference; unfamiliar categories need understanding. Choose LinkedIn for useful professional targeting rather than as a default for all B2B.

## 6. Measurement plan

**Primary commercial outcome:** [Activated user, accepted lead, opportunity, paying customer or other justified stage.]
**Capture diagnostics:** [Relevant queries, supported-market traffic, fit and progression.]
**Education diagnostics:** [Target-account reach, repeated exposure, understanding, recognition and sales feedback.]
**Signal verification:** [Actual action confirmed in CRM or product system; proxy-to-outcome checks.]
**Attribution boundary:** [Brand/non-brand separation and what journey influence remains unknown.]
**Cost boundary:** [Media, management, creative, expert time and other included costs.]
**Evidence limit:** [Sample size, sales-cycle lag and unsupported assumptions.]

Guidance: Distinguish diagnostic activity from business success. A conversion report does not establish causality or demand creation.
