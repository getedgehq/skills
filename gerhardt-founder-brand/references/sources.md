# Sources

All five sources were fetched successfully as public HTML with curl on October 2, 2026, and their transcript text was inspected. No book text, paid material, or book-listing description is used. Identifiers are local to this package and replace the original file's numbering. All method explanations are paraphrases; no source quotations are reproduced.

1. [S1] **Fostering founder brand as a competitive advantage with Dave Gerhardt**, How to Win with Peep Laja. URL: https://howtowin.transistor.fm/episodes/dave-gerhardt/transcript
   Contribution: Gerhardt explains founder commitment, educational social content, product and story alignment, contrasting Drift and Privy distribution, and qualitative evidence alongside metrics.

2. [S2] **Dave Gerhardt: Episode 878**, Author Hour, February 15, 2022. URL: https://scribemedia.com/author-hour/episode/ep-878-dave-gerhardt-episode-878
   Contribution: Gerhardt explains storytelling, relevant backstory, customer obstacles, introversion, flexible channel order, audience benefit, internal customer proximity, and feedback as input to judgment.

3. [S3] **Content, Community, and Building a 200,000+ B2B Audience with Dave**, Exit Five episode 225. URL: https://exitfive.com/podcast/content-community-and-building-a-200-000-b2b-audience-with-dave/
   Contribution: Gerhardt describes niche focus, an idea backlog, repurposing strong content, and using audience response to develop longer content and event programming.

4. [S4] **Why Every Founder Needs a LinkedIn Strategy with Brad Zomick**, Exit Five episode 275. URL: https://exitfive.com/podcast/why-every-founder-needs-a-linkedin-strategy-with-brad-zomick/
   Contribution: Gerhardt describes founder interviews, transcripts and voice notes for AI-assisted writing, preservation of personality, and his own scheduling experiments.

5. [S5] **How This B2B Brand is Building Community**, Exit Five conversation with Daniel Cmejla. URL: https://exitfive.com/podcast/how-this-b2b-brand-is-building-community/
   Contribution: Gerhardt recounts choosing LinkedIn from buyer presence and observed response, distinguishes an audience from a hosted community, and warns against mechanically repeating old playbooks.

## Attribution boundaries

These are conversations with multiple speakers. Cite Gerhardt's own explanations as his method. Peep Laja's commentary and inserted guests in [S1], Brad Zomick's suggestions in [S4], and Daniel Cmejla's tactics in [S5] are not automatically Gerhardt prescriptions. In particular, Cmejla's influence-map terminology is not presented here as a Gerhardt framework. Episode introductions, sponsor copy, platform claims, and historical performance numbers do not establish current guarantees.

The operational template, claim ledger, review checklist, and invented examples are adaptations created for this package. Citations in those files identify the principle being applied, not a claim that Gerhardt authored the artifact.
