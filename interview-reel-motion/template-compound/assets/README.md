# assets

Media paths in reel.json and beats.json are relative to this folder. The starter reel expects:

- `footage/interview.mp4`: the locked interview cut source (1440x1920 or 1080x1920, 30 fps)
- `brand/logo.png`: a square logo for the cover chip and the lower third (official asset, unaltered)
- `music/bed.mp3`: a music bed under the voice
- `sfx/whoosh.mp3`, `sfx/click.mp3`: short effects for graphic pops (never on caption words)
- `ramp/*.mp4`: speed ramps rendered with `tools/ramp.py`

Use media you have the rights to; keep license notes for downloaded effects next to them.
