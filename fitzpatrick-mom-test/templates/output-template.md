# Customer discovery brief

Fill in the sections relevant to the task. For planning, leave evidence fields explicitly pending. For review, use supplied notes and identify gaps. For mixed work, separate the original record from proposed follow-up questions. Bracketed fields are template placeholders, not instructions to fabricate data.

The schema and status labels are package adaptations. Method citations explain why a field matters; they do not substantiate customer findings. For each finding, cite the supplied interview, note location, or observed artifact separately.

## 1. Context and decision

- **Mode:** [Planning / evidence review / mixed]. Choose the actual assignment.
- **Customer group:** [Who, relevant activity, and distinguishing context]. Avoid a demographic description with no link to the activity.
- **Stage and offer:** [Idea / prototype / available product; what can honestly be offered]. Do not assume a product is ready.
- **Decision:** [The concrete choice this work must inform]. Example: whether to test report generation before a dashboard.
- **Inputs and limits:** [Supplied materials, missing context, explicit assumptions]. Name the source of each interview record.
- **Conversation goal:** [Learning / selling / planned transition]. Keep behavior understanding distinct from purchasing progress. [S2]

## 2. Three learning objectives

For each objective, repeat:

- **Unknown:** [A decision-relevant uncertainty]. Favor behavior, alternatives, or purchasing constraints. [S4]
- **Current assumption:** [What the team presently believes]. Label it as a belief.
- **Disconfirming answer:** [What could undermine that belief]. Include the uncomfortable possibility. [S5]
- **Decision consequence:** [What the team would change if that answer were supported].

## 3. Participants and access

- **Relevant experience:** [Why this person can describe the situation].
- **Role and authority:** [User, advocate, buyer, approver, or unknown].
- **Relationship and recruiting route:** [How reached; any friendship or existing commercial relationship]. Friendly first contacts help practice but require careful interpretation. [S6]
- **Conversation context:** [Practical setting and duration]. Keep the guide useful for a short exchange. [S4]
- **Coverage gaps:** [Missing roles or contexts that limit the conclusion].

## 4. Conversation guide or question audit

Repeat this block for each main objective:

- **Original question, if supplied:** [Exact user-provided wording]. Preserve the record during an audit.
- **Risk:** [Idea judgment, forecast, generalization, leading premise, or none].
- **Improved opening:** [A neutral invitation to reconstruct a relevant past episode]. [S3]
- **Conditional probes:** [Short prompts selected according to the answer]. Cover sequence, rationale, effort, alternatives, and outcome rather than interrogating a checklist.
- **Evidence sought:** [What answer would resolve the objective].
- **If no episode exists:** [Record absence; ask about adjacent experience where relevant].
- **Pitch boundary:** [Where, if anywhere, the guide explicitly transitions to an offer].

## 6. Feature and workflow fit

- **Requested feature:** [What the customer proposed, if applicable].
- **Underlying result:** [What they want to accomplish and the evidence supporting that interpretation]. [S5]
- **Current workaround:** [What they already do].
- **Product boundary:** [The part of the activity the proposal would handle].
- **Surrounding tools and habits:** [What it must cooperate with].
- **Adoption friction:** [Behavior or belief changes required; unknowns]. Strong problem evidence does not prove solution fit. [S7]

## 7. Commitment and advancement

Complete only when there is an offer or meaningful experiment:

- **Ask:** [Specific action involving time, reputation, money, or another meaningful resource]. [S4]
- **Purpose:** [The uncertainty or purchase step this action addresses].
- **Status:** [Requested / agreed / completed / declined]. Do not upgrade a promise to completed action.
- **Evidence and timing:** [What actually happened, or agreed next date].
- **Meaning:** [What this demonstrates and what it cannot establish].
- **Remaining purchase path:** [Stakeholders, approvals, commercial terms, and unresolved authority]. [S2]
- **If declined:** [The explanation, follow-up, and decision implication]. Avoid treating all refusals as equivalent.

## 8. Decision note and next action

- **Changed belief:** [Before and after, or explicitly no change].
- **Supporting evidence:** [Relevant facts with customer evidence locations].
- **Contrary evidence:** [Facts that weaken the recommendation].
- **Remaining uncertainty:** [The unresolved risk that matters most].
- **Recommendation:** [Proceed with a bounded test / revise / investigate / pause]. Explain the link to evidence.
- **Next action:** [Conversation, prototype task, or buying step; owner and date when supplied or agreed]. Experience-led value may need an earlier prototype. [S4] [S6]
- **Review result:** [Pass, or unresolved failed gates from the review checklist].

Reflect on what information changes the team's thinking and behavior. This final synthesis adapts the learning reflection principle in [S8]; it is not an expert-prescribed report format.
