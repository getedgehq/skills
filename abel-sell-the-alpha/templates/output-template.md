# Account-specific enterprise sales brief

Use the fields below as the deliverable. Replace braces with actual information. Keep unknowns visible. Omit a genuinely irrelevant section with a short reason, especially sales handoff. Fields and status labels are package conventions derived from the method, not an official Abel worksheet.

Source citations support the method. Account evidence needs its own provenance, such as supplied call notes, an authorized research URL, or a user-provided document. A method citation cannot substantiate a buyer's savings or commitment.

## 1. Assignment and motion

- **Product and maturity:** {What exists today, what is unfinished, and what is demonstrated.}
- **Account and buyer:** {Named organization, role, and why this account fits.}
- **Task and stage:** {Prepare outreach, plan discovery, shape an offer, unblock a deal, or assess handoff.}
- **Current revenue and wins:** {Relevant enterprise history; distinguish small business revenue and unknowns.}
- **Motion decision:** {Enterprise or low-touch, with observed buying behavior and economics.}
- **Main uncertainty:** {The unanswered question that determines the next move.}

Use motion reasoning from [method.md](../references/method.md#1-choose-the-motion). Do not let company size alone settle the sales model. [S2]

**Current capability:** {What the buyer can do now.}

| Statement | Status | Provenance | Validation or implication |

## 4. Outreach and discovery plan

**Outreach, three or four sentences:** {Role relevance, useful observation or explicit hypothesis, and invitation.} [S6]

**Discovery questions:** {Prior attempts, measurement, ownership, investment, why now, and evidence that would contradict the hypothesis.} [S3]

**Demo plan, if appropriate:** {Replay confirmed concerns; invite a starting point; show the one or two relevant capabilities. Do not fill this with a generic product tour.} [S3]

**Next conversation:** {Purpose, participants, proposed date, and what the buyer has actually agreed to.} [S6]

## 5. Engagement and commercial proposal

- **Engagement type:** {Design partnership, evaluation of a built product, bounded service entry, or deployment; explain why.}
- **Scope and access:** {Customer profile, contained use case, users, deliverables, duration, and exclusions.}
- **Participation and evidence:** {Each party's work and what will demonstrate useful progress.}
- **Price and rationale:** {Proposed amount, term, delivery cost, value assumptions, and approval status.}
- **Introductory arrangement:** {Any discount or learning fee, what it buys, and when it ends.}
- **Future destination:** {Product conversion or expansion, additional scope, expected pricing, and conditions.}
- **Limitations:** {Unfinished capabilities and delivery dependencies affecting expectations.}

Choose the engagement from the underlying uncertainty. Use Abel's enterprise price discussion as context, not an automatic quote. Make service-to-product transitions explicit. [S2] [S5]
