# Public sources

All sources below were fetched from Ann Handley's public website on 2026-10-02. The linked infographic under S1 was also fetched and inspected. No book, paywalled material, or third-party source is used. Titles identify sources; the method and illustrations paraphrase their guidance.

[S1] **A Writing GPS: Step-by-Step Directions for Writing Your Next Piece of Content [Infographic].** https://annhandley.com/writing-gps-step-by-step-guide-to-creating-content-infographic/
Contributes the sequence from business purpose and reader reframing through evidence, organization, rough drafting, revision, headline, editing, readability, and next action. The public infographic inspected with this article is https://annhandley.com/ah/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/AnnHandley-Writing-GPS_visually-preview.png.

[S2] **13 Writing Rules.** https://annhandley.com/13-writing-rules/
Contributes reader respect, understandable language, practice, useful deadlines, rough drafts as intermediate work, and editing for clarity rather than grammar alone.

[S3] **The One Question Your Marketing Copy Should Answer.** https://annhandley.com/how-to-write-marketing-copy/
Contributes repeated reader-consequence questioning before writing, strategic pauses, empathy, and framing choices on the audience's terms.

[S4] **5 Keys to Developing a Strong Tone of Voice in your Content Marketing.** https://annhandley.com/5-keys-to-developing-a-strong-tone-of-voice/
Contributes specific voice attributes, concrete style examples, consistent pronouns, context-sensitive jargon, differentiation, and voice on small content surfaces.

[S5] **Make Your Customer the Hero of Your Story: 'The Fault in Our Stars' Video.** https://annhandley.com/make-your-customer-the-hero-of-your-story/
Contributes customer participation, attribution and republishing permission, contribution effort matched to campaign goals, and ongoing community engagement.

[S6] **3 Truths Every Story Should Reveal.** https://annhandley.com/3-truths-every-story-should-reveal/
Contributes a narrative balance among understanding the writer, recognizing oneself, and discovering an idea, with warnings about self-absorption, pandering, and lecturing.

[S7] **What's Your Writing Process? [Sneak Peek At New Data].** https://annhandley.com/writing-process/
Contributes the importance of human thinking in first drafts and the warning that habitual AI first-draft outsourcing can weaken creativity, skill, and distinctive voice.

[S8] **3 Approaches to Great Writing.** https://annhandley.com/approaches-to-great-writing/
Contributes voice-specific language, cadence, memorable word choices, and writing as a connection between people rather than mechanical correctness alone.
