# Review checklist

Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL against the finished output. Use N/A only for conditional items, such as narrative balance when no story is involved. Fix failures before calling the piece ready; if a missing fact or permission prevents a fix, label the output as proposed and state the exact gap. This review is an agent implementation of the public method.

## Pass/fail criteria

- [ ] **Purpose:** A specific business purpose guides the piece, and the copy delivers a clear reason for the intended reader to care. FAIL if it merely announces the business's excitement. [S1] [S3]
- [ ] **Reader:** The language addresses a recognizable situation and decision. It explains unfamiliar ideas while respecting the reader's intelligence. [S1] [S2]
- [ ] **Consequences:** Benefits follow reasonably from supplied capabilities. FAIL if minor features become guaranteed life, career, or financial outcomes without support. This is an evidence-handling adaptation. [S1] [S3]
- [ ] **Evidence:** Material assertions have adequate support at the stated scope. Unsupported claims are removed, narrowed, or named as unresolved outside the copy. [S1]
- [ ] **Human contribution:** Supplied observations and point of view remain visible. No invented lived experience appears as fact, and AI assistance is not presented as Handley's preferred human drafting process. [S7]
- [ ] **Customer material, if present:** Contributions have provenance, required reuse permission, and attribution. Any missing clearance is explicit. [S5]
- [ ] **Structure:** Information follows the reader's need, with sufficient connections to understand the point. FAIL if business talking points determine the order regardless of usefulness. [S1] [S2]
- [ ] **Story, if present:** The narrative reveals something about the writer, something the reader recognizes, and an idea. It avoids self-absorption, pandering, and detached lecturing. [S6]
- [ ] **Revision:** The draft has undergone a substantive clarity pass. FAIL if only grammar changed while the premise or structure still confuses. [S1] [S2]
- [ ] **Voice:** Concrete sentence choices reflect the established or explicitly provisional voice, including supporting microcopy. Pronouns are consistent within context. [S4]
- [ ] **Vocabulary and cadence:** Necessary specialist terms clarify; empty buzzwords do not substitute for meaning. Memorable wording serves connection and does not impede understanding. [S2] [S4] [S8]
- [ ] **Headline or label, if needed:** It represents the delivered idea accurately and helps the reader recognize its value or action. [S1] [S3]
- [ ] **Mechanics and surface:** Grammar, usage, punctuation, paragraphs, subheads, and lists fit the delivery format. Scanning aids have not removed necessary explanation. [S1] [S2]
- [ ] **Next step:** The ending offers an appropriate action or useful application. Link placeholders and other operational gaps are named. [S1]
- [ ] **Delivery status:** Copy and internal notes are separate. No unresolved placeholder is concealed as finished prose, no self-review is misrepresented as independent editing, and writing scope is not treated as publication authorization. These are agent adaptations. [S1] [S5]

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Practical fix | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand excitement occupies the opening | Replace it with the concrete reader situation and useful consequence. | [S3] |
| Benefit chain escalates into an unsupported promise | Return to the last meaningful consequence supported by facts. | [S1] [S3] |
| Polished prose hides an undeveloped thought | Restore the author's observations; revise the premise before the sentences. | [S2] [S7] |
| Rough drafting is treated as completion | Resolve placeholders and conduct a separate reader-focused rewrite. | [S1] [S2] |
| Voice consists of generic virtues | Define specific attributes through sentence choices and examples. | [S4] |
| Every specialist term is removed | Restore terms that aid precision or shared understanding; explain only where needed. | [S4] |
| Narrative becomes founder worship | Shorten autobiography and connect the incident to reader recognition and an idea. | [S6] |
| Reader praise sounds manufactured | Remove flattering assumptions and name an observable situation. | [S6] |
| Idea becomes a lecture | Use a relevant supplied incident or example; do not fabricate experience. | [S6] [S7] |
| Customer story lacks reuse clearance | Keep it out of ready-to-publish copy and identify the permission needed. | [S5] |
| Headline creates a promise the body cannot meet | Revise the promise to match the supported content. | [S1] [S3] |
| Compression removes meaning | Restore the missing connection, then simplify its wording. | [S2] |
| Sentence polish erases personality | Recover concrete detail and useful cadence without imposing another writer's style. | [S4] [S8] |
| Closing merely celebrates the company | State the next useful action or application for the reader. | [S1] |
