## 6. Internal positioning record

Use these six fields as a compact alignment record, optionally one per slide. [S1]

1. **Audience:** <Champion, situation, market state, and useful boundaries.>
2. **Product identity:** <Plain description and chosen category or job reference point.>
3. **Problem:** <Specific current workflow failure or unmet task.>
4. **Alternative:** <What the audience uses or does now.>
5. **Differentiation:** <How the product changes that situation, with mechanisms and evidence.>
6. **Value:** <Direct benefit, prioritized supporting arguments, and limits.>

**Position sentence:** <For CHAMPION doing JOB, PRODUCT is REFERENCE POINT that enables CAPABILITY through MECHANISM, improving on CURRENT ALTERNATIVE by DIRECT BENEFIT.>

This sentence is a completeness check for internal use. Do not force its entire structure into the H1.
