# Sources

All ten public articles below were fetched successfully with curl on 2026-10-02. Only the public page content was used, without following paid course or book material. Titles identify sources; the method and examples paraphrase their ideas.

- [S1] **7 Rules for Creating Gorgeous UI (2024 update)**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/7-rules-for-creating-gorgeous-ui-part-1.html
  Contributes overhead lighting cues, grayscale-first design, purposeful color, and generous whitespace with exceptions for strong visual personalities.
- [S2] **7 Rules for Creating Gorgeous UI, Part 2 (2024 update)**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/7-rules-for-creating-gorgeous-ui-part-2.html
  Contributes image text treatments, competing emphasis, stable interactive text styles, clean fonts, and deliberate reference study.
- [S3] **Color in UI Design: A (Practical) Framework**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/color-in-ui-design-a-practical-framework.html
  Contributes HSB variation rules, the limits of black overlays, secondary hue adjustment, and perceived lightness distinctions.
- [S4] **The 3 Laws of Locality**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/the-3-laws-of-locality.html
  Contributes object proximity, controls above their scope, nested regions, and stronger visibility for displaced actions.
- [S5] **4 Rules for Intuitive UX**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/4-rules-intuitive-ux.html
  Contributes dropdown alternatives, frequency-based attention, the squint test, long-list handling, and teaching through examples.
- [S6] **Copywork: The Ultimate Way to Rapidly Improve Your Design Skills**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/copywork-ultimate-way-rapidly-improve-design-skills.html
  Contributes controlled reproduction of work beyond current ability and articulate comparison of subtle design choices; the public page is an excerpt.
- [S7] **The King vs. Pawn Game of UI Design**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/king-vs-pawn-game-ui-design.html
  Contributes small-component practice, shape and brand coherence, uppercase tracking, contrast measurement, and contextual borders, shadows, and icons.
- [S8] **The Reframe Technique**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/the-reframe-technique.html
  Contributes identifying fixed assumptions, changing form or placement, visualizing related numbers, and checking newly introduced ambiguity.
- [S9] **The #1 Way to Spice Up Your Designs (And Create a More Cohesive Brand)**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/spice-up-designs-create-cohesive-brand.html
  Contributes recurring motifs across shapes, typography, dividers, special text, and color, with a warning that motifs cannot rescue bad fundamentals.
- [S10] **Why Beginning Designers Don't Need to Learn Grids, Type Scales, or Color Theory (and other Designer Dogma)**. https://www.learnui.design/blog/why-beginning-designers-dont-need-grids-type-scales-color-theory.html
  Contributes content-led alignment, distinguishable and consistent type roles, color practice, and prioritizing craft over personal style.
