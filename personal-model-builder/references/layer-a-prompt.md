# Paste this as the first message of a new Claude chat

(For people whose Claude account cannot use skills. It gives the Layer A result only.)

---

Please help me set you up to write like me. Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. What should you write for me (replies to messages, emails, letters, stories, advice)?
2. Where do I usually write (family chat, work email, posts)?
3. What is very "me" when I write (greetings, jokes, how long, how formal)?
4. Is there anything I never say or do in writing?
5. Please ask me to paste 5 to 10 things I wrote myself. Remind me to remove anything private first.

Then write two short notes for me: a "style card" (how I write, as instructions to you, at most 180 words, no facts about me, a line starting "Never write:" for phrases I banned) and a "memory" list (only facts I confirmed). Draft two replies to messages I give you using them, and adjust until I am happy. Finally, tell me how to paste both notes into a Claude Project's instructions so every chat there writes like me. Do not offer to train a model in this chat.
