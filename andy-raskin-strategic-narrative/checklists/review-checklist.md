# Review checklist

Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL and explain failures. Mark conditional items N/A with a reason. A hypothesis draft can be useful while failing evidence gates; label it accordingly. Do not call an output evidence-backed or ready for publication when material unsupported claims remain.

## Pass/fail gates

- [ ] **Audience:** A specific audience, decision, and requested deliverable are clear.
- [ ] **Opening:** The opening establishes buyer context rather than leading with the company, product, investors, logos, or an accusation about the buyer's failings. [S2]
- [ ] **Change:** The change is supported as happening, independent of the seller, and consequential for this buyer. [S1]
- [ ] **Mindset:** Old and new games describe winning mindsets, not a workflow cleaned up by the product. [S4]
- [ ] **Specificity:** The change goes beyond generic technology-era claims, including undifferentiated AI messaging. [S11]
- [ ] **Stakes:** Positive and negative paths are concrete, relevant, and proportional to available evidence. [S2]
- [ ] **Enemy:** The old mindset is the antagonist; competitor discussion concerns supported assumptions rather than insults. [S5]
- [ ] **Mission:** Success describes the buyer's future, not possession of the product. [S1]
- [ ] **Mission level:** The goal is aspirational enough to matter and specific enough to define a distinct movement. [S7]
- [ ] **Decision filter:** The mission implies a meaningful strategic choice and permits reasonable disagreement. [S7][S12]
- [ ] **Obstacles:** Barriers explain why the mission is hard to achieve without help. [S2]
- [ ] **Capabilities:** Every included feature or service answers a mission obstacle; the explanation states how it helps. [S1][S2]
- [ ] **Proof:** Evidence addresses mission progress, with customer stories preferred and demos or internal results honestly identified. [S1]
- [ ] **Evidence integrity:** No invented statistics, quotes, customers, winner examples, exclusivity claims, or implied causal guarantees appear. Gaps remain explicit. Package safeguard.
- [ ] **Adaptation:** Audience variants preserve a coherent shared change and tailor futures, barriers, and proof; justified pivots are identified. [S3]
- [ ] **Compactness:** Each storyboard part carries one useful idea; detail sits in notes or evidence rather than overwhelming the core message. [S10]
- [ ] **Leadership truth:** The team can stand behind the narrative; unresolved top-message choices are visible for leadership. [S8][S13]
- [ ] **Citation hygiene:** Every method citation resolves to references/sources.md. Business claims use their own evidence, not framework citations. Package safeguard.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic AI transformation opener | Establishes no differentiated buyer mindset. [S11] | Name the specific change in what buyers must do to win. |
| Product before/after presented as a new game | Seller improvement replaces external demand. [S4] | Remove the product mentally and identify the change that remains. |
| Seller credentials first | Asks for interest before establishing relevance. [S2] | Move credentials into supporting proof after the mission. |
| Buyer blamed for doing things wrong | Can provoke defensiveness. [S2] | Discuss shared changes and ask about the buyer's experience. |
| Competitor list as villain | Misidentifies the enemy and returns to superiority claims. [S5][S6] | Name the obsolete assumption; compare alternatives only with support. |
| Vague happiness or growth mission | Defines no movement or decision filter. [S7] | Choose a more specific goal state that still matters to the buyer. |
| Feature-level mission | Undersells the goal and confuses help with success. [S7] | Move one level up to the buyer condition the feature enables. |
| Mission softened until universal | Loses its strategic usefulness. [S12] | State the tradeoff and the plausible buyer who would reject it. |
| Feature parade | Capabilities lack purpose. [S1][S2] | Map each feature to a barrier, then remove unmapped features. |
| Logos presented as outcome evidence | Purchase does not establish the promised future. [S2] | Show a relevant customer result or a clearly bounded demonstration. |
| Story fragments for every persona | Brand loses a coherent entry point. [S3] | Share the change, then tailor the mission and obstacles. |
| Overloaded manifesto | Avoids hard choices about what matters. [S10] | Reduce to a compact storyboard before writing longer prose. |
| Consultant line retained despite team objections | Weakens truth and alignment. [S13] | Examine the objection and revise the underlying claim. |

## Review handoff

State the overall status, the most consequential failures, and the smallest next steps needed to resolve them. Distinguish an appealing but unverified hypothesis from a supported narrative. If proof cannot support the promise, narrow the promise; if the shift cannot withstand scrutiny, return to discovery.
