# Creator campaign decision memo

Use this template for planning, auditing, or rebooking. Replace bracketed fields. Write unknown when evidence is absent and explain how it affects the recommendation. References to method sections point to [references/method.md](../references/method.md).

## 1. Decision

- Decision requested: [select / evaluate / rebook / scale / long-term package]. Name the exact spending decision.
- Recommendation: [stop / repair / observe / bounded retest / rebook / staged scale / commit with conditions]. Lead with the action and its reason.
- Confidence: [low / medium / high], because [specific measurement and history]. Distinguish forecast confidence from observed evidence.
- Maximum new commitment: [currency and amount]. Include already committed exposure separately.
- Owner and next review: [name or role, date, observation to obtain]. Use a calendar date when available.

## 2. Goal and economics

- Brand, product, geography, channel: [fields]. State customer and product context.
- Acquisition target: [distinct new customers, CAC ceiling, deadline]. Do not substitute total orders.
- Net basket revenue and contribution: [amount, margin rate, costs included]. Describe discounts, refunds, tax basis, and fulfillment treatment.
- Cash/payback and loss limits: [amount and horizon]. Separate first-order viability from validated lifetime economics.
- Spend convention: [creator/media spend M, additional costs, all-in B]. Prevent denominator changes across options.

## 3. Creator and audience hypothesis

- Creator, format, proposed dates: [fields]. Identify each placement separately.
- Target overlap and brand fit: [evidence and gaps]. Include location, active audience, content style, and prior integrations.
- Estimated delivery range: [views / impressions / unique reach]. Name the denominator.
- Comparable evidence: [category, platform, audience, age of data]. Explain limits rather than citing follower count as acquisition evidence.
- Creative requirements and freedom: [product facts, benefits, CTA, creator discretion]. Include rights only when paid reuse is proposed.

## 7. Booking and discount choices

- Options compared: [single booking / staged rebooking / fixed package]. Include real quotes and comparable forecast periods.
- Later placement economics: [placement-level delivery, new customers, marginal CAC, contribution]. Include decay and overlap assumptions.
- Staffelrabatte assessment: [fee savings versus delivery/yield decline]. Show effective TKP with consistent denominator.
- Commitment and exit exposure: [unused fees, notice, performance gate, rights, exclusivity]. Label proposed terms as negotiation guidance.
- Discount-code phase: [baseline / benchmark translation / bounded reduction / not applicable]. State UTM coverage and effect on net contribution.
