Credit: Brendan Gahan and Creator Authority for the public method; the named publishers and authors for interview coverage and research context.

Research date: 2026-10-02. All seven sources below were fetched directly with curl, followed through redirects, returned HTTP 200, and exposed readable public text without authentication. Sources are distinct pages, but [S2] and [S3] discuss the same research and [S6] and [S7] are commercially interested Favikon coverage. This is not seven independent validations of effectiveness. No paywalled text was used and no third-party skill was copied.

Neither is included as a cited source, and search snippets from them were not used as evidence. LinkedIn comments and autogenerated page summaries were excluded from attribution to Gahan. The actual Advertising Week talk and the interview video underlying [S7] were not reviewed; this package claims only the fetched public text.
