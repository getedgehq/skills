# B2B creator campaign blueprint

Use the relevant sections for the user's assignment. Replace bracketed fields. Mark unknowns and assumptions visibly. This is a planning deliverable; sending outreach, publishing, and spending require authorization for those actions. See [method.md](../references/method.md) for decisions and [review-checklist.md](../checklists/review-checklist.md) before handoff.

## 1. Assignment and decision

- **Brand and offer:** [Name, product, market, and verified capability]. Explain what is being promoted without adding unsupported differentiation.
- **Requested decision:** [Launch a pilot, select creators, approve a concept, or revise a program]. State the output scope.
- **Constraints:** [Budget currency and ceiling, dates, geography, production capacity, channel limits]. Separate firm limits from preferences.
- **Evidence status:** [Provided facts, assumptions, unknowns]. Name the source of each material claim.

## 2. Buyer insight and primary objective

- **Buyer:** [Professional role, industry, company profile, purchase influence]. Include other committee roles only when relevant.
- **Trigger and obstacle:** [What prompts attention and what blocks confidence]. Link to customer or sales evidence.
- **Differentiator:** [Supported product advantage or narrower useful benefit]. Avoid unsupported uniqueness.
- **Insight:** [This buyer wants X but hesitates because Y; evidence Z supports the concept]. Keep it concrete.
- **One primary objective:** [Awareness, consideration, or qualified lead response]. Describe the behavior required.
- **Primary metric and definition:** [Metric, denominator, qualification criteria, and collection method]. Do not substitute a proxy silently.
- **Baseline, threshold, and date:** [Known baseline or unknown; agreed pilot target; review date]. Label targets as targets.
- **Secondary diagnostics:** [A small set of supporting indicators]. Explain what each can diagnose.

## 3. Concept and creator shortlist

- **Concept:** [Buyer problem, creator contribution, audience value, product role, next step]. Explain why the idea merits attention without sponsorship.
- **Asset value:** [What the audience learns or can use]. For lead generation, describe the actual resource and how the creator helps build it.

- **Selection rationale:** [Why the proposed creator has credible authority for this topic]. Do not use follower count as a proxy for expertise.
- **Draft invitation:** [Specific relevant observation, paid proposal, concise ask]. Write original copy rather than reproducing a source's example pitch.
- **Vetting agenda:** [Product experience, genuine interest, proposed changes, schedule, conflicts]. Record what remains unverified.

## 5. Creator brief and video outline

- **Goal and audience:** [One objective and specific professional audience].
- **Core message:** [One defensible idea].
- **Reasons to believe:** [Up to three supported proof points, provenance, and allowed wording]. Identify gaps rather than fabricating a third point.
- **Deliverables:** [Assets, posting expectations, resource contribution, dates]. Separate essential items from options.
- **Creative freedom:** [What the creator decides]. Preserve honest viewpoint and natural style.
- **Required boundaries:** [Unsupported claims to avoid, factual reviewer, disclosure, confidential details]. Make constraints usable.
- **Video outline:** [Buyer problem, expert insight, demonstration, limitation, next step]. Suggested length and style are test choices.
- **Logistics:** [Disclosure placement, links, tracking parameters, hashtags if relevant, review contact]. Confirm requirements before implementation.

## 6. Distribution and measurement

- **Organic route:** [Creator post and approved supporting activity]. State realistic audience uncertainty.
- **Paid route:** [Thought Leader Ads or suitable alternative, professional targeting, budget, dates]. Check current platform eligibility.
- **Permissions and rights:** [Content authorization, usage scope, outstanding dependencies].
- **Tracking:** [Asset IDs, creator IDs, message variants, spend, link parameters, outcome capture]. Keep organic and paid reporting distinct where available.
- **Quality review:** [How sales or another owner checks audience and lead relevance].
- **Comparability limits:** [Unequal spend, different audiences, small samples, long purchase cycles].
- **Decision rules:** [Evidence required to scale, revise, hold, or stop]. Establish the review window before launch.

## 7. Handoff and evidence

- **Next owner and date:** [Named person or role and next decision]. Do not invent an owner commitment.
- **Open questions:** [Only gaps that could change the decision].
- **Review status:** [Pass/fail items and resolutions].
- **Method citations:** [Relevant S identifiers with links to the source register]. Use them for method attribution.
- **Client evidence:** [Research, product verification, analytics, creator quotes]. Use it for campaign-specific assertions.
- **Authorization status:** [Planning only or specific actions authorized]. List remaining implementation dependencies without treating draft approval as permission to spend.
