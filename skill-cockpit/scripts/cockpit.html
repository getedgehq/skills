Edge Skill cockpit
local

Every time your agent fails, it gets a chance to get better.

Failures come from your own session logs. Candidate skills are re-run on the exact task, judged blind, and only reach your harness when they measurably win. Everything runs on this machine.

Mine your corrections→Find or draft a skill→Re-run the task→Blind judge→Adopt or reject
Local only. Actions start the skill-miner and skill-eval-loop scripts with your own agent CLI and models. Evals are never overwritten, and every decision is appended to ledger.jsonl.
