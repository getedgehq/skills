# Worked examples

Both examples are invented illustrations. Company names, budgets, targets, buyer research and results are fictional. They demonstrate the package's implementation guidance, not Leeuwis case studies or promised outcomes. Source identifiers refer to principles documented in references/method.md.

## Illustration 1: Repair a volume-led agency brief

### Situation

HarborOps sells workflow software to European industrial distributors. Marketing's hypothetical baseline shows 300 inquiries, 30 accepted SQLs and 12 opportunities in a mature quarter. Average opportunity value is EUR 25,000 annual contract value. Sales rejects most inquiries from very small companies or buyers seeking a feature the product does not support.

### Weak first draft

Spend EUR 15,000 per month on LinkedIn. Generate 450 leads per quarter at the lowest possible CPL. Target operations job titles across Europe. Offer an ebook and pass every download to Sales. Report leads and creative delivered at month end.

### Improved result

**Business goal:** Propose EUR 500,000 of unweighted, program-sourced pipeline created over a quarter, measured on annual contract value. The Marketing lead owns strategy and full-funnel accountability. The agency owns execution. RevOps owns event integrity. Sales owns acceptance and rejection.

**ICP:** Industrial distributors in Germany and the Netherlands with 100 to 500 employees, multiple warehouses and a supported ERP integration. Exclude unsupported integration requirements and single-location businesses. These criteria are invented research inputs for this illustration.

**Persona messages:** The operations champion needs to reduce manual handoffs between warehouses. The finance buyer needs evidence that implementation effort is justified by operating savings. Validate both problems against sales-call samples; provide approved product proof rather than an invented savings percentage.

**Quality:** A SQL is a Sales-accepted contact from a qualifying account with a confirmed handoff problem, an applicable use case and willingness to discuss evaluation. An ebook download alone does not qualify. Use explicit rejection reasons and return them to the agency weekly.

**Target model:** EUR 500,000 / EUR 25,000 = 20 opportunities. At the baseline SQL-to-opportunity rate of 40%, the plan requires 50 accepted SQLs. At 10% inquiry-to-SQL conversion, 500 inquiries would be required. If improved qualification raises that rate to 20%, 250 would suffice. Neither assumption is a result or a guarantee. This exposes the weakness of promising a lead count without its conversion economics.

**Program:** Propose EUR 8,000 per month for LinkedIn demand generation, EUR 5,000 for high-intent capture and EUR 2,000 reserved pending evidence. These allocations are illustrative. LinkedIn tests the warehouse handoff problem with relevant proof; capture uses a matching commercial destination and SQL feedback. Educational engagement is a diagnostic signal, followed by cohort quality and pipeline review.

**Measurement and review:** Pass campaign and test identifiers into the CRM, verify qualification events and deduplicate opportunities. Compare acceptance and rejection weekly; review pipeline only after a declared cohort maturity window. If the agency attracts the wrong use case, change the message and qualification path before raising spend. If the assumed target is unaffordable, revise budget, timeframe or target openly.

### Rules that made the difference

- Briefing ICP, SQL quality and pipeline changes what the agency optimizes. [S1]
- One full-funnel owner and rejection feedback connect execution to Sales outcomes. [S4]
- A baseline and trustworthy conversion path support the next controlled change. [S3]
- Persona messages, budget rows and forecasting math are package implementation guidance.

## Illustration 2: Resist a misleading channel cut

### Situation

SignalBoard sells analytics software through a five-month buying cycle. Fictional CRM reports show LinkedIn last-click cost per SQL of EUR 900 and search cost per SQL of EUR 350. Finance requests lower acquisition cost. Several recent opportunities encountered educational content before later converting through search, but attribution is incomplete.

### Weak first draft

Pause LinkedIn next week and put its EUR 12,000 monthly budget into Google. Search is cheaper, so this will lower CAC and hit next month's pipeline target. Increase retargeting because its conversion report is strongest.

### Improved result

**Decision:** Do not infer customer acquisition cost from cost per SQL. Reconcile actual customer acquisition cohorts and cost definitions first. Treat the cut as a hypothesis that may affect future pipeline, not a guaranteed efficiency improvement.

**ICP and personas:** Focus the initial test on B2B software firms with an expanding revenue team and inconsistent account reporting. A RevOps champion needs dependable account data; an executive buyer needs evidence that the system improves decision quality. Product, Marketing and Sales agree on the supported problem and proof before additional production.

**Campaign jobs:** LinkedIn explains the reporting problem to suitable future buyers. Search captures explicit solution interest through matching product and demo pages. Retargeting supports an existing audience and is evaluated in the context of how that audience was acquired. Classify each actual campaign by its audience and offer.

**Plan:** Retain a bounded LinkedIn test while reviewing search's additional qualified demand capacity. Choose one message hypothesis and a spend ceiling. Record an immediate audience-response review and a later pipeline review consistent with the observed buying cycle. The exact dates and allocation require business evidence, rather than a universal rule.

**Measurement:** Preserve first and later touches where available. Compare mature, deduplicated cohorts and gather buyer discovery accounts as supporting evidence. Report sourced and influenced pipeline separately. Do not count one deal twice or claim that touching a deal proves incremental impact.

**Agency operating brief:** Marketing owns the allocation decision, RevOps reconciles events and Sales provides acceptance and rejection samples. The agency reports relevant audience response, accepted SQLs, opportunity progression and spending against limits. A next-month gap is handled separately from the acquisition plan; newly educated buyers are not assumed to close immediately.

**Decision rule:** Expand capture if additional commercial demand produces acceptable SQL quality and economics. Continue the generation test if relevant response warrants a later outcome review. Change or stop it if repeated tests attract unsuitable buyers or fail to produce useful evidence within the agreed limit. Return an uncertainty statement when sample sizes cannot support a decision.

### Rules that made the difference

- Alignment makes the advertised solution credible across the customer journey. [S2]
- Reliable downstream measurement precedes confident optimization. [S3]
- Shared ownership prevents channel metrics from becoming disconnected objectives. [S4]
- The future-demand versus existing-intent distinction has partial indexed support. [S5]
- Cohort comparisons, bounded tests and persona details are package implementation guidance.
