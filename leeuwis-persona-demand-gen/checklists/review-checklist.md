# Review checklist

Mark each row PASS or FAIL and supply evidence. A material failure makes the brief conditional or incomplete until fixed. Do not convert missing data into an assumed pass. When a criterion is irrelevant, mark PASS with a reason explaining why it does not affect the requested decision.

| Criterion | PASS condition | FAIL condition | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attribution and scope | Credit is retained; published claims and package additions are distinguished | Persona fields or formulas represented as a verified proprietary framework | Relabel implementation guidance and cite the actual evidence |
| Source integrity | Only retrieved evidence is used; indexed profile limits are visible | Profile described as fully fetched, or other authors' recommended posts used as Leeuwis evidence | Correct provenance and remove unsupported claims |
| Assignment | A concrete decision, period, budget and product are stated | A generic campaign checklist replaces the requested brief | State the decision and business constraints |
| Baseline | Comparable historical performance and missing data are recorded | Rebuild recommended without knowing current outcomes | Preserve and inspect a baseline first |
| Alignment | Product, Marketing and Sales share a substantiated customer story | Ad promise exceeds product capability or contradicts Sales | Resolve or narrow the claim |
| Ownership | Client strategy owner and one full-funnel accountable person are named | Agency owns CPL while nobody owns CRM outcomes | Assign accountability and outcome access |
| Executable ICP | Inclusions, exclusions and purchase circumstances are specific | Broad sector and title labels stand in for fit | Use accepted and rejected customer evidence |
| Useful personas | Role, problem, trigger, objection and proof change the message | Decorative persona names or unsupported buyer psychology | Replace with evidence and a testable hypothesis |
| SQL quality | Sales acceptance, rejection and pending states are defined | Every download becomes an SQL | Agree explicit acceptance and disposition criteria |
| Pipeline target | Amount, period, contribution and value basis are clear | Lead count used as a revenue proxy | Define opportunities and pipeline, then derive input needs |
| Forecast arithmetic | Rates use coherent cohorts; counts and capacity reconcile | Mixed rates, impossible counts or invented benchmarks | Recalculate and use labelled scenarios |
| Program split | Creation and capture have different buyer jobs and evaluation windows | Both optimized only for low CPL | Assign readiness, offer and relevant measures |
| Allocation | Budget sums reconcile and the rationale is contextual | A fixed split is attributed to Leeuwis without evidence | Explain assumptions and decision conditions |
| Measurement | CRM identifiers, events and qualified feedback are verified | Platform form fills assumed equivalent to Sales outcomes | Trace records and reconcile differences |
| Attribution limits | Sourced and influenced views remain separate | Double-counting or causal claims from touchpoints alone | Deduplicate and disclose evidence strength |
| Testing | One clear hypothesis, limit, evidence standard and next decision exist | Multiple simultaneous changes prevent interpretation | Narrow the test and define inconclusive handling |
| Timing | Outcomes are evaluated against observed cohort maturity | A new campaign promises immediate pipeline repair | Separate near-term and future contributions |
| Feedback | Rejections affect the next campaign brief | Sales findings remain in the CRM unused | Name the feedback owner and decision rhythm |
| External action scope | The deliverable respects the user's authorization | Planning silently triggers spend or sends a brief | Return the reviewable plan within authorized scope |

## Common failure modes

**A cheaper CPL is called success.** Examine accepted SQLs and opportunity progression. Improve fit or offer quality when low-cost submissions do not become viable deals. The original brief may be causing the behavior. [S1]

**The agency is blamed before its brief is inspected.** Compare requested outputs, supplied ICP information and access to downstream outcomes. Fix the shared operating structure as well as any execution issue. [S1][S4]

**Precise targeting is expected to solve a vague message.** Translate real buyer problems into copy and qualification. Platform filters and message relevance must work together. Detailed persona mapping is this package's implementation guidance.

**LinkedIn is removed because search has a lower last-click number.** Check whether the comparison measures customer acquisition, cost per SQL or merely form fills. Evaluate campaign roles and mature outcomes. Partial indexed evidence supports the warning about future demand loss; it does not justify protecting every LinkedIn campaign. [S5]

**A complete rebuild erases the baseline.** Inventory the inherited account and repair measurement, then test one meaningful change with an explicit success measure. [S3]

**CTR becomes a substitute for business quality.** Keep engagement as diagnostic evidence. Check responding buyer fit, qualification and cohort progression before scaling. This distinction is package implementation guidance.

**Product and Sales cannot support the ad promise.** Narrow the advertised outcome to a demonstrated solution and reconnect the story across teams before increasing spend. [S2]

**A quarterly target conceals timing and capacity gaps.** Show opportunity requirements, qualification capacity and cohort maturity. Revise the target, timeframe or budget when evidence does not support the plan. These feasibility checks are package implementation guidance.

**Evidence is overstated to make the package look complete.** Keep the fifth source labelled as indexed excerpts and the agency holding page outside substantive evidence. Seek direct permalinks during future maintenance. Do not invent inaccessible posts or count excerpts from one page as distinct sources.
