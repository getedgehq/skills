# Elon Musk: verified public quotes

Short excerpts from public essays, presentations, interviews and emails, each with its source. Excerpts were matched against the linked source when collected; links can rot, so re-check one before relying on it. Excerpts are short on purpose: quote only these words, never the paraphrase around them. Numbers are referenced from SKILL.md.

Independent reference list. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Elon Musk or any of his companies.

## How he opens, financing and negotiation

41. “The impact that Tesla will have is fairly small in and of itself.” The mission can be bigger than the company's own sales. Date: June 2, 2015 publication. Occasion: Musk's interviews with Tim Urban for Wait But Why, explaining how Tesla might change the wider automobile industry even if Tesla itself remained a small fraction of global production. Source: [Tim Urban, How Tesla Will Change The World](https://waitbutwhy.com/2015/06/how-tesla-will-change-your-life.html). Status: VERIFIED as a direct quotation in the interviewer's first-hand account.
42. “So, in desperation, we’re going to dig a tunnel. And maybe that tunnel will be successful and maybe it won’t.” State the literal solution and admit uncertainty. Date: September 6, 2018 recording, released as Joe Rogan Experience #1169. Occasion: Rogan asks why Musk started the Boring Company to address Los Angeles traffic. Source: [Full podcast transcript, Sonix](https://sonix.ai/resources/full-transcript-joe-rogan-experience-elon-musk/). Status: VERIFIED against the published episode transcription.
43. “we need to expand the scope and scale of consciousness” A very abstract ambition is possible in a long-form interview. Date: December 28, 2021. Occasion: Lex Fridman Podcast #252, discussing life's meaning and what questions humanity should ask about the universe. Source: [Full recorded-interview transcript](https://elonmuskarchive.org/video/lex-fridman-252). Status: VERIFIED.
44. “Based on the cash we have in the bank right now, we will bounce payroll next week.” Disclose the immediate consequence of a failed financing. Date: December 2008 events, recounted in Ashlee Vance's 2015 biography. Occasion: Musk describes what he told VantagePoint cofounder Alan Salzman during Tesla's emergency financing negotiations. Source: Ashlee Vance, Elon Musk: Tesla, SpaceX, and the Quest for a Fantastic Future (2015), chapter 8. Status: VERIFIED as a quotation Musk retrospectively gave his biographer, not as a 2008 recording.
45. “Physics does not care about hurt feelings. It cares about whether you got the rocket right.” Judge the physical result rather than the feelings around it. Date: 2023 publication. Occasion: Musk explaining to Walter Isaacson his approach to technical feedback at SpaceX, in the biographer's account of Starship and Raptor-engine development. Source: [Authorized excerpt from Walter Isaacson's Elon Musk, Starship section](https://www.space.com/elon-musk-walter-isaacson-book-excerpt-starship-surge). Status: VERIFIED as a direct quotation in an authorized biography excerpt. This is not from the 2008 crisis.
46. “If you're not occasionally adding things back in, you are not deleting enough.” Deletion needs to be measurable. Date: July 30, 2021 tour, published August 2021. Occasion: Tim Dodd's Everyday Astronaut Starbase interview, during Musk's explanation of the five-step engineering algorithm. Source: [Recorded-tour transcript](https://elonmuskarchive.org/video/starbase-tour-with-everyday-astronaut-part-1-202), also summarized by [Everyday Astronaut](https://everydayastronaut.com/starbase-tour-and-interview-with-elon-musk/). Status: VERIFIED.
47. “We achieved GAAP net income of over $300 million” Report the actual quarter before forecasting the next one. Date: October 24, 2018. Occasion: Tesla third-quarter earnings call, in Musk's opening remarks. In the same passage he also reported vehicle deliveries, additional cash, and Model 3 gross margin. Source: [Earnings-call transcript with named speaker attribution](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/transcripts/23956-q3-2018/). Status: VERIFIED.
48. “what the hell is really going on?” Express a scientific mission as a direct question. Date: July 14, 2023. Occasion: xAI's founding-team X Spaces discussion. Musk offers this as a reformulation of xAI's mission to understand the universe. Source: [X Spaces recording transcript](https://elonmuskarchive.org/video/x-spaces-xai-launch-2023-07-14). Status: VERIFIED. The transcript starts mid-discussion, so this is not verified as his opening sentence.
49. “if that launch had not succeeded, that would have been the end of SpaceX.” Say exactly how close the company came to ending. Date: September 28, 2019. Occasion: Starship presentation, remembering the fourth Falcon 1 launch on September 28, 2008. Source: [Transcription of the 2019 Starship presentation](https://elonmuskinterviews.wordpress.com/2021/03/27/starship-presentation-2019-english/), from the [original SpaceX presentation recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sOpMrVnjYeY). Status: VERIFIED.
50. “a fundamental transformation of how the world works” Open with the system-level change rather than the battery. Date: April 30, 2015. Occasion: Tesla Energy and Powerwall launch in Hawthorne, California. Musk identifies the energy-system problem before introducing the home battery. Source: [Full Tesla Energy launch transcript, page 1](https://fusion4freedom.com/pdfs/Elon-Musk-Introduces-Tesla-Energy.pdf#page=1), corroborated by [ABC's contemporary broadcast transcript](https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/backgroundbriefing/the-big-disconnect/6972114). Status: VERIFIED.

## Never quote these as his

1. “When something is important enough, you do it even if the odds are not in your favor.” DISPUTED as an exact primary-source transcript quotation. Versions circulate with different wording. Lex Fridman also recites a version while attributing it to Musk, which verifies Lex's attribution, not independently Musk's original utterance. The related 2014 60 Minutes interview discusses proceeding despite unfavorable odds, but should not be silently normalized to the popular quote-card wording. [2014 interview](https://www.elonmuskarchive.org/video/60-minutes-2014-03-30).
2. “Failure is an option here. If things are not failing, you are not innovating enough.” VERIFIED, not misattributed. Jennifer Reingold quoted Musk in the February 2005 Fast Company SpaceX profile. The important distinction is that this one has an attributable near-primary source. [Hondas in Space](https://www.fastcompany.com/74516/hondas-space-2).
3. “The first step is to establish that something is possible; then probability will occur.” VERIFIED as a near-primary attributed quotation. It appears in the December 2012 Esquire profile of Musk. It is presented by the journalist as something Musk says, not as a timestamped passage from a published interview recording. [Original Esquire profile](https://www.esquire.com/news-politics/a16681/elon-musk-interview-1212/).
4. Any popular quote-card line that is not in this file with a source. Several widely shared lines attributed to him have no dated recording, writing or interview behind them.
