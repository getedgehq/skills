# Worked examples

These are invented illustrations. Brands, observations, counts, dates, and proposed changes are fictional. They are not duwerk clients, published case studies, or evidence of achieved GEO improvements. Rule references point to [the method](../references/method.md); source IDs identify the public principles underlying the illustration.

## Illustration 1: A B2B software brand with inflated discovery reporting

### Situation and fictional evidence

LedgerNest offers invoice-approval software for German businesses with 20 to 100 employees. It supports approval routing and CSV accounting exports but not full accounting or payroll. Its homepage describes an all-purpose financial platform; an old partner profile calls it accounting software.

An invented audit export contains 40 valid responses from one search-enabled AI surface over four dates. Ten brand-led responses mention LedgerNest. Of 30 non-branded responses, six mention it. Two cite its domain. Four of the six mentions place it correctly in invoice approval, while two misclassify it as accounting software. On the same 30 non-branded responses, two declared competitors have 12 and nine response-level mentions.

### Weak first draft

> LedgerNest has 40% AI visibility and is already strong in ChatGPT. Write 20 SEO articles about accounting software and publish reviews on every big platform. Add FAQ schema so AI will recommend us. We should see higher rankings within two weeks.

### Why it fails

The overall 16/40 calculation blends brand-led answers with discovery. It conceals category errors and the limited test period. The content proposal amplifies the wrong category, the external plan ignores relevance, and the result promise has no evidence. Citation and mention are conflated.

### Improved audit result

**Decision:** Correct the category evidence before scaling content. The sampled non-branded mention rate is 6/30 = 20%, and the owned-content citation rate is 2/30 = 6.7%. The ten branded responses are a separate accuracy cohort. Visibility Share within the declared three-brand set is 6/(6 + 12 + 9) = 22.2%. These are package-default calculations for this sample, not population market share.

**Finding F1:** Two of six non-branded mentions misclassify the product. Evidence A12 and A25 preserve the relevant answers. The homepage and partner listing contain inconsistent category descriptions. This supports a plausible explanation, not proof that those pages caused the errors.

**Proposed content replacement, based on fictional approved product facts:**

> LedgerNest manages invoice approvals for German teams with 20 to 100 employees. Finance staff can route invoices to approvers and export approved records as CSV for accounting. LedgerNest does not replace accounting or payroll software. Teams requiring direct ERP posting should confirm integration support before selecting it.

**Prompt map:**

| Stage | Prompt | Evidence origin | Branded status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision | Can LedgerNest replace our accounting software? | Fictional sales objection | Brand-led accuracy |
| Consideration | Which invoice-approval tools suit a German company with 50 employees? | Fictional sales-call theme | Non-branded discovery |
| Awareness | How can a small finance team reduce delays in invoice approval? | Fictional support theme | Non-branded discovery |

**Action A1, P1:** Product marketing owns category and limitations updates on the homepage and product page within ten working days. Partner management requests a factual correction to the existing partner profile after the owned facts are approved. Acceptance: both controlled pages agree with the fact ledger; the partner response is recorded, including refusal or delay.

**Action A2, P1:** Content lead prepares one invoice-approval guide connected to the product page, answering the priority Consideration question with workflow examples and a dated capability table. No invented benchmark or superiority claim is added.

**Verification:** Repeat the unchanged 30-prompt cohort with the same surface and mode. Report mention, citation, category accuracy, and competitors separately. Publication completion and correct facts are controllable acceptance criteria; future AI selection is an observed outcome.

### Rules that changed the result

Intent-plus-context mapping and branded separation prevent false discovery confidence. [S3] Consistent category evidence and cross-team ownership address the actual problem. [S1] Source-worthy answers and qualified claims replace promotional copy. [S2][S4] The scoring and evidence conventions in method sections 4 and 8 make the results inspectable.

## Illustration 2: A regional attraction with limited live evidence

### Situation and fictional evidence

ForgeHouse is an interactive industrial-history attraction near Leipzig. It offers workshops for families with children aged eight and older, school visits on weekdays, and selected weekend sessions. Three publicly reviewed pages disagree: the website says workshop booking is required, a tourism listing implies walk-in participation, and an outdated profile describes an adults-only museum.

The team supplies one AI answer that calls ForgeHouse an adults-only attraction. The screenshot lacks a date, exact prompt, and visible model mode. The auditor has no live AI access. Public website and listing review is possible.

### Weak first draft

> ForgeHouse has zero visibility with families. GEO requires a new Groundingpage, Wikipedia entry, Reddit activity, and daily posts. Rewrite the website to say it is the best attraction for every age. After this, ChatGPT will send bookings.

### Why it fails

One incomplete answer cannot establish zero visibility. The claims about every age contradict the actual offer. Mandatory channels and a special page are unsupported, and bookings are promised without measurement. It skips the known factual conflicts.

### Improved audit result

**Audit type:** Desk audit with a proposed visibility baseline. No live visibility rate, citation rate, or competitive share was measured. The supplied answer is a lead for investigation, not a scored observation, because its prompt and settings are missing.

**Finding F1, P0:** The tourism listing conflicts with the approved booking policy. This can mislead prospective visitors regardless of AI behavior. Operations verifies the current policy; marketing updates controllable listings and requests corrections elsewhere.

**Finding F2, P1:** An old profile presents the attraction as adults-only. The documented offer includes families and schools. Whether this profile informed the supplied AI answer is unresolved.

**Proposed answer passage, based on fictional approved offer facts:**

> ForgeHouse offers hands-on industrial-history workshops near Leipzig for families with children aged eight and older. Workshop participation requires advance booking. School groups can request weekday sessions. Weekend dates vary, so visitors should check the current booking calendar before planning a visit.

The brief includes a maintained calendar link, an operations reviewer, and a review trigger when age limits, booking policy, or opening dates change. The passage does not invent prices or promise accessibility features.

**Proposed Top-Prompts:**

| Stage | Prompt | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | What hands-on industrial-history activities can families do near Leipzig? | Provisional, validate against visitor questions |
| Consideration | Which attractions near Leipzig offer workshops for children aged eight and older? | Provisional, validate against booking enquiries |
| Decision | Does ForgeHouse require advance booking for family workshops? | Brand-led policy accuracy |

**Action plan:** Operations owns factual verification within five working days. Marketing owns the website and tourism-profile updates in the following week. Partnerships asks the existing regional tourism network to use the corrected description. Content prepares a family-visits page that answers age suitability, booking, location, and session questions with verified facts.

**Monitoring proposal:** Once access is available, establish separate German regional discovery and branded accuracy cohorts, recording exact prompts, modes, dates, and sources. Ask consenting enquirers how they first heard about the attraction and record AI mentions alongside other answers. This is a proposed measurement change, not an already implemented form.

### Rules that changed the result

Technical, content, and external consistency work can proceed while visibility remains unmeasured. [S2][S5] Prompt-Mapping preserves realistic contexts and labels unvalidated ideas. [S3] Groundingpage is an optional identity example rather than a requirement. [S8] Ownership and lead-origin evidence connect the audit to maintainable work without promising bookings. [S1][S4]
