# Sources

All sources below were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02. Citations refer to the public page text or interview transcript, not linked books, recordings, paid lessons, or workbooks. Source numbering is local to this package.

1. **[S1] Matthew Dicks, Homework for Life.** https://matthewdicks.com/homework-for-life/
   Contributes the public description of brief daily capture, noticing small moments, and building an archive that also serves memory keeping.
2. **[S2] Matthew Dicks, My Homework for Life exercise will change your life. I promise. And you'll find a bunch of stories in the process.** https://matthewdicks.com/2015-01-minutes-through-exercise-html/
   Contributes the nightly review question, five-minute practice, fragment-length entries, ordinary-day persistence, and resurfacing memories.
3. **[S3] The Art of Manliness, Podcast #462: How to Tell Better Stories, interview with Matthew Dicks.** https://www.artofmanliness.com/social/social-skills/how-to-tell-better-stories/
   Contributes the change test, opposite-state opening, stakes and plans, concise length, and Dicks's distinction between omission and fabricated additions.
4. **[S4] Stacking Knowledge, Storyworthy, with Matthew Dicks, episode 043 interview transcript.** https://stackingknowledge.com/ep043/
   Contributes multiple daily entries, retrospective review, labelling older memories, starting close to the ending, spoken present tense, and economical location cues; only Dicks's conversational explanations are used, excluding the host's book quotations.
5. **[S5] Pfeiffer Law, Matthew Dicks: Homework for Life, interview transcript.** https://www.pfeifferlaw.com/entertainment-law-blog/matthew-dicks-homework-for-life
   Contributes compact dated capture, small internal shifts, construction from the ending, delayed background, and maintaining audience concern.
6. **[S6] Matthew Dicks, Storyworthy Moments, public course overview.** https://www.storyworthymd.com/storyworthy-moments
   Contributes intended applications for authentic story banks and memory keeping without stage experience; no paid course or workbook content was accessed or used.

## Scope of attribution

The originally cited Creativity Portal article was fetched but excluded because it identifies itself as an excerpt from *Storyworthy*. It contributes no claims to this package and has no source number. No book text is used. The method reference explicitly labels agent implementation choices. Marketing claims and personal reports about the benefits of memory capture are not treated as guaranteed outcomes or scientific evidence.
