Program: YC (Winter 2027). Company: Tidewater. Founders: Maya Chen, Leo Brandt.

Q: Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less.
A: An AI-powered maritime logistics intelligence platform

Q: What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.
A: Global shipping is a $14 trillion industry plagued by inefficiency, and ports are the bottleneck of the world economy. Tidewater is building the next-generation intelligence layer for maritime logistics, leveraging AI to unlock seamless operations. Our platform predicts berth delays for container terminals 72 hours ahead from AIS ship positions, weather and crane schedules, and automatically re-plans the berth schedule. Today terminal planners rebuild the plan by hand every shift.

Q: How far along are you?
A: We have over 1,200 users and are live at 2 terminals in Rotterdam. Our automatic re-planning engine is saving terminals millions. First paid pilot signed in October at EUR 4k/month.

Q: Why did you pick this idea to work on?
A: Although we are first-time founders and don't have a lot of enterprise sales experience, Maya ran berth planning at a terminal for 5 years and watched every plan break by noon.

Facts from the founder (when asked): 1,200 = everyone who ever created an account, including a 2023 student pilot; 14 planners use it every shift (daily actives). Automatic re-planning is NOT built yet, only the delay forecast is live. No measured savings yet. Leo Brandt was a backend engineer at a freight-tracking startup for 4 years.
