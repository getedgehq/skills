# Founder Brand Review Checklist

Mark each applicable check PASS or FAIL and cite the draft field that supports the judgment. Mark N/A only when the user's narrower scope excludes the item, with a reason. An unresolved factual or confidentiality failure prevents the affected copy from being labeled publishable. Other failures require revision or a clearly stated limitation. This review format is a package adaptation of the source method.

## Buyer and purpose

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A specific buyer, urgent priority, and buying context are identified; assumptions are visible. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The desired perception has a clear connection to a commercial action rather than general fame. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Audience engagement and qualified buyer interest are distinguished. [S3]

## Story and credibility

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A full plan includes customer priority, transformation, and personal journey, with themes and meaningful overlap. [S2][S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The customer story offers a useful prescription, the transformation explains navigation, and the journey supports relevant credibility. [S2][S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The founder's point of view is specific and earned; a competitor could not reuse every claim unchanged. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Contrarian claims explain their reasoning and avoid trolling or clickbait. [S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The founder recognizes the voice and confirms first-person experiences. [S3][S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Numbers and anecdotes are supported, private details are cleared or removed, and forecasts are labeled. Anonymization follows the playbook; explicit verification and forecast labeling are package safeguards. [S3]

## Content and distribution

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A full plan supplies two or three ideas per story type, with specific observations, buyer takeaways, formats, and dates. [S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Draft content has an engaging opening and delivers a useful payoff rather than empty curiosity. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Production fits the founder's natural medium, with a three-times-weekly target or an explicitly justified capacity adaptation. [S2][S3][S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: One primary channel is selected using buyer presence, format support, and publishing effort. [S2][S5]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The participation routine includes substantive contributions and timely relevant replies. [S2][S3]

## Revenue and learning

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Follow-up filters for buyer fit and distinguishes direct interest, social engagement, and weak account impressions. [S2]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The first touch comes from the founder, stays short and personal, and makes an appropriate concrete invitation. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A teammate's tracking or handoff role preserves the founder's initial contact. [S2][S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Qualified conversations and meetings are reviewed alongside reach; no conversion result is guaranteed. The measurement format is a package adaptation. [S3]
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Any publication or sending action is within the user's authorization. This is a package execution boundary.

## Common failures and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad founder fame objective | General attention does not identify a buyer response. | Name the buyer and desired meeting or purchase implication. |
| Generic technology slogan | No unique expertise or prescription is visible. | Add a real observation, bounded claim, and practical buyer consequence. |
| Autobiography without relevance | Personal detail does not explain value to the buyer. | Keep the incident that shaped useful expertise or company purpose. |
| Provocation without reasoning | Attention tactics displace a defensible view. | State the belief being questioned, evidence, and tradeoff. |
| Polished borrowed persona | The founder cannot sustain the voice. | Start with spoken notes and edit for clarity without imitating a creator. |
| Fabricated numbers or experience | Apparent specificity conceals unsupported claims. | Verify, remove, or recast as an explicit hypothesis outside factual claims. |
| Forced daily calendar | Frequency produces filler and exhaustion. | Simplify format, batch real material, and state any cadence adaptation. |
| Several channels from day one | Effort spreads before a habit forms. | Prioritize one channel where buyers actually participate. |
| Publishing without replying | Content lacks the participation loop. | Reserve time for relevant replies and useful comments elsewhere. |
| Every like becomes a lead | Engagement is mistaken for need and buyer fit. | Qualify the person and match the next step to the signal. |
| No follow-up or generic automated pitch | The revenue connection and founder credibility are lost. | Draft a timely personal founder note with a relevant invitation. |
| One quiet post triggers a new narrative | Too little evidence drives a large strategy change. | Inspect channel, cadence, and participation; gather more buyer responses. |
| Account impression becomes claimed individual readership | Weak exposure is overstated. | Use a normal topical outbound proposal without claiming who read it. |
| Missed date stops the entire practice | The plan becomes more important than momentum. | Reschedule a useful piece and continue the routine. |

The failure diagnoses apply the buyer, authenticity, distribution, and monetization rules in [S2][S3][S5]. Specific repair language and evidence thresholds are package adaptations.
