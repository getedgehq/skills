# Sources

All six sources below were fetched in full from their public URLs with curl on October 2, 2026. The PDF and DOCX were extracted locally for reading. No book, paywalled text, or linked third-party example was used. This package paraphrases the material without direct quotations. Slide page numbers refer to the PDF's displayed page numbers.

1. **[S1] Scale Venture Partners, Founder brand workshop.** https://www.scalevp.com/resources/founder-brand-workshop
   Public workshop overview confirming the four-part scope and connection between founder branding and GTM revenue.
2. **[S2] Scott Albro, The Founder Brand, workshop slides, November 15, 2024.** https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/692f28f1d17b8ec4f8005e36/6978d0f98d707fed606fb31a_Founder-brand-workshop.pdf
   Primary framework: story types, content practices, channel criteria, follow-up signals, founder first touch, cadence, and well-being guidance; the publicly linked deck retains a proprietary/confidential footer.
3. **[S3] Scale Venture Partners, Founder brand playbook, based on Scott Albro's presentation, June 22, 2025.** https://www.scalevp.com/resources/how-to-build-your-founder-brand
   Detailed prose explanation of buyer grounding, authenticity, market narratives, participation, and revenue follow-up, including explicit cautions.
4. **[S4] Scale Venture Partners, The 5 rules of founder-led GTM, March 24, 2025.** https://www.scalevp.com/resources/the-5-rules-of-founder-led-gtm-b5aa0
   Brief session landing page establishing broader founder-led GTM context; its fetched text does not enumerate five rules, so none are inferred here.
5. **[S5] Scott Albro, Founder Brand Template.** https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/692f28f1d17b8ec4f8005e36/6978d0f4c88f7791537bf7cf_Founder-brand-template.docx
   Primary planning sequence and fields: story, themes, differentiation, personality, distribution, content, format, and publication, plus overlap and momentum guidance.
6. **[S6] Scale Venture Partners, Founder brand template, May 30, 2025.** https://www.scalevp.com/resources/founder-brand-template
   Public resource page confirming the template's authorship and purpose for founder story, personality, and content execution.
