# Review checklist

Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL with evidence or a concrete repair. Mark N/A only with a reason. Missing critical inputs remain visible failures or dependencies; a plausible assumption is not verified evidence. An adapted test can pass as a labelled adaptation, but must not be described as the complete squad method.

## Method fidelity

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The deliverable credits Elisabeth Greger and does not imply endorsement or access to private Bloom procedures.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The configuration is explicit; full squad, adapted test and optional organic extension are distinguished.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: A full squad has at least five locally relevant creator slots, with selection evidence or stated recruiting gaps.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The brand workshop is one to four hours and covers positioning, goals and usable product knowledge.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: An onboarding kit includes products and company merchandise, with access or shipping dependencies recorded.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Weekly or monthly retainers specify output and sprint cadence; the scope does not imply unlimited work.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Half of deliverables originate in brand briefs and half in creator ideas, with an explicit count or balancing plan.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Brand briefs connect to available numbers, or clearly identify initial hypotheses where no numbers exist.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: There is one thirty-minute weekly meeting returning outcomes and exchanging angles, rather than only status.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Weekly and monthly bonus proposals have declared budgets and understandable criteria.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: The three-month review evaluates reliability and management readiness before recommending scale.

## Executability and evidence

- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Market, audience, objective, internal owner, budget and missing inputs are explicit.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Creator slots account for communication, editing and recurring availability as well as cultural fit.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Workshop comprehension is checked through a product demonstration or relevant creator pitch.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Briefs include an opening, three supported benefits, essential shots and production boundaries.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Creator concepts retain genuine creator ownership after factual review.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Filming, editing, finishing, delivery, revisions and acceptance responsibilities are assigned.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Usage and account ownership are stated as proposed or agreed; permissions are not invented.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Budget arithmetic includes coordination, shipping, finishing and bonuses where applicable.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Production costs are separated from media spend, and planned assets are distinguished from accepted assets.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Asset IDs, observation windows, outcomes and next production decisions form a traceable learning loop.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Organic spikes are labelled directional when causal attribution is unavailable.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Historical prices, performance anecdotes and platform capability statements are not presented as current guarantees.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Source-backed claims and package implementation guidance are distinguishable.
- [ ] PASS / FAIL: Drafting authorization has not been expanded into creator outreach, spending or account publication.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Five names without local selection logic | Headcount conceals fit and availability gaps | Specify market, language, product credibility and evidence for each slot |
| Logo PDF substituted for immersion | Brand knowledge is assumed rather than learned | Hold the workshop and check product understanding |
| Product shipment without belonging or clarity | The kit has become logistics only | Include merchandise, contacts, expectations and a discussion of goals |
| Fifty percent creator ideas rewritten into brand scripts | Origin labels conceal central control | Preserve the proposed story or reclassify and restore a creator slot |
| Older marketplace rules applied to the entire squad | Transactional procurement suppresses the newer collaborative lane | Use clear briefing in the brand lane and creator initiative in the other |
| Vague retainer with emergency finishing | Workload and handoff remain unowned | Specify output, revisions, editing and acceptance before the sprint |
| Views treated as purchases | Exposure is confused with business contribution | Return conversion measures or labelled directional business signals |
| Every coincident download assigned to one video | Promotions and other posts are ignored | Record confounders and run a comparable follow-up test |
| Two weekly calls plus constant live reviews | Coordination crowds out the simple cadence | Keep one thirty-minute meeting and move routine work asynchronously |
| Bonus rewards only the biggest audience | Exposure differences obscure reliability and learning | Declare understandable contribution criteria and budget |
| Free promotion into management | Management adds unplanned responsibilities | Propose paid duties and adjust production capacity |
| Automatic expansion after three months | Time substitutes for operational evidence | Review reliability, outcomes, cost and workload first |
| Dedicated accounts imposed on every squad | A separate distribution model is silently added | Make the organic extension an explicit choice with ownership |
| Quoted historical cost becomes a forecast | Different scopes and dates are collapsed | Use real quotes or labelled estimates and show included costs |
| Missing inputs replaced by invented results | The plan appears executable but cannot be trusted | Mark unknowns with an owner and next action |

## Release decision

Return the operating plan when its applicable checks pass. If a dependency prevents execution, deliver the usable plan with the failed item, consequence and next action clearly identified. Do not promise readiness while product access, production ownership or measurement remains unresolved. This review is part of the planning work and does not require a separate permission flow.
