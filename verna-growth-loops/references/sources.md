# Sources

All six sources below were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02. Their public article or post bodies were readable without a subscription or sign-in. Only those public bodies informed this package; linked courses, books, subscriber material, comments, and suggested posts were excluded. The package paraphrases the sources and contains no direct source quotations.

[S1] Elena Verna, **My 9 Favorite Growth Frameworks**, October 25, 2024. https://www.elenaverna.com/p/my-9-favorite-growth-frameworks
Contribution: Verna's loop diagnosis questions, Dropbox example, attribution of foundational frameworks, investment balance, adjacent audiences, growth sequencing, and warning about tactical over-testing.

[S2] Brian Balfour, Casey Winters, Kevin Kwok, and Andrew Chen, **Growth Loops are the New Funnels**, Reforge, July 31, 2018. https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops
Contribution: The framework recommended by Verna defines reinvestment and loop closure, connects product with distribution and monetization, discourages weak loop proliferation, and calls for quantitative modeling and shared outcomes.

[S3] Lenny Rachitsky and Dan Hockenmaier, **The Racecar Growth Framework**, Reforge, January 21, 2021. https://www.reforge.com/blog/racecar-growth-framework
Contribution: The extension recommended by Verna distinguishes engines, temporary boosts, lubricants, and fuel, with stage-specific investment errors, cash payback, and monetization or referral tradeoffs.

[S4] Elena Verna, **Six rules of hiring for growth**, free guest article in Lenny's Newsletter, November 9, 2021. https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/hiring-growth
Contribution: Product value versus distribution, levers and motions, data readiness, founder ownership of the first model, builder/optimizer/innovator profiles, and placement of growth responsibility.

[S5] Elena Verna, **Retention measurement mistakes**, public LinkedIn post, identified by its discussion of five retention mistakes. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/elenaverna_growth-retention-guide-activity-7110676438403440640-l1gd
Contribution: Meaningful activity, habitual frequency, retained free participants, activation before retention, and measurement of B2B value at account or team level.

[S6] Elena Verna, **11 hard truths learned in growth**, public LinkedIn post, identified by its eleven-item list. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/elenaverna_growth-activity-7370865006113992704-R2EQ
Contribution: Loop deterioration, product and retention investment, organizational constraints, resourcing failures, and caution about treating attribution as precise causal evidence.

The LinkedIn titles above are descriptive labels rather than formal article titles. Their URLs identify the exact fetched posts. Relative age labels were not converted into invented publication dates.
