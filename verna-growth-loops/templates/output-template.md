# Growth-model brief template

Replace bracketed fields with evidence or explicit assumptions. This template is a package adaptation of loop modeling, growth motions, retention measurement, and investment classification. Its thresholds and layout are not prescribed by Verna. [S1] [S2] [S3] [S4] [S5]

Retain only the relevant participant or paid-loop calculation. If evidence is incomplete, title the result a growth-model hypothesis and make the evidence request the next decision. Do not invent measurements to fill a field.

## 1. Decision

**Product and brief date:** [Name; analysis date; dataset cutoff.]

**Decision requested:** [One concrete allocation, design, or investigation decision.]

**Recommendation:** [Primary intervention and why the evidence supports it.]

**Confidence:** [Observed, inferred, or provisional; identify the decisive uncertainty.]

Guidance: Lead with the choice and its consequence. Avoid an unranked backlog.

**Retained-value definition:** [Value event plus recurrence window and eligibility boundary.]

## 3. Loop map

Repeat for the strongest one or two candidates.

**Loop name and status:** [Existing, hypothesized, or proposed; acquisition, engagement, content, or paid outcome.]

**Chain:** [Starting eligible input] -> [participant action] -> [output] -> [reinvestment mechanism] -> [next eligible input].

**Closing transition:** [Precisely how output becomes usable input.]

## 5. Constraint and investment

**Primary constraint:** [Evidence explaining what currently limits repeated output.]

**Selected intervention:** [Specific change, scope, expected mechanism, and customer tradeoff.]

**Racecar component:** [Engine/fuel/lubricant/temporary boost, with explanation.]

**Alternatives deferred:** [One or two alternatives and concrete reasons to wait.]

**Value guardrails:** [Meaningful activity, retention, conversion, or cash conditions the intervention must protect.]

Guidance: Match work to the stage and constraint. A temporary boost can seed a loop but does not establish an engine. [S3]

**Hypothesis and learning objective:** [Uncertain mechanism and what the next step must establish.]

**Method and scope:** [Cohort analysis, interviews, instrumentation, pilot, or experiment; why appropriate.]

**Success condition:** [Threshold or evidence criterion, with rationale.]

**Observation window:** [Intake and follow-up periods sufficient for activation, habit, or cash recovery.]

**Stop or revise condition:** [Evidence, cost, or value deterioration that ends or changes the work.]

**Next decision and review date:** [Who decides what after observing which evidence.]

Guidance: Resolve organizational dependencies and distinguish strategic learning from tactical testing. [S1] [S4] [S6]
