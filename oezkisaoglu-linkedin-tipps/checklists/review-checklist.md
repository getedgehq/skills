# Pass/fail review checklist

Credit: An original review instrument derived from Ilkay Özkisaoglu's public principles, with package-specific evidence and attribution safeguards.

Mark each applicable row PASS or FAIL. Mark optional format rows N/A with a short reason. A failed essential row requires revision or an explicit missing-input note before presenting a draft as ready. No score or reach prediction replaces judgment.

| Check | PASS condition | If FAIL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Delivers the requested post, newsletter, or plan without unrequested campaigns. | Remove extra work or identify it as an optional proposal. |
| Purpose | Names the professional audience and its practical problem. | Narrow the audience and ask what decision the contribution helps. |
| Expert territory | The author has a defensible connection to the topic. | Obtain relevant material or change the subject. |
| Konsistenz | Topic and perspective fit values, expertise, and corporate role. | Replace trend-led content with a relevant contribution. |
| Evidence | Consequential claims are supported and permission is clear. | Source, qualify, anonymize with approval, or remove the claim. |
| Authenticity | Experiences and credentials belong to the author; invented examples are labeled. | Replace manufactured stories with supplied or hypothetical material. |
| Usefulness | Readers receive a concrete insight or action. | Add a decision rule, practical step, or substantive qualification. |
| Tone | Language earns attention through substance without abusive provocation or unsupported superiority. | Replace outrage and adjectives with explanation. |
| Format | Depth and production demands fit audience needs and available capacity. | Select a manageable format or clarify needed resources. |
| Kontinuität | Rhythm is feasible and distinct from thematic consistency. | Reduce cadence and reserve reply capacity. |
| Newsletter, if used | A recurring promise and connected issue depth are clear. | Define subscriber value or keep the item as a post. |
| Employee roles, if used | People retain their own voice within professional boundaries. | Replace identical mandatory copy with evidence, training, and guardrails. |
| Participation | Comments and questions can advance relevant conversation; response ownership is clear. | Replace reaction bait and assign follow-up. |
| Measurement | Indicators reflect the goal and include data limits. | Add audience or opportunity evidence; avoid relying only on likes. |
| Platform claims | Feature-dependent advice is verified or explicitly historical. | Remove outdated mechanics or consult current official documentation. |
| Attribution | Public principles, case testimony, and adaptations are distinguished. | Correct the source or relabel the rule as an implementation choice. |
| Authorization | Drafting has not been treated as permission to publish or contact people. | Return the draft and state its actual status. |

## Common failure modes and fixes

- **Generic corporate announcement:** It names products and achievements without helping a reader. Fix by extracting one approved lesson and a practical application.
- **AI-polished but empty:** It sounds fluent while offering no author-specific knowledge. Fix by interviewing the expert, supplying evidence, and retaining their judgment.
- **Daily publishing imposed by the series name:** Quantity overwhelms substance and replies. Fix by agreeing on a feasible rhythm; daily idea collection can remain internal.
- **Konsistenz confused with Kontinuität:** A calendar is mistaken for positioning. Fix by checking topic alignment and publishing rhythm separately.
- **Video prescribed to everyone:** Production blocks participation. Fix by using the author's preferred format and testing whether video serves the audience.
- **Newsletter as a sales brochure:** Subscription has no recurring educational value. Fix by defining reader benefit and explaining a connected problem in depth.
- **Vanity metric victory:** Likes are presented as qualified demand. Fix by checking target-audience attention and goal-aligned conversations without overstating attribution.
- **Silent-reader fantasy:** Low engagement is treated as proof that CEOs are secretly convinced. Fix by seeking other evidence and acknowledging what is unknown.
- **Copy-and-paste employee advocacy:** Staff become duplicate distribution channels. Fix by supporting voluntary original contributions within factual and confidentiality boundaries.
- **Historical platform trick presented as current:** Old hashtag-follow, Creator Mode, or search-ranking advice is hard-coded. Fix by keeping the durable principle and verifying any feature needed for execution.
- **Provocation used as a positioning shortcut:** Attention comes from unrelated conflict. Fix by tying the viewpoint to professional purpose, evidence, and civil discussion.
- **Attribution inflation:** A guest's comment, marketing multiplier, or package template is called the author's proven method. Fix by labeling the speaker and evidence type; remove unsupported guarantees.
