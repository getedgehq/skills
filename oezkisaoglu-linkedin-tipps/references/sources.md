## Retrieval limits and attribution boundaries

The OMT HTML article was attempted with curl but returned HTTP 520, so it is not a numbered source. S10 is the accessible PDF version. S4 and S11 contain sales claims and changing counters; these are not independent proof or performance targets. Comments by other LinkedIn users were not treated as Özkisaoglu's instructions. Client statements inside S8 are identified as client testimony. Its sensXPERT account describes a launch, not demonstrated ROI.

S3 discusses LinkedIn engineering publications. Those technical publications were not fetched for this package. Its algorithm statements remain attributed interpretations, not independently verified specifications. Older hashtag, Creator Mode, notification, search-indexing, and Live eligibility claims should not be presented as current guarantees.
