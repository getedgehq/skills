# Ugly Ads creative brief

Fill bracketed fields with verified facts, observed evidence, or explicitly labeled assumptions. Delete drafting guidance from the delivered brief once it has been followed. This structure is a package application aid; it is not a form published by Hott.

## 1. Assignment

- **Brand and product:** [Name and plain description.] Explain the product without relying on brand familiarity.
- **Requested work:** [New concepts, revision, or diagnosis.] Identify the existing asset if relevant.
- **Audience in a situation:** [Person, context, need.] Include the viewing moment, not only demographics. [S4][S8]
- **Placement and objective:** [Platform, placement, conversion or awareness goal.] Specify the viewing context that informs style. [S3]
- **Offer and desired action:** [Verified offer; one next step; destination.] Confirm that the destination supports the promise.
- **Verified product capabilities:** [Facts and supporting material.] Distinguish capabilities from assumed customer results.
- **Constraints:** [Production resources, duration, speakers, brand requirements, deadline.] Explain which constraints affect the concept.
- **Unknowns and assumptions:** [Missing facts; provisional choices; how to resolve them.] Never fill evidence gaps with invented observations.

## 3. Concept card

Repeat this card for each distinct concept. Select the number of concepts according to the assignment and production capacity.

- **Concept identifier:** [Short title and version.] Make it easy to track review and results.
- **Human premise:** [One recognizable frustration, desire, or win.] This is the reason to care, separate from format. [S4]
- **Audience connection:** [Why this particular person recognizes the situation immediately.] [S8]
- **Native vessel:** [Phone recording, note, founder account, demonstration, other.] Explain the evidence supporting this choice. [S1][S2]
- **Speaker and provenance:** [Founder, actual customer, actor, demonstrator, synthetic spokesperson.] State whether first-person experience is genuine or dramatized.
- **Product contribution and benefit:** [What the product does here; what changes.] Use a verified capability and avoid unsupported results.
- **Credibility and claim support:** [Sources, real demonstration, customer approval, limitations.] Flag unresolved claims for revision.

**Production notes:** [Setting, props, camera, audio, caption legibility, quality cues, required permissions or materials.] Preserve useful human detail while keeping comprehension clear. [S1][S2]

**Alternate opening or angle:** [Changed beat, changed words, changed shot, and hypothesis.] State whether this is a new premise or another entry into the same story.

**Why it should fit the feed:** [Reference-linked reasoning.] Phrase the expected response as a hypothesis, not a promise.

## 4. Review record

- **Phone-size context check:** [Placement preview or organic reference comparison; reviewer; date.]
- **Cold-review response:** [What the reviewer thought was happening, who it concerned, and what the product changed.] Record their initial understanding before explaining.
- **Checklist outcome:** [Pass/fail by item in checklists/review-checklist.md.]
- **Revisions made:** [Failure, correction, and remaining gap.]
- **Readiness:** [Ready for production, revise, or provisional.] Separate creative readiness from performance validation.

## 5. Proposed test and learning plan

- **Authorization status:** [Proposal only, or scope of existing live-test authorization.] A creative brief does not authorize account spending.
- **Benchmark:** [Best existing ad and relevant historical outcome.] Compare within the same environment. [S6]
- **Comparison conditions:** [Audience and exclusions, placements, objective, attribution, destination, offer, delivery period, budget approach.] Document material differences.
- **Delivery opportunity:** [How concepts can receive useful exposure; limitations.] Identify bid restrictions or allocation problems. [S6]
- **Primary outcome:** [Business metric suited to the objective; acceptable range and rationale.]
- **Diagnostic signals:** [Shares, cost per share, and other relevant available evidence.] Shares indicate possible distribution value, not proven sales. [S5]
- **Evidence goal and stopping limits:** [Budget ceiling, duration, expected signal needs, review owner.] Base them on economics and normal volume. [S6]
- **Decision table:** [What would justify continuing, revising, stopping, or investigating insufficient evidence.]
- **Next hypothesis:** [One meaningful creative change and what it would teach.]

## 6. Delivery note

[Summarize the selected concepts, unresolved evidence, review outcome, and next decision. If no test has run, state that performance is unvalidated. If results exist, distinguish observed outcomes from explanations that remain hypotheses.]
