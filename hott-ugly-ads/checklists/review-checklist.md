# Finished-output review

Use this checklist on the complete brief and each proposed asset. These are package review gates derived from the cited method, not a published scoring system from Hott.

Mark every item **PASS** or **FAIL**, with a short evidence note. A missing fact is a failure when it affects production or truthfulness; label the brief provisional until it is resolved. An unrun test is acceptable when the requested deliverable is a proposal and that status is explicit.

## Pass/fail gates

- [ ] **Audience and moment:** A specific person and recognizable situation are stated; a demographic label alone does not pass. [S4][S8]
- [ ] **Immediate relevance:** The opening gives a cold viewer a reason to care without prior brand knowledge. [S8]
- [ ] **Feed evidence:** Style choices link to observed references, or missing research is explicitly labeled and the concept marked provisional. [S7]
- [ ] **Placement fit:** Production choices match the intended viewing context; awareness or premium pricing alone does not justify the style. [S1][S3]
- [ ] **Premise before vessel:** The concept explains a human experience independently of whether it uses a note, phone recording, or other rough format. [S1][S4]
- [ ] **Meaningful message:** A reviewer can describe what the product contributes and why that contribution matters. [S1][S4]
- [ ] **Useful imperfection:** Natural detail supports credibility; roughness does not obstruct reading, listening, or understanding. [S1][S2]
- [ ] **Truthful provenance:** Speaker roles, real experiences, dramatizations, and synthetic material are accurately described; no invented customer testimony is presented as real. [S2]
- [ ] **Supported claims:** Capabilities and offers have evidence; fabricated statistics from the Motion demonstration are excluded. [S2]
- [ ] **Clear action:** The requested next step is explicit, appropriate to the objective, and supported by the destination. [S1]
- [ ] **Executable brief:** Shots, draft words, product reveal, and production constraints are specified sufficiently for production.
- [ ] **Meaningful alternative:** The alternate opening or angle names its changed human reason to care or its learning hypothesis.
- [ ] **Context review:** Phone-size viewing and cold-review understanding are recorded, or clearly identified as pending.
- [ ] **Comparable test proposal:** Benchmark, material settings, delivery opportunity, evidence needs, budget limits, and outcome criteria are documented when testing is part of the assignment. [S6]
- [ ] **Balanced interpretation:** Acquisition results and objective-appropriate evidence lead decisions; shares can trigger investigation but cannot alone prove profitability. [S3][S5][S6]
- [ ] **Honest status:** Untested performance is labeled unvalidated, and the brief does not imply permission to publish or spend.

## Decision

**Ready:** Production-critical gates pass and remaining optional uncertainties are documented. This means the brief is executable, not that the ad is a proven winner.

**Revise:** The premise, product connection, claim support, action, or execution fails a gate. Name the failed item and repair it before recommending production.

**Provisional:** Research, product facts, or required review remain missing. Supply a usable draft and the exact evidence needed to finish it. Do not invent a passing result.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure mode | Concrete fix |
|---|---|
| Deliberately ugly layout with no relevant premise | Write the recognizable moment first, then choose its vessel. [S1][S4] |
| Interface tour before any reason to care | Open with the person's situation and show only the product detail that explains the change. [S4] |
| Generic polished campaign imported into a feed | Rebrief around observed content grammar for this placement. [S3][S7] |
| Fake spontaneity or invented customer confession | Use a real supported account, or a clearly described dramatization with truthful product facts. [S2] |
| AI ideas based on assumed audience tastes | Supply current feed observations and verified product context before requesting variants. [S7] |
| A rough ad is unreadable or confusing | Repair captions, sound, framing, or the causal link while retaining useful human detail. [S1] |
| Executive taste overrides audience evidence | Show the relevant references and explain the concept's audience connection. [S3][S7] |
| Only a headline changes between supposedly distinct concepts | Change the underlying situation or specify that it is an opening test within one concept. |
| Heavy sharing is ignored because reported CPA is poor | Investigate possible distribution and attribution effects within the budget limit; do not assume success. [S5] |
| Heavy sharing is treated as proof of sales | Return to business outcomes and record the attribution hypothesis separately. [S5][S6] |
| Low delivery is called creative failure | Check restrictive bidding, allocation, and comparability before drawing a creative conclusion. [S6] |
| Universal spend or click threshold determines the verdict | Set evidence expectations from price, conversion volume, account context, and financial tolerance. [S6] |
| Rapid iteration becomes a flood of unrelated edits | Name the hypothesis and change a meaningful variable that can inform the next decision. [S2] |
