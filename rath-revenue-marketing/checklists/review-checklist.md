# Review checklist

Apply this checklist to the completed [output template](../templates/output-template.md). For each item record Pass or Fail and point to evidence in the plan. A blank field is a failure. An explicit unknown can pass transparency checks but cannot pass a readiness gate that requires that fact. Mark a conditionally irrelevant item Pass with the reason, rather than silently omitting it.

## Pass/fail gates

- [ ] **Attribution of the method:** Credits Tim Rath and distinguishes his post from YOYABA implementation material and this package's synthesis.
- [ ] **Evidence integrity:** Uses fetched public sources accurately; does not claim access to unfetched audio, newsletters, talks, or private material.
- [ ] **Commercial assignment:** Names the business motion, ICP, target, period, available capacity, and decision owner.
- [ ] **Fact discipline:** Separates inspected evidence, stakeholder statements, assumptions, and proposed hypotheses.
- [ ] **Shared metrics:** Defines relevant pipeline, revenue, conversion, cost scope, cohort windows, and economic guardrails.
- [ ] **Capability coverage:** Assesses RevOps, Organic Growth, and Paid Growth, with reasons for priority and use of internal strengths.
- [ ] **Buyer-stage distinction:** Covers creation, capture, and conversion without forcing each into one exclusive capability.
- [ ] **Constraint diagnosis:** Identifies a supported bottleneck and alternative explanations; does not mistake missing tracking for zero effect.
- [ ] **RevOps readiness:** Specifies data repairs, lifecycle definitions, source capture, identity links, handoff ownership, and readiness evidence where needed.
- [ ] **Organic usefulness:** Content addresses buyer pains or objections, has a discovery plan, and supports a commercial next step.
- [ ] **Paid fit:** Channels and offers match buyer readiness, business motion, value, and unit economics; existing valuable campaigns are preserved unless evidence supports change.
- [ ] **Integration:** Traces at least one insight through content, paid distribution, destination, CRM or activation, follow-up, and a commercial outcome.
- [ ] **Measurement honesty:** Keeps recorded and self-reported sources distinct, retains unknowns, labels overlapping influence, and avoids causal overclaims.
- [ ] **PLG adaptation:** If relevant, defines activation and tracks usage, paid conversion, and retention rather than stopping at signups.
- [ ] **Execution readiness:** Each proposed action has an owner, dependency, date, capacity requirement, and budget or effort limit.
- [ ] **Experiment decisions:** Defines observation windows and continue, change, stop, and scale conditions, including insufficient evidence.
- [ ] **Realistic expectations:** Does not promise published case-study uplift, fixed time to revenue, or universal platform thresholds.
- [ ] **Scope:** Planning instructions do not authorize unrelated system changes, external outreach, or spending.

## Review outcome

**Pass:** All applicable quality gates pass, and prerequisites for proposed execution are met.

**Revise:** Unsupported claims, incompatible metrics, broken integration, or undefined decisions remain. Repair them before presenting the deliverable as ready.

**Provisional:** The reasoning and uncertainty disclosure are complete, but necessary client data or prerequisites remain unavailable. Deliver the useful diagnosis with named dependencies and spending limits; do not label dependent scaling ready.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure mode | Why it fails | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three separate channel plans | Handoffs and dependencies remain implicit | Trace one shared buyer insight through all three capabilities |
| Equal budget split by default | Ignores the limiting constraint and internal strengths | Allocate effort according to evidence, prerequisites, and economics |
| CPL or CTR becomes the success verdict | Activity can improve while customer value worsens | Pair diagnostics with mature commercial progression |
| Pipeline target counts every lead | Quantity obscures qualification and sales ownership | Define accepted opportunity criteria and amount basis |
| More ads before fixing routing | Amplifies operational leakage | Repair assignment and follow-up, then verify sampled journeys |
| Dashboard before data repair | Visualizes inconsistent inputs | Reconcile stages, identities, amounts, sources, and timestamps |
| General content quota | Produces assets without a buyer purpose | Brief pain, objection, discovery path, and next step |
| Expensive platform chosen by convention | Channel cost may exceed customer economics | Check ICP reach, contract value, conversion, margin, and payback |
| Every channel launched together | Dilutes expertise and obscures what changed | Establish a viable motion and test one justified expansion |
| Signup count treated as PLG growth | Users may never reach value or become customers | Add activation, retained usage, and paid conversion cohorts |
| Last click receives all demand credit | Observable conversion source hides earlier discovery | Pair technical sources, self-reporting, and aggregate economics |
| Self-reporting treated as complete truth | Missing responses and recall affect reliability | Preserve unknowns and triangulate evidence |
| Sourced and influenced pipeline added | Same opportunity can appear multiple times | Use a deduplicated total and label overlapping influence separately |
| Small or immature cohorts called a failure | Buying-cycle lag can mislead decisions | State maturity, cap spend, and set an appropriate decision window |
| Public results reused as guaranteed targets | Agency cases are context-specific reported outcomes | Use client baselines and explicit planning assumptions |
| Synthesis presented as a Rath command | Overstates the source evidence | Label package conventions and cite only actual public support |
