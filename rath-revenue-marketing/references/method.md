# The integrated Revenue Marketing method

Credit: Based on Tim Rath's public Revenue Marketing method and YOYABA's public implementation material. Independent synthesis; not affiliated with or endorsed by Tim Rath.

## Evidence boundary

This document separates **published principles** from **operational synthesis**. Citations identify public support. Proposed metric contracts, prioritization gates, experiment fields, and review cadence are this package's implementation guidance, not a proprietary Rath playbook. See [sources.md](sources.md) for authorship and fetch limits. Examples elsewhere are invented.

## 1. Governing principle: one revenue system

Rath's starting post supplies the formula: Revenue Marketing = Revenue Operations + Organic Growth + Paid Growth. Its argument is that operations enable paid scaling, organic supports acquisition economics, and paid provides repeatable distribution. It also describes complementing internal strengths rather than rebuilding every function. These are strategic claims, not mathematical identities or universal guarantees. [S1]

The current agency About page gives paid a central role while retaining dedicated organic and RevOps capabilities. It describes beginning with CRM and advertising analysis and accepting responsibility beyond lead delivery. Reconcile the pages as an integrated approach whose positioning has evolved, not an immutable equal-weight channel allocation. [S2]

**Operational synthesis:** Diagnose the entire system, then act on its strongest constraint. All three disciplines must be considered; they need not receive equal spend or launch simultaneously. A single accountable revenue owner coordinates specialist work. Existing teams can deliver any component.

## 2. Commercial accountability and buyer journey

YOYABA organizes the journey into Demand Creation, Demand Capture, and Demand Conversion. Creation builds awareness and understanding; capture serves buyers evaluating a solution; conversion connects qualified interest to purchase through sales and marketing cooperation. It names pipeline, close rates, CAC, LTV, and closed business among useful measures. [S3]

The Revenue Marketing Realtalk page reinforces shared sales and marketing accountability and education distributed through relevant content and channels. Retain this German program name when referring to the podcast. The fetched material does not establish a separate German-named methodology requiring translation. [S11]

**Operational synthesis:** Keep the two three-part models distinct. RevOps, Organic Growth, and Paid Growth are capabilities. Creation, capture, and conversion are buyer tasks. Paid can create demand; organic can capture it; RevOps supports every stage. Do not map one capability exclusively to one buyer stage.

### Metric contract

Write definitions before analysis:

| Measure | Required definition | Misinterpretation to prevent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified pipeline | Accepted opportunity criteria, amount basis, creation date, exclusions | Treating every inquiry as pipeline |
| Closed revenue | Booked contract value, ARR, or recognized revenue; currency and period | Combining different revenue bases |
| Conversion | Numerator, denominator, eligibility, cohort, observation window | Comparing immature and mature cohorts |
| CAC | Included acquisition costs divided by new customers from a compatible cohort | Presenting media-only cost as fully loaded CAC |
| Payback | Acquisition cost divided by monthly gross profit per acquired customer | Ignoring margin or using annual revenue as monthly profit |
| Retention | Customer or revenue retention, period, expansion treatment | Calling expansion and logo retention the same thing |

These definitions and formulas are planning conventions supplied by this package. Select the relevant measures, not every possible KPI. Revenue is a lagging result; pipeline quality, activation, and buyer progress help explain whether the system is improving. CTR, clicks, rankings, impressions, and CPL remain diagnostics, not sufficient evidence of commercial success.

If useful, calculate planning scenarios: target new revenue divided by average new contract value gives required wins; wins divided by historical win rate gives required qualified opportunities. Use compatible periods and mature cohorts. Label the result a scenario, not a forecast or Rath's formula. Where historic rates are unreliable, show a range and the missing evidence.

## 3. Revenue Operations: make progress observable and executable

YOYABA's RevOps scope includes processes, people, platforms, data quality, marketing-sales throughput, CRM setup, attribution, automation, and revenue analytics. It extends into customer success and recurring revenue. Its attribution FAQ combines technical source capture with self-reporting and adapts the approach to the GTM motion. [S4]

Clockodo's published case describes duplicate and synchronization repairs, an app-to-CRM connection, improved lifecycle representation, and hybrid attribution. It illustrates foundational work that improves decisions rather than a requirement to increase ad spend. [S8]

**Operational synthesis:** Inspect the following before committing to scale:

- Can a buyer be traced from inquiry or signup through qualification, purchase, and renewal?
- Are contact, account, product, deal, and billing records linked consistently?
- Do stage definitions reflect actual buyer progress, with an accountable owner at each transition?
- Are source fields, campaign identifiers, amounts, timestamps, and loss reasons sufficiently complete?
- Does sales receive the context promised by marketing, and does feedback return to campaign owners?
- Are stale records, duplicate opportunities, and inconsistent currencies distorting reporting?

Recommend the smallest repair that restores trustworthy decisions. A dashboard cannot correct absent or inconsistent inputs. Automation should implement agreed processes and ownership; it should not accelerate confusion. If direct access is unavailable, request an export and document its age, coverage, and limitations. Deliver a provisional diagnostic rather than claiming verified revenue performance.

## 4. Organic Growth: build useful discovery and trust

The organic service page connects content, site structure, authority, technical accessibility, and buyer journey improvements across conventional search, AI discovery, and social. It warns about random tactics, content without commercial function, absent evaluation pages, and insufficient execution capacity. [S5]

The content service page focuses on ICP pain, motivation, objections, education, long purchase cycles, and proof. Blogs, landing pages, and case studies serve different buyer needs rather than forming a generic publishing quota. [S7]

**Operational synthesis:** Build a compact content map:

| Buyer task | Asset choice | Commercial connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognize the problem | Expert explanation, practical diagnostic, educational video | Relevant awareness and problem understanding |
| Evaluate approaches | Use-case page, comparison, implementation guide | Qualified consideration and next-step intent |
| Reduce purchase risk | Customer evidence, objection response, product walkthrough | Sales enablement and purchase confidence |

Choose topics from buyer interviews, sales questions, existing customer language, and search evidence. Repair discovery and conversion barriers where they prevent valuable content from working. Distribution belongs in the brief: where it is published, how it reaches the ICP, what paid can amplify, and what sales can reuse.

Do not interpret low category search volume as proof of absent buyer pain. Buyers may search around the problem or need education before searching for a solution. Avoid claiming that every content asset creates trackable revenue independently. Exposure, search visibility, and engagement require a plausible connection to commercial progress.

## 5. Paid Growth: create and capture with economic discipline

YOYABA describes paid as both demand creation and capture, with channel mix, messaging, creative, and targeting adapted to growth stage, GTM motion, and unit economics. Its paid social approach combines direct response, account-based tactics, and content consumed within the feed. [S6]

Workist's case describes research with sales, message and audience testing, education for an unfamiliar category, website improvements, and both direct requests and indirectly sourced inbound interest. It explicitly cautions that the inbound outcome cannot be attributed solely to paid as organic grew. [S9]

**Operational synthesis:** Choose the role before the platform. An educational campaign may aim to make the problem and solution understandable; a capture campaign may serve active evaluation. Match the promise and destination. A ready buyer needs an easy next step; an unaware buyer may need an explanation before a demo request.

Test meaningful hypotheses about pain, proof, offer, audience, and buying context. Cosmetic variants are appropriate only when the main message already works. Feed sales objections and organic insights into creative briefs; feed paid response patterns back into content research.

Each test needs an owner, budget ceiling, audience, offer, prerequisite, decision date, observation window, leading signal, commercial outcome, and stop rule. Values are client-specific. This package prescribes no universal split, platform, CPL threshold, or minimum spend.

## 6. Integration and attribution

**Operational synthesis:** Trace a complete chain: buyer pain -> expert content -> paid amplification -> relevant destination -> inquiry or activation -> CRM record -> accountable follow-up -> opportunity or customer -> commercial review. If a link fails, identify the repair and its owner instead of compensating automatically with more traffic.

Retain software-recorded and self-reported sources separately. They answer different questions and can disagree legitimately. Use one governed classification for reporting, preserve original responses, and retain an unknown category. Do not force missing attribution into a winning channel. Attribute each opportunity once in an additive total; assisted or influenced views may overlap and must be labeled accordingly.

Technical attribution shows observable paths, not causal proof. Self-reporting is incomplete and subject to recall. Compare both with buyer research and aggregate economics. When making an incremental claim, describe the counterfactual evidence needed, such as an appropriate controlled comparison. Avoid presenting all revenue credited to search as revenue created by search.

### PLG extension

Dennis Krcek's public YOYABA article warns about isolating channels through last-click reporting, diversifying too widely, and counting signups that never become active users. It recommends economic channel fit, activation measures, overall efficiency, and establishing a viable channel before expansion. This is a colleague's practical extension, not evidence of a universal Rath platform rule. [S10]

**Operational synthesis:** For PLG, connect signup to activation, meaningful usage, paid conversion, and retention. A sales-led funnel alone cannot diagnose self-serve growth. If activation is weak across acquisition sources, investigate product and onboarding before scaling traffic. Evaluate expensive platforms against contract value and conversion economics rather than B2B convention.

## 7. Prioritization and edge cases

The following decision rules are operational synthesis informed by the principles above, not verbatim public instructions.

| Observed condition | First decision | Evidence needed before expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large unattributed share or inconsistent stages | Repair measurement and ownership | Sample journeys reconcile with CRM and commercial totals |
| Good leads but stalled qualification | Examine fit, routing, response, and promises | Stage progression improves without inflated qualification |
| Healthy conversion but little suitable demand | Increase education and ICP distribution | Relevant buyer progress and sustainable acquisition economics |
| Useful attention but weak inquiries | Repair offer, proof, evaluation pages, or destination | Qualified conversion improves, not just form fills |
| Paid produces customers at worsening cost | Find waste and strengthen organic and creative support | Comparable cohort economics support continued spend |
| Signup growth without activation | Diagnose onboarding and expectation mismatch | Meaningful usage and paid conversion recover |
| Low data volume or long sales cycle | Use bounded tests and staged evidence | Mature cohorts or sufficient evidence to update the hypothesis |
| Capable internal organic or RevOps team | Integrate ownership and fill the missing capability | One plan, shared measures, clear handoffs |
| Acquisition works but retention deteriorates | Include product, CS, and renewal friction | Cohort retention and customer economics stabilize |

Unknown metrics are not zero. Small samples justify cautious decisions and spending bounds, not fabricated certainty. Strong performance in one market does not prove transferability to another. Plan localization and test the new buyer context.

Public client outcomes demonstrate possible applications, not expected uplift for a new client. Avoid borrowing their numerical results as targets. Published timelines likewise depend on foundations and sales cycle. Set client-specific review dates with milestones for data readiness, buyer response, opportunity progression, and eventual revenue.

## 8. Deliverable and review rhythm

**Operational synthesis:** Return the filled [output template](../templates/output-template.md). Lead with the commercial gap and limiting constraint. Include existing strengths, one integrated initiative, prerequisite repairs, a bounded experiment, measurement limits, owners, and decision dates.

At a regular cadence agreed with the team, reconcile the revenue scorecard, inspect stalled journeys, review experiment evidence, and decide whether to continue, modify, stop, or scale. Revisit buyer insights and capacity as conditions change. The output should make the next decision possible, even when evidence remains incomplete.
