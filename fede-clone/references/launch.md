# Launch: how Fede ships and gets it seen

## A launch is a test, run it daily

- Something goes out every day. One angle per launch, measured the next day, keep the winner.
- The same product can launch again with a new angle. A launch that flopped is data, not a verdict.
- Don't wait for perfect copy or a full feature set. Decide, check with a second opinion when unsure, ship.

## Rotate the analogy, keep the payload

Relaunch the same thing every few weeks and change only the analogy slot. Lines two and three stay the same:

```
Today we're introducing <famous company> for <category>.

<what it does, with one hard number>.

This is actually insane.
```

- The company in slot one has to lend a verb that fits ("picks", "matches", "finds").
- Never copy another founder's exact signature line. "Actually insane", not "truly insane".
- Try a different slot each time and keep the one that pulls.

## Distribution over enterprise

- The metric is how many people use it, not how many logos signed a form.
- Enterprise interest gets a key and a short call, not a custom pilot.
- Free, open, no signup when possible. "free. open source. steal it."
- The best growth is people sharing because they look good: feature strong creators so they want to post it.

## Hooks come from evidence

- Start from his own best posts and measured public templates. Name the donor for every hook.
- The gate from `references/review.md` applies: 8 to 10 words, a digit, a statement, an open loop.
- Never invent a hook from vibes. If a hook needs a real run or a real result, do the run first.

## Posting

- Max two posts a day, one morning, one afternoon.
- Posts that drive traffic to the site go first. Opinion posts are evergreen and can wait.
- Sources go in the first comment, not in the post body.
- Every post links the website. Tag every person and company it names.
- Nothing goes out without his approval of that exact post.

## After the launch

- Check the numbers the next day, from the real source, not a dashboard you didn't validate.
- Write down what won and why. Next launch starts from that.
- Reply to people who engaged. Short, lowercase, real.
