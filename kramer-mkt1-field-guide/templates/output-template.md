# Marketing decision package

This is a fill-in implementation aid based on the public method, not an official MKT1 template. Delete bracketed prompts when completing it. For one campaign, use sections 1, 2, 5, and 8, with concise strategic context; add the others only where relevant. For an annual or quarterly plan, use all applicable sections. Explain omitted sections instead of fabricating inputs.

## 1. Decision and evidence

**Company and deliverable:** [Company; annual plan, quarterly portfolio, campaign, roadmap, or team assessment.]

**Planning period:** [Start, end, and relevant revenue window.]

**Recommended decision:** [State the work to commit to, expected benefit, and main tradeoff.]

**Constraints:** [Available people, production capacity, budget ceiling, tools, and dependencies.]

| Input | Evidence or source | Confidence | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and market | [Customer research or supplied record] | [Observed, inferred, unknown] | [What it changes] |
| Business model and GTM | [Contract value, buying process, sales cycle] | [Confidence] | [Channel and lifecycle consequences] |
| Current performance | [Baseline with period and definition] | [Confidence] | [Constraint or opportunity] |

**Evidence gaps:** [List only gaps that affect the decision; name the research owner and next action.]

Guidance: these are the three-driver inputs and constraints, not an invitation to list fashionable tactics. Startup facts require startup evidence; framework citations do not prove them. [S1]

**Positioning:** [For whom; what the product is; meaningful difference from the selected alternative.]

**Perceptions:** [Three to five beliefs written as the audience would say them; cover company and product story.]

| Rank | Revenue lever | Reason now | Effort allocation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Guidance: include all four levers and distinguish Perceptions from content topics. Reuse this dashboard's terms in each initiative row and brief. [S1] [S2] [S5]

## 5. GACCS campaign brief

**Campaign and horizon:** [Name, start/end dates, and decision this campaign supports.]

**Goals:** [Primary outcome, leading signals, measurement source, and link to the selected lever.]

**Audience:** [Specific company/person ICP, lifecycle readiness, problem, and exclusion criteria.]

**Creative:** [Useful idea or anchor asset, evidence of audience need, differentiated contribution, and Perception it reinforces.]

**Channels:** [Primary and supporting engines, distribution owner, reach assumptions, sequence, and consumption-to-next-action journey.]

**Stakeholders:** [Single DRI, contributors, reviewers, handoff owners, and review deadlines.]

**Readiness gates:** [Assets, routing, measurement, partner confirmation, or other prerequisites before launch.]

Guidance: write this before production and review against it. Creative is fuel; Channels is engine. Stakeholders comes from the newer five-part version. [S1] [S6] [S7]

## 7. Resources and forecast

**Capability gaps:** [Foundation, fuel, engine, or coordination gap; cover with existing team, contractor, or hire; state why.]

**Spend and capacity:** [Headcount, programs, tools, ongoing commitments, and production/distribution effort.]

**Bottom-up model:** [Reachable demand x stage conversions x customer value, with timing and consistent units; adapt for the motion.]

**Assumption register:** [Historical value, proposed change, evidence, confidence, and validation plan.]

**Target reconciliation:** [Top-down target, bottom-up result, shortfall or surplus, and the real activity/resource changes required.]

Guidance: use efficiency metrics suited to the company. Do not double-count accounts across engines or confuse pipeline creation with booked revenue. Label unsupported lifts as hypotheses. [S1] [S2]

## 8. Review and learning

**Review date and owner:** [Monthly check for quarterly campaigns; other cadence justified by the task.]

**Scale evidence:** [Result and capacity conditions supporting greater investment.]

**Revise or stop evidence:** [Observed constraint, failed hypothesis, or strategic change requiring a decision.]

**Open decisions:** [Decision, accountable person, date needed, and consequence of delay.]

**Checklist result:** [Pass, fail, or not applicable with reason; link unresolved failures to a concrete action.]

Guidance: use [../checklists/review-checklist.md](../checklists/review-checklist.md). Thresholds are planning choices, not prescribed MKT1 benchmarks. Reuse successful campaigns and replace lower-priority commitments when opportunities change. [S8]
