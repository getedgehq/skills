# Review checklist

Review the completed deliverable against its requested scope. Mark each row Pass, Fail, or Not applicable with a reason. Pass requires visible evidence in the output, not an intention to add it later. Any failed critical decision or launch prerequisite means the output needs revision or must be explicitly marked provisional. These review conventions are package implementation choices.

## Pass/fail gates

| Check | Pass condition | Method basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | The package answers the requested decision without unnecessary planning layers. | Planning purpose [S4] |
| Strategy drivers | Audience research, business model/GTM motion, and advantages explain the choices. | [S1] |
| Evidence | Company facts are supported; assumptions and material gaps are labeled. | Research and forecast [S2] |
| Audience | Company and person ICPs are usable and investment priorities are explicit. | [S2] |
| Ecosystem | Relevant partners, communities, and trusted sources extend beyond competitors; unconfirmed access is labeled. | [S2] [S3] |
| Advantages | Each advantage has evidence, a growth mechanism, and activation work; no forced category or founder story. | [S3] |
| Revenue levers | All four are ranked for the period, and commitments reflect the ranking. | [S1] [S5] |
| Positioning | Who it is for, what it is, and differentiation from the selected alternative are understandable. | [S2] |
| Perceptions | Full plans have three to five distinctive audience-voiced beliefs; campaign briefs identify relevant beliefs. | [S2] |
| Dashboard | ICPs, advantages, Perceptions, levers, and goals recur consistently in work decisions. | [S1] [S5] |
| KPO goals | Applicable KPI, Project, and Ops commitments have measurable outcomes or conditions, dates, and owners. | [S4] |
| Portfolio | Existing work is stopped, maintained, or scaled; new work has strategic metadata and justified deferrals. | [S5] |
| Depth and capacity | Ongoing work, big bets, tests, and foundation fit available capacity; proven priorities receive meaningful investment. | [S5] |
| Fuel and engine | Each committed campaign pairs useful creative with feasible distribution, processes, and measurement. | [S7] |
| Channel mix | Engines fit the audience and GTM; tests precede scale, with dependencies between channels considered. | [S2] |
| GACCS brief | Goals, Audience, Creative, Channels, and Stakeholders are defined before production. | [S1] [S6] |
| Ownership | Each campaign has one DRI and named contributors, reviewers, and handoff owners. | [S8] |
| Audience value | Content solves a real audience problem and contributes something beyond generic repetition. | [S2] [S6] |
| Mileage | Anchor and derivative assets each have distribution, a useful next action, and a planned revisit. | [S2] |
| Lifecycle | Where relevant, account and contact stages, qualification signals, ownership, and stage measures are distinct. | [S2] |
| Team design | Resource recommendations address foundation, fuel, engine, or coordination gaps rather than titles alone. | [S1] |
| Forecast | Where in scope, historical inputs, timing, costs, assumptions, and target gaps are explicit and arithmetic is checked. | [S2] [S4] |
| Learning | The output states review timing, what to learn, and evidence for scaling, revision, or stopping. | [S2] [S8] |

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure mode | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Founder request becomes the priority because it is urgent | Evaluate audience fit, advantage, lever, and displaced work before committing. [S1] [S5] |
| Lead targets substitute for the plan | Add big-bet Project work and the Ops prerequisites needed to achieve the outcome. [S4] |
| Content volume is mistaken for strategy | Choose a useful anchor asset and explain the audience problem and Perception it serves. [S2] [S6] |
| Every strength becomes an advantage | Require evidence of a distinct growth mechanism; label potential advantages requiring investment. [S3] |
| Feature list becomes the entire story | Connect positioning with company insight, customer problems, and differentiated Perceptions. [S2] |
| All initiatives receive equal attention | Rank levers and audiences, remove mismatches, then deepen proven priorities while retaining focused tests. [S5] |
| Low reach is blamed on bad content | Audit distribution, audience access, and next-action friction before commissioning replacements. [S7] |
| Repurposing is treated as distribution | Give each derivative a channel, owner, consumption context, and next step. [S2] |
| Automated channels have no useful fuel | Brief the audience value and creative concept before expanding tooling or spend. [S7] |
| No one owns the cross-functional handoff | Name one DRI and define contributors, qualification triggers, and follow-up ownership. [S2] [S8] |
| Forecast magically matches leadership's target | Restore historical rates, show the gap, and propose evidenced changes to reach, activities, or resources. [S2] |
| Every quarter requires a new campaign | Reuse, adapt, and extend successful campaigns; replace weaker work when better opportunities emerge. [S8] |

## Final review note

Record: [Pass or provisional], [remaining failures], [decision or evidence needed], [owner], [date]. A provisional plan can be useful when uncertainty is explicit; a supposedly final plan with hidden assumptions cannot guide allocation reliably.
