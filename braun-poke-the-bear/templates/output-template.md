# Prospecting deliverable template

Fill in the brief, choose one channel draft, and complete the branch notes and rationale. Bracketed fields are instructions to replace, not text to send. This schema is the package's operational convention. It applies Braun's progression from relevant job to current-process exploration and an optional next step. [S1][S3][S5]

## 1. Internal brief

- **Assignment:** [Draft or review; call or email; intended audience. State what the user requested.]
- **Seller identity and capability:** [Name, company, and one truthful sentence about what the offering changes. Avoid unsupported guarantees.]
- **Prospect and job:** [Name if supplied, responsibility, and outcome they own. Separate a known responsibility from an inferred title.]
- **Known facts and evidence:** [List relevant facts with their actual provenance. Method sources do not substantiate claims about this prospect.]
- **Unknowns:** [Ownership, current tools, completeness, consequences, and willingness to talk that remain unverified.]
- **Current approach:** [Known process, or an explicitly labelled hypothesis. Do not assert a tool they may not use.]
- **Possible exposure:** [One mechanism that could leave a gap in that approach. If no credible mechanism is available, begin with a process question.]
- **Possible consequence:** [Why the exposure could matter. Quantify only with applicable support; otherwise stay qualitative.]
- **Success criterion:** [Determine relevance and willingness. Include satisfaction or no fit as legitimate findings.]

Use these fields to prevent a generic benefit from replacing an investigation of the job. The fact/hypothesis distinction is a package safeguard for applying the source examples. [S2][S3][S4]

## 2A. Call draft

**Opening:**

[Identify yourself and company. Choose one direct or permission opening. If asking for a brief exchange, make permission real.]

**Ownership or relevant context:**

[Ask who handles the job if unknown, or use a tentative observation supported by research. Omit redundant qualification. Mention shared context only if true.]

**Optional problem frame:**

[One short, non-accusatory description of a possible exposure. Use a peer pattern only with evidence. Leave room for it not to apply.]

**Main process question:**

[One how or what question about the job or verification gap. Optional small menu of current approaches, with room for correction.]

**Answer point:**

[Pause. Listen. After the answer, wait briefly before responding.]

**Possible reflection:**

[Provide a flexible cue for reflecting what they actually say. Do not script an assumed admission or emotion.]

**Conditional explanation and next step:**

[Use only when asked or when interest appears: explain the relevant capability briefly, then offer an optional later conversation. Honor the short-call promise.]

The opening choices, menus, listening cues, and brief follow-up reflect distinct public examples; do not stack them all into a monologue. [S1][S5][S7][S9][S10][S11]

## 2B. Email draft

**Subject:** [Short, accurate process topic. Do not imply an incident or loss you have not verified.]

[Greeting]

[Brief truthful identity and relevance. If ownership is uncertain, leave room for correction.]

[Optional one-sentence exposure context, only if needed.]

[One neutral process question as the reply point.]

[Signature]

Remove call permission rituals and stage directions. Keep branches outside the sent body. The one-question format is this package's adaptation; Braun's underlying email guidance rejects leading benefit questions. [S2]

## 3. Response branches

| Situation | Fill-in guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Adequate process | [Acknowledge success and close. Do not invent another threat to preserve the pitch.] |
| Revealed gap | [Reflect their specific gap and, if welcome, ask one clarification about how it affects the job.] |
| Explicit refusal | [Acknowledge and stop. No final rebuttal or clarification question.] |
| Dismissal with continued willingness | [Optional permission-based clarification of satisfaction, relevance, or ownership. Skip when unwelcome.] |
| Expressed interest | [Explain the capability in relation to their process and offer a useful, optional next step.] |
| Wrong owner | [Accept correction. Ask about ownership only when the conversation remains welcome.] |

These branches reconcile detached intent with optional discovery and conditional progression. [S5][S8][S10][S12]
