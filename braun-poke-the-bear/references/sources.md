# Public source register

All sources below were fetched with curl and read on 2026-10-02. The listening PDF was extracted locally for reading. This package paraphrases public material; it includes no book or paywalled text. Source numbers remain consistent across the package. LinkedIn comments are not treated as Braun's guidance.

[S1] Josh Braun, **Poke the Bear cold call framework**. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/josh-braun_poke-the-bear-cold-call-framework-1-open-activity-7367171705728937984-0qeR
Contribution: A direct opening, responsibility check, contextual problem, open process question, pause, and conditional offer.

[S2] Josh Braun, **How to Poke the Bear**. https://joshbraun.com/how-to-poke-the-bear/
Contribution: Contrasts leading benefit questions with questions that examine an overlooked exposure in the current solution, including cold email.

[S3] Josh Braun, **Lowering Resistance During a Cold Call**. https://joshbraun.com/lowering-resistance-during-a-cold-call/
Contribution: Explains sales resistance, detached curiosity, permission, and determining whether a problem exists.

[S4] Josh Braun, **95% of Salespeople Don't Need to Overcome Objections, They Need to Poke the Bear**. https://joshbraun.com/95-of-salespeople-dont-need-to-overcome-objections-they-need-to-poke-the-bear/
Contribution: Shows why generic savings messages resemble existing solutions and how a specific potential cost can earn attention.

[S5] Josh Braun, **Lowering Resistance**. https://joshbraun.com/lowering-resistance/
Contribution: Demonstrates permission, ownership verification, a menu of current approaches, and a product explanation after a prospect asks why.

[S6] Josh Braun, **I Would Love To . . .**. https://joshbraun.com/i-would-love-to/
Contribution: Replaces seller-centered demo requests with a possible unexamined problem, using a peer story to avoid personal criticism.

[S7] Josh Braun, **How to Approach People Who Have Little Desire to Talk With You**. https://joshbraun.com/how-to-approach-people-who-have-little-desired-to-talk-with-you/
Contribution: Uses real shared context to establish relevance and stresses curiosity over a fixed resistance-lowering script.

[S8] Josh Braun, **People Aren't Targets**. https://joshbraun.com/people-arent-targets/
Contribution: Warns against objectifying prospects, omission, refusing to accept no, and pressure created through offer time limits.

[S9] Josh Braun, **Listening**, public PDF. https://joshbraun.com/?download_id=14562&sdm_process_download=1
Contribution: Gives listening practices: a two-beat pause, tentative labels, short mirrors, elaboration, presence, and a listening-to-speaking goal.

[S10] Josh Braun, **Poke the Bear for Aunt Flow**. https://joshbraun.com/poke-the-bear-for-aunt-flow/
Contribution: Demonstrates a specific process question and ending a promising unexpected call with a later, optional conversation.

[S11] Josh Braun, **Do Your Cold Calls Feel Like An Interrogation?**. https://joshbraun.com/do-your-cold-calls-feel-like-an-interrogation/
Contribution: Warns against excessive questioning and shows how research-based observations and labels support conversation.

[S12] Josh Braun, **Defusing I'm not interested.** https://joshbraun.com/defusing-im-not-interested/
Contribution: Diagnoses low relevance, vague language, wrong ownership, overexcited delivery, and monologues; offers menus and detached clarification.
