# Amanda Natividad's Zero-Click Marketing

Based on Amanda Natividad's public method. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Amanda Natividad.

Build marketing that teaches, clarifies, or earns recognition where an audience already spends time. A reader should gain something meaningful before visiting a website. Links can extend that experience and support conversion; the useful substance must survive without them. Treat comprehension, trust, recall, preference, and future demand as possible contributions, then connect the chosen contribution to a specific business purpose. [S1] [S2] [S7]

## When to use

Use this skill to create or improve social posts, newsletter issues, webinar promotions, short native explainers, or derivatives of research and longer content. Also use it to plan audience-led distribution or review campaigns whose referral reports capture only part of their influence. Start with the requested deliverable and scale the supporting brief to its size. A single post needs a short rationale; a recurring program needs a premise, distribution choices, and a review plan. [S3] [S4]

If the request concerns a transactional announcement, preserve the facts and required action. Apply standalone usefulness where it helps readers decide, without forcing an educational essay into a simple notice. If audience evidence is missing, label assumptions and suggest a small validation step. Never invent customer research, performance results, or proof that a platform will reward a particular tactic. These are implementation safeguards for this package.

## Procedure

The following loop synthesizes Natividad's public guidance into an agent workflow. The template and review gates are adaptations, rather than an additional framework attributed to her. Read the relevant sections of the linked files as needed.

1. **Scope the assignment.** Identify the audience, offer, requested channels, existing assets, constraints, and required action. Establish whether the output is a draft, an audit, or a campaign plan. Use the brief fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md).

2. **Define the contribution.** Write a specific audience change and explain how it could support the business. Choose a quality standard narrower than increasing revenue. See the purpose rules in [references/method.md](references/method.md). [S3] [S7]

3. **Find an observed trigger.** Review supplied questions, support notes, interviews, reviews, and community discussions. Record the evidence and audience language; distinguish observation from inference. Use the research rules in [references/method.md](references/method.md). [S6]

4. **Select and validate the idea.** Compare audience need, business relevance, information gap, and lasting usefulness. Use a quick audience question, social test, search check, or competitive gap to validate production effort. Consult the idea decisions in [references/method.md](references/method.md). [S6]

5. **Plan the destination first.** Explain who should encounter the idea, where, in which native format, and how feedback can return. Match the brand's defensible viewpoint to that audience's need. See distribution guidance in [references/method.md](references/method.md). [S4]

6. **Choose a complete unit of value.** Extract one useful conclusion, demonstration, practical instruction, or recognizable frustration. Select a compact argument, substantive bullets, or an emotionally grounded opening. See treatments in [references/method.md](references/method.md) and comparisons in [examples/worked-examples.md](examples/worked-examples.md). [S1]

7. **Write for native consumption.** Put the explanation inside the post, video, or email. Include the context necessary to defend it. For newsletters, make tips and curated summaries useful in the inbox. Follow the draft fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). [S1] [S3]

8. **Add the next action.** Offer relevant depth, subscription, registration, or commercial follow-up after delivering value. Keep the route easy to find and explain what additional benefit it provides. Apply the CTA rules in [references/method.md](references/method.md). [S2] [S5]

9. **Design meaningful derivatives.** Give each destination its own complete insight and appropriate form. Sequence related ideas to build understanding instead of repeating a title and URL. Use the derivative table in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). [S4] [S6]

10. **Set the measurement plan.** Choose business outcomes, audience signals, a comparison period, and attribution limits before evaluating performance. Use the measurement guidance in [references/method.md](references/method.md) and fields in [templates/output-template.md](templates/output-template.md). [S2] [S5]

11. **Review, deliver, and learn.** Remove links temporarily and test usefulness. Fix failed checks, deliver usable copy with a proportional rationale, and feed relevant questions and observations into the next research cycle. Apply [checklists/review-checklist.md](checklists/review-checklist.md). [S1] [S6]
