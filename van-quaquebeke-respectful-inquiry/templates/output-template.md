# Conversation plan or exchange record

Use this fill-in template for a concrete conversation. Select the relevant fields and omit unnecessary ones. Label the artifact **preparation**, **revised script**, or **actual exchange record**. Keep hypothetical branches separate from observed answers. The fields are this package's implementation of inquiry plus listening, honest advocacy, and visible treatment of contributions. ## 1. Context and purpose

- **Output mode:** [Preparation / revised script / actual exchange record]
- **Issue:** [Observable event or practical question]
- **Participants and roles:** [Who is involved and whose perspective is needed]
- **Purpose:** [What this exchange should help clarify or decide]
- **Known facts:** [Evidence already supplied, including its origin]
- **Working assumptions:** [Your current explanation, explicitly tentative]
- **Missing information:** [What cannot be answered from the available record]

Guidance: Describe behavior or circumstances before interpreting motivation. Attribute reported information to its speaker. Do not turn your assumption into the opening question's premise.

## 2. Boundaries and listening conditions

- **Fixed requirements:** [What is already settled and why]
- **Open choices:** [What input can actually influence]
- **Decision authority:** [Who decides and what the speaker can propose]
- **Intention check:** [What an unexpected answer could change]
- **Attention risks:** [Time pressure, distractions, hierarchy, distance, or control pressures]
- **Relevant history:** [Reported earlier experiences that may affect this invitation]
- **Listening opportunity:** [Available interval or agreed continuation]

Guidance: Use a direct statement for a fixed requirement. Explain the scope of influence without promising adoption. Consider the theory's contextual barriers and interaction history. Do not diagnose the participant's attitude or personality. [S1] [S3]

## 3. Opening and conditional exploration

- **Boundary statement, if needed:** [Plain statement of the constraint]
- **Opening invitation:** [One open question about the person's experience or assessment]
- **Listening instruction:** [Wait, attend to the answer, then reflect before advancing]
- **Reflection form:** [Specific point understood + tentative interpretation + invitation to correct]
- **If the person introduces [topic]:** [Follow-up that explores their meaning]
- **If the person challenges [assumption]:** [Acknowledge and revise the interpretation]
- **If they need information before answering:** [Relevant fact or guidance to supply]

Guidance: Include only branches likely to help the listener attend. These are possibilities, not forecast answers. During live chat, send the opening question and wait instead of sending the whole branch set. Sustained listening is required after the invitation. [S2] [S4]

## 4. Contribution record

For preparation, leave these fields pending. Complete them only from an actual exchange.

- **Speaker's account:** [Faithful summary of what was actually said]
- **Reflection and correction:** [What you understood and what the speaker corrected]
- **Follow-up tied to their answer:** [Question asked and the contribution it explored]
- **Updated understanding:** [What changed, or what remained unchanged and why]
- **Unverified details:** [Reports that require checking]
- **Remaining uncertainty or disagreement:** [Unresolved points without invented consensus]

Guidance: A reflection is tentative. Avoid asserting feelings or causal claims the speaker did not supply. Distinguish no change after consideration from no evidence that the contribution was considered.

## 5. Options, guidance, and closure

- **Ownership invitation:** [Prompt for their preferred next step and support needs]
- **Their proposal:** [Proposed option, or pending if not yet heard]
- **Leader or agent contribution:** [Information, constraint, recommendation, or decision]
- **Treatment of input:** [What was adopted, declined, or left open, with reasons]
- **Next action:** [Concrete action]
- **Status:** [Proposed / accepted / assigned by decision authority / pending]
- **Owner and timing:** [Supported responsibility and date or review point]
- **Follow-through:** [How the contributor will hear what happened]
- **Preserved disagreement:** [Position still held, if any]

Guidance: Supply expertise when useful; avoid endless questioning. Only label an action accepted when the record supports acceptance. A leader's commitment is not a commitment from another team. Make input traceable rather than silently discarding it. [S2] [S3] [S6]

## 6. Final design check

- **Autonomy:** [Where the person can express a different view or develop an option]
- **Competence:** [Where their actual knowledge or reasoning is engaged]
- **Relatedness:** [Where the response attends to this person's account]
- **Review status:** [Pass / revise / incomplete evidence]
- **Needed repair:** [Specific change or missing listening opportunity]

Guidance: These are qualitative design checks derived from the motivational account, not psychological scores. Run [review-checklist](../checklists/review-checklist.md) before delivering. [S1]
