Use this as a working record and adapt the visible handoff to the request. Put final copy first. Bracketed fields are prompts to replace, not text to publish. Keep methodological [S#] citations separate from assignment-specific evidence identifiers such as E1.

**[Headline or subject]:** [State the main promise or recognizable use moment. Make the offer clear in this line or its immediate context.]
