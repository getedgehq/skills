# Review checklist

Review the finished unit in its real context. Mark every row Pass or Fail, with a brief reason. A failed row needs a repair or a clearly disclosed limitation before handoff. The checklist is a package review convention; citations identify the underlying advice.

## Pass/fail checks

| Check | Pass condition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assignment | Copy matches the requested reader, channel, length, and deliverable. | [Pass/Fail] |
| Reader value | The reader can identify why the offer matters to their situation. [S2] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Offer clarity | An unfamiliar reader can tell what is offered from the complete unit. [S3] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Picture | The main promise evokes a relevant object, action, or recognizable moment. [S1] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Checkability | Material assertions have a defined observation that could confirm or contradict them. [S1][S2] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Actual support | Each factual promise has adequate assignment-specific evidence and necessary conditions. [S6] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Brand fit | A useful fact grounds the copy in this offer; unresolved differentiation is disclosed. [S1] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Customer language | Documented phrases preserve their meaning; invented testimonials are absent. [S4] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Mechanism and proof | Explanation and available proof support this exact promise. [S3] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Natural voice | The copy reads aloud comfortably without forced personality or imitation. [S5] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Economy | Every retained phrase adds meaning, tone, or necessary accuracy. [S6] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Next step | The CTA and destination agree on what the reader gets next. [S2][S3] | [Pass/Fail] |
| Honest handoff | Missing facts are visible; a passing writing audit is not represented as a conversion guarantee. | [Pass/Fail] |

A final copy can be handed off with a disclosed specificity limitation. Unsupported factual claims must be removed or narrowed. Do not turn a partial audit into an unconditional three-test pass.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Diagnostic sign | Targeted fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstraction dressed up | New synonyms still describe growth, excellence, or transformation. | Return to a real use moment and name the activity or artifact. [S1] |
| Decorative vividness | The scene is memorable, but nobody can identify the offer. | Add a plain explanation or choose a product-relevant scene. [S3] |
| Invented precision | A number appears without a source or measured conditions. | Remove it; describe supported behavior instead. [S2][S6] |
| Mechanism inflated into outcome | A feature is treated as guaranteed revenue, speed, or reliability. | State the demonstrated operation and separate any outcome evidence. |
| Cosmetic differentiation | The only offer-specific element is the brand name. | Examine the reader, process, terms, or useful combination of facts. [S1] |
| False exclusivity | Copy says only or best because alternatives were not checked. | Remove the superlative and disclose what remains unknown. |
| Irrelevant proof | A large metric sits beside an unrelated promise. | Use evidence for that exact claim or narrow the promise. [S3] |
| Customer voice distorted | A quotation becomes a universal guarantee or invented endorsement. | Restore its scope and attribution; use observed patterns carefully. [S4] |
| Forced casualness | Slang, jokes, or intimacy distract from the reader's problem. | Read aloud and use familiar, appropriate language. [S5] |
| Cleverness without direction | A striking line leads nowhere useful. | Reconnect the hook, mechanism, and next step. [S6] |
| Overloaded headline | Every feature, proof point, and condition is crammed into one line. | Keep the central promise; move support into adjacent copy. [S3] |
| Misleading brevity | Editing deletes an important limit on the claim. | Restore the condition even if the line becomes longer. |

## Release decision

Deliver when the requested copy is understandable, relevant, and supported. If all three rhetorical tests fail, revisit the idea. If specificity alone remains unresolved, provide the honest draft and state that gap. Keep any internal audit out of the visible response when the user requested final copy only. [S1][S6]
