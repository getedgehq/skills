# Public source register

All eight sources were fetched with curl and their public text read on 2026-10-02. Only Dunford's own article or post content informs the method, not reader comments, linked books, or material behind access restrictions. No book text was fetched or used. All guidance is paraphrased.

1. [S1] April Dunford, **LinkedIn post on apparent lack of product differentiation**. URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aprildunford_one-of-the-most-common-questions-i-get-is-activity-7242555636188676097-b937
   Contribution: Distinguishes a real value gap from internal misunderstanding and identifies causes such as overlooking wins, misunderstanding capabilities, and overly broad segmentation.
2. [S2] April Dunford, **A Quickstart Guide to Positioning**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning
   Contribution: Explains the five components, their dependency order, status quo alternatives, and the risks of unnecessary category creation.
3. [S3] April Dunford, **Positioning and Competition**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/positioning-and-competition
   Contribution: Defines competition through actual buyer choices, including manual workflows and existing suites, and excludes merely theoretical rivals.
4. [S4] April Dunford, **A Product Positioning Exercise**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-product-positioning-exercise
   Contribution: Covers releasing the inherited frame, unusual attributes, value mapping, recognizable customer traits, implementation, dated documentation, and iteration.
5. [S5] April Dunford, **An Introduction to Positioning**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/an-introduction-to-positioning
   Contribution: Explains positioning as context that shapes buyer assumptions and distinguishes the strategic components from messaging and branding.
6. [S6] April Dunford, **Product Framing Can Help Grow Your Startup (or Kill It)**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/product-framing-can-help-grow-your-startup-or-kill-it
   Contribution: Describes four framing styles, their risks, and consequences for pricing, channels, customer expectations, and product direction.
7. [S7] April Dunford, **Storytelling vs Positioning**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/storytelling-vs-positioning
   Contribution: Warns that persuasive stories built on internally invented customer and value assumptions can fail; positioning must supply the inputs.
8. [S8] April Dunford, **Positioning vs Strategy vs Vision**. URL: https://www.aprildunford.com/post/positioning-vs-strategy-vs-vision
   Contribution: Separates present buyer value from future ambition and explains why sales and investor narratives have different purposes.
