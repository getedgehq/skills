# Positioning canvas and validation plan

Use this after investigating the components, not as a sentence-filling exercise to discover positioning. Delete instructional text from the finished deliverable. Keep empty fields explicitly unknown rather than supplying invented facts. This is a package-created analytical template, not Dunford's own template. [S2] [S4]

- **Offering and current version:** [Name and the product or service available today. Separate optional modules if their capabilities differ.]

- **Prepared on:** [Date.]

## 2. Evidence register

| ID | Observation or claim | Evidence and date | Label | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | [Specific observation] | [Interview, deal note, demo, or usage record] | [Observed / hypothesis / unknown / future] | [What it cannot establish] |

Use E identifiers for product evidence and S identifiers only for method sources. For example, a demo can verify an export function without establishing labor savings. Record contrary observations as well as supportive ones. Reliance on actual business results is central to avoiding a persuasive but unsupported story. [S7]

## 5. Best fit customers and boundaries

- **Target segment:** [Recognizable customers with the strongest need for the mapped value.]
- **Observable fit signals:** [Workflow, dependency, data characteristic, frequency, or trigger that a seller could identify.]
- **Why they care intensely:** [Connect each condition to V IDs.]
- **Examples in the evidence:** [E IDs for customers or wins that support the pattern.]
- **Poor fit boundary:** [Conditions where the outcome matters less or the current alternative is adequate.]
- **Reach and commercial viability:** [How they can be found, whether the reachable opportunity supports the goal, and remaining uncertainty.]
- **Other segment considered:** [Its value intensity, different alternatives if applicable, and reason for lower priority.]

Industry and company size can aid discovery, but explain the specific conditions behind fit. A narrow segment still needs to support the business objective. [S4] [S6]
