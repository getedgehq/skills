# Method: CEO-led thought leadership with execution

Credit: Based on Moritz Neuhaus's public guidance and INSIGHT's public service descriptions. The 2020 founder interview also includes Thomas Reck; advice from his answers is identified separately. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Moritz Neuhaus.

## 1. What is documented, and what is reconstructed

Neuhaus's early published process has five stages: understand the environment, position the brand, produce content, expand, and achieve Messbarer Profitshift, or measurable business change. This is a strategic sequence, not a mandate to post first and infer a brand later. [S2]

The later service model, Thought-Leadership-as-a-Service (TLaaS), integrates positioning, content, reporting, and wider distribution. The INSIGHT Thought Leader Framework is publicly named, but its complete internal implementation is not disclosed in the fetched sources. This package does not reconstruct hidden scoring systems or claim proprietary completeness. [S7]

Sections labeled **Application** translate those public principles into agent decisions, fields, and safeguards. Those translations, sample schedules, qualification definitions, and review gates are package conventions. They should not be attributed as Neuhaus's own prescribed tools. Source IDs resolve in [sources.md](sources.md).

## 2. Readiness: amplify genuine leadership

Start with existing business expertise and a leader whose actions can support the public story. Neuhaus says the service is not intended to invent an entirely new expertise or business model. A credible relationship and executive involvement matter. [S4]

In the joint interview, Reck argues that business fundamentals should already work and warns that amplifying a poor leader can backfire. This is Reck's contribution to the shared public account, not an independently documented Neuhaus rule. [S2]

**Application:** Record real expertise, evidence, willingness to engage, support capacity, and internal credibility. If the executive wants a fabricated persona or refuses to contribute views, pause content production and state the prerequisite. A quiet technical expert may be suitable without becoming an entertainer. The public podcast summary explicitly questions whether every leader should pursue entertainment-led visibility. [S6]

Agency commercial qualification criteria are context, not universal eligibility rules. Adapt the scope to the user's actual organization and budget rather than treating INSIGHT's published client thresholds as mandatory.

## 3. Define the business job before the channel

The purpose is company value through an identifiable person, not attention for its own sake. Neuhaus explicitly distinguishes actual customer or talent appointments from likes and reach. [S8]

**Application:** Choose a primary job and an observable outcome:

| Job | Relevant audience | Example qualified outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer acquisition | Buyers with the problem the company solves | A conversation with a fitting buyer and an agreed next step |
| Talent attraction | Candidates for priority roles | A relevant applicant entering the hiring process |
| Industry relevance | Journalists, organizers, peers, decision makers | A relevant invitation, citation, or stakeholder conversation |

These qualification definitions are illustrative conventions, not source-provided benchmarks. Define them with the user. Separate primary and secondary objectives when one leader serves several stakeholders. Do not resolve competing objectives by counting all interactions as equally valuable.

## 4. Understand the environment

Neuhaus starts with the CEO's setting, company objectives, role models, and anti-role models. The 2020 account describes a four to six week setup phase, not a timeless deadline. [S2]

**Application:** Build a compact map of internal stakeholders, external decision makers, market concerns, existing company narratives, competing voices, and the CEO's established reputation. For each role model, capture the useful characteristic and the contextual difference. Anti-role models expose boundaries: excessive self-promotion, a voice the executive cannot sustain, or topics irrelevant to buyers.

A celebrity's high engagement is not evidence that the same approach fits a specialist executive. Ask what the audience should associate with this leader and what concrete proof would make that association credible.

## 5. Position before producing

Positioning defines what the brand stands for, content formats, and internal and external communication guidelines before production starts. [S2]

**Application:** Use this working statement:

> For [stakeholder] facing [decision or problem], [CEO] contributes [specific expertise and stance], supported by [evidence], advancing [company objective].

This sentence structure is a package aid. Test whether a rival could use it unchanged. Replace interchangeable words such as visionary or innovative with an actual judgment, tradeoff, or practice. Select a small, workable set of topic areas; no fixed number of pillars is established in the fetched sources.

For each topic, record the audience question, executive view, available evidence, company relevance, and excluded claims. A promising topic with no authentic executive contribution belongs in the interview queue, not in a fabricated post.

## 6. Competence, warmth, and boundaries

The MYNDS episode page foregrounds competence combined with human warmth as a core success theme. It also discusses identifying suitable leadership personalities. These are show-note themes, not a fully verified transcript-level formula. [S3]

Neuhaus distinguishes persönlich, meaning personal opinions and experiences, from privat, meaning private life. Disclosure should feel acceptable to the executive and support the business objective; visibility does not require exposing family or weekend routines. He also notes that employed leaders have tighter constraints than owners. [S5]

**Application:** Demonstrate competence through a real decision, explanation, or observable practice. Add warmth through humility, attention to other people, or an authentic account of learning. Do not substitute intimate disclosure for relatability.

For an employed CEO, identify corporate communications and board interests, agreed topic boundaries, and authorized spokesperson scope. Neuhaus warns that a personal brand can create dependency on one person and provoke internal tension. [S4] Plan how company-owned materials and other credible leaders can support continuity. That continuity plan is an implementation inference, not a promised cure for person dependency.

## 7. Make the profile a useful destination

The MYNDS show notes describe LinkedIn profiles as landing pages and LinkedIn as a B2B relationship platform. [S3] Neuhaus's earlier interview says the objective determines the platform rather than declaring one platform right for everyone. [S4]

**Application:** Align headline, summary, evidence, featured material, and next action with the same positioning. A buyer should recognize relevance before being asked to engage. Use verified accomplishments and approved examples. Do not invent awards, Top Voice recognition, or client results.

Choose LinkedIn when the relevant B2B audience is present. Consider other channels for demonstrated stakeholder reasons. Do not confuse LinkedIn-as-CRM framing with permission to scrape contacts or send automated messages.

## 8. Design execution around scarce CEO time

Neuhaus describes full execution with writers, designers, and videographers, supported by planned categories and executive wording input. In video, preparation can be delegated but the CEO's presence cannot be replaced. [S4]

The TLaaS post emphasizes prepared recommendations, implementation, measurement, one coordinating contact, and weekly executive involvement. [S7]

**Application:** Name a coordinator. Assign content capture, writing, design, fact verification, executive review, distribution, and reporting. One person may cover several roles. Offer the CEO a prepared set of questions and decisions, not an empty request to provide content.

Use a bounded cycle: capture genuine input, draft, check evidence, obtain necessary executive review, prepare distribution, and report learning. Draft with existing material if the CEO is unavailable, clearly marking anything requiring confirmation. Never impersonate engagement or invent first-person stories.

Early interviews cite only a few CEO hours per month, while the later model requests weekly input. Treat these as historical service descriptions, not compatible guarantees. Agree a current time budget with the user.

## 9. Produce content that serves the positioning

Neuhaus uses individualized formats and categories, an editorial plan, and testing of tone and topics. He criticizes profiles whose large audience is disconnected from their customer structure. [S4]

**Application:** Build each draft from an audience problem, a defensible executive judgment, and specific evidence or experience. Connect it to a useful next action when appropriate. Not every post needs a sales request.

A useful working structure is situation, decision, reasoning, consequence, and reader implication. This is an editorial aid created for the package, not a named Neuhaus writing formula. Favor an actual operational lesson over a generic inspirational declaration. Preserve the CEO's natural language and privacy boundaries.

If evidence is unavailable, use a clearly labeled viewpoint, ask for a real example, or narrow the claim. Avoid synthetic anecdotes, invented customer reactions, and universal causal claims about leadership branding.

## 10. Expand according to evidence and capacity

The current homepage lists positioning, social content, public relations, long-form video, podcasts, keynotes and events, webinars, and Thought Leader Ads. Its diagram and numbered service list differ slightly, so neither should be represented as a rigid universal sequence. [S1]

The HY episode summary emphasizes organic LinkedIn activity before paid campaigns become sensible. [S6] Neuhaus's later post argues for integrated amplification beyond organic content alone. [S7] These statements can coexist: begin with useful positioning and audience response, then broaden delivery as objectives and resources justify it.

**Application:** Before recommending paid promotion, check whether the message is credible, the intended audience is defined, organic response offers useful evidence, and the destination and measurement are ready. No public source establishes a numerical readiness threshold.

For PR or speaking, match a specific executive contribution to a relevant publication or event. For video or podcasts, confirm participation and production capacity. A multi-channel list is not an execution plan unless each channel has a business reason, owner, and resource allocation.

Publishing, contacting outsiders, and spending remain governed by the user's actual authorization. The skill prepares assets and recommendations without granting additional external permissions.

## 11. Measure business change and revise

The five-stage account ends with Messbarer Profitshift, assessed through outcomes such as customers, investors, media opportunities, and speaking requests. Expansion includes three-month feedback loops and possible repositioning. [S2]

The current site specifies project-specific reporting, monthly reports, and quarterly strategic audits. Its 475+ and 500+ figures are agency claims rather than a validated success rate. [S1]

**Application:** Separate four layers:

1. Visibility: reach, profile activity, audience growth.
2. Relevance: response from intended stakeholders, recurring audience questions.
3. Qualified outcomes: buyer conversations, fitting candidates, suitable media invitations.
4. Business evidence: attributable opportunities, hiring progress, revenue where supported.

Record baseline, observation period, data source, qualification rule, and uncertainty. Use tagged links, authorized CRM records, and self-reported discovery where available; these attribution tools are package suggestions. Distinguish observed association, assisted influence, and direct attribution. Do not count the same opportunity twice or claim incremental ROI without adequate evidence.

The HY show notes describe an 18 to 24 month horizon for becoming a leading industry voice. [S6] Treat this as a source-reported expectation, never as a contractual promise. Shorter operational review periods and longer reputation development are different clocks.

At quarterly review, test the audience, stance, themes, format, and distribution before deciding that more volume is the answer. Keep what improves relevant response and business outcomes; revise what merely attracts broad applause.

## 12. Edge cases and warnings

- **High reach, few useful conversations:** Recheck stakeholder fit and destination before increasing production. The critique of reach-led success is explicit. [S8]
- **CEO demands total absence:** Offer preparation and production support, but explain the later model's participation requirement. [S7]
- **Private-life reluctance:** Use professional experiences and considered opinions within personally acceptable boundaries. [S5]
- **No established expertise:** Return a prerequisite plan rather than an invented authority narrative. [S4]
- **Internal resistance or executive departure:** Document interests and person dependency; agree continuity materials and company roles. The risk is public guidance, the proposed mitigation is package guidance. [S4]
- **Pressure for instant results:** Clarify that recognition develops over time and report early learning separately. [S6]
- **AI-generated sameness:** The HY summary distinguishes process efficiency from effectiveness. Use AI for preparation and editing while retaining original judgment and verified evidence. [S6]
- **Sensitive incident:** Package safeguard: verify facts and involve the appropriate company decision maker before drafting a public position. Reputation work cannot replace resolving the underlying incident.
- **Contradictory historical advice:** Preserve dates and service context. Do not blend old fees, time budgets, or market statistics into current universal rules.

There is no universal golden move in Neuhaus's 2021 account. Adapt to the leader and context while preserving the sequence from objectives and positioning to execution and measurable impact. [S5]
