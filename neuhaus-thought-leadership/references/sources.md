## Evidence boundaries

The homepage now foregrounds a wider service mix than the early interviews. It contains both 475+ strategy and 500+ project references; these are different agency statements, not independently reconciled counts. Preserve the user's 475+ framing as an attributed claim and do not silently convert it into verified performance.

The homepage's resource links include a third-party thought leadership study and a signup-gated LinkedIn book. Neither is cited here because their contents were not fetched. No dedicated agency blog article, full client case-study document, newsletter edition, or talk transcript is represented as read. S1 contains testimonial excerpts and S7 names client examples, but neither supports guaranteed outcomes or causal performance claims.

Source titles above are descriptive paraphrases where needed. This package uses no verbatim source excerpts. Original method and service names are retained for attribution, including Thought-Leadership-as-a-Service, INSIGHT Thought Leader Framework, and Messbarer Profitshift.
