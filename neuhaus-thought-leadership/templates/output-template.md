# CEO positioning and execution brief

This is a package-created deliverable structure, not an official INSIGHT template. Replace bracketed fields with verified inputs, clear recommendations, or explicit unknowns. Remove field guidance from the client-facing version. Do not fill gaps with invented executive experience, customer results, budgets, or permissions.

## 1. Mandate and decision

- **Executive and role:** [Name, title, owner or employed executive, organization]. Guidance: role affects discretion and stakeholder responsibilities.
- **Business situation:** [What the organization is trying to achieve and why now]. Guidance: state the concrete trigger, not merely a desire to be visible.
- **Requested scope:** [Strategy, profile, content batch, execution plan, review, or combination]. Guidance: preserve the user's requested deliverable.
- **Primary objective:** [One prioritized business job]. Guidance: identify the decision this brief supports.
- **Secondary objectives:** [If any, with tradeoffs]. Guidance: distinguish recruiting, customer acquisition, and industry influence.
- **Readiness recommendation:** [Proceed, narrow scope, or prerequisite work]. Guidance: provide evidence and explain missing fundamentals or executive participation.
- **Known constraints and unknowns:** [Time, support, budget, evidence, permissions]. Guidance: identify which missing inputs actually block a recommendation.

## 3. Positioning and credibility

- **Positioning statement:** For [audience] facing [problem], [CEO] contributes [expertise and stance], supported by [evidence], advancing [company objective].
- **Central judgment:** [What the CEO believes and the tradeoff behind it]. Guidance: ensure the executive can explain and defend it.
- **Proof available:** [Verified experience, approved case material, work product, credential]. Guidance: add the owner or location of supporting evidence.
- **Claims awaiting confirmation:** [Specific claim and person who can verify it]. Guidance: prevent uncertain facts from becoming publication-ready language.
- **Topic areas:** [Workable set]. Guidance: give each an audience question, stance, evidence, company connection, and excluded claims; no mandatory number applies.
- **Differentiation test:** [Why a rival could not use this unchanged]. Guidance: resolve interchangeable language before production.

## 4. Voice, personal boundaries, and company alignment

- **Voice traits:** [Natural qualities with a short wording example]. Guidance: preserve the executive's language rather than inventing a persona.
- **Competence signals:** [Decisions or expertise to demonstrate]. Guidance: identify substance before stylistic polish.
- **Human warmth:** [Confirmed professional learning or attention to others]. Guidance: do not require intimate disclosure.
- **Persönlich content permitted:** [Opinions and experiences the executive accepts].
- **Privat content excluded:** [Private-life boundaries]. Guidance: only record necessary boundaries, not unnecessary private details.
- **Company alignment:** [Relevant decision makers and agreed scope]. Guidance: distinguish internal review needs from general publishing permissions.
- **Sensitive topics and handling:** [Real constraints, reviewer, escalation trigger]. Guidance: keep this proportional to the actual organization.

## 5. Profile and channel recommendation

- **Summary draft:** [Problem, stance, proof, company relationship, next action].
- **Featured material:** [Existing approved assets or proposed assets with owners].

- **Starting channel and rationale:** [Where the audience is and what the channel enables].
- **Additional channel conditions:** [Business reason and readiness requirement]. Guidance: avoid adding channels only to complete a list.
