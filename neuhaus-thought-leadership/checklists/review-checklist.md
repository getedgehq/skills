# Review checklist

This is a package-created quality gate. It translates the public method into reviewable output; it is not an official INSIGHT audit. Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL, with an evidence pointer. Use N/A only with a reason. A plan can pass with clearly disclosed unknowns that do not undermine its recommendation. Unverified claims in publishable content fail.

## Pass/fail checks

| Check | PASS condition | FAIL condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Deliverable answers the user's actual request | Unrequested channels or services dominate |
| Readiness | Existing expertise, business context, and executive participation are documented | Authority or experiences are invented |
| Objective | Primary business job and qualified outcome are defined | Success means only followers, likes, or raw reach |
| Stakeholder fit | Positioning responds to a specific audience decision | Audience is everyone or an irrelevant viral crowd |
| Environment | Competitors, role models, anti-role models, and internal interests inform decisions | Celebrity tactics are copied without context |
| Positioning | Stance, proof, and company link are defensible | Generic inspiration or unsupported category leadership |
| Competence and warmth | Substance and authentic human perspective both appear | Corporate jargon or forced intimate disclosure |
| Personal boundaries | Persönlich and privat boundaries are respected | Private details are exposed to manufacture relatability |
| Employed executive context | Actual company constraints and relevant review roles are clear | Public statements exceed the executive's agreed scope |
| Profile destination | Role, relevance, proof, and next action reinforce the positioning | Profile and content promise different things |
| Evidence | Experiences, numbers, client claims, and credentials are verified or withheld | Fictional illustration is disguised as a real case |
| Execution | Coordinator, production roles, executive input, and review timing are workable | CEO is absent or responsible for an unrealistic workload |
| Distribution | Each channel has a reason, capacity, and owner | A service list substitutes for a plan |
| Paid expansion | Credible message, audience, measurement, budget, and authorization are addressed | Paid reach is used to compensate for unclear positioning |
| Measurement | Baseline status, qualification rules, data sources, and attribution limits are explicit | Correlation is called causal ROI or opportunities are counted twice |
| Learning | Reporting and quarterly strategy review have owners and timing | More volume is the only optimization lever |
| External actions | Draft status and authorization match actual user instructions | Strategy approval is assumed to authorize sends or spending |
| Provenance | Sources support attributed statements; additions are identified | Hidden proprietary rules or audio content are falsely claimed |
| Handover | Concrete next action, owner, date, and material unknowns are stated | The user receives only abstract recommendations |

## Common failure modes and fixes

- **Content calendar mistaken for strategy:** Return to audience, business objective, stance, and evidence. Then assign formats.
- **Interchangeable CEO voice:** Replace broad adjectives with a confirmed decision or tradeoff the executive can defend.
- **High engagement from irrelevant people:** Review who responds, tighten topic selection, and check whether the profile addresses the intended audience.
- **Promotion without expertise:** Obtain real supporting material or produce a prerequisite plan. Do not invent thought leadership.
- **Generic AI first-person story:** Replace with a verified interview detail or explicitly label it an illustration requiring replacement.
- **Forced private disclosure:** Use professional experience and opinion. Remove anything outside the executive's chosen boundaries.
- **Hands-off CEO expectation:** Delegate preparation and production; agree actual executive input and confirmation responsibilities.
- **Unsupported employer promise:** Use observable practices, acknowledge limitations, and involve the people who know daily conditions.
- **Premature multi-channel expansion:** Choose the smallest system that serves the objective; add channels when evidence and capacity justify them.
- **Unfounded business claims:** Separate visibility, relevance, qualified outcomes, and revenue evidence. Explain attribution uncertainty.
- **Short-term fame promise:** Distinguish operational review dates from reputation development. No audience ranking is guaranteed.
- **Historical advice treated as current doctrine:** Preserve source context. Old fees, time budgets, and market statistics are not current requirements.
- **475+ treated as a verified success rate:** Identify it as INSIGHT's experience claim; do not turn project counts into promised outcomes.
- **Source overclaim:** Podcast show notes support only stated themes. Testimonials and named examples do not establish independent causal case-study evidence.

## Final disposition

- **Ready:** All applicable checks pass, and remaining unknowns are visible and nonblocking.
- **Ready for executive review:** Strategy is coherent, but named facts or voice decisions require confirmation before publication.
- **Revise:** One or more failed checks undermine the recommendation or content credibility. State the specific correction and owner.
