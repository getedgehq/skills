# Review checklist

Mark each applicable item PASS or FAIL and attach a short reason or evidence pointer. Mark irrelevant items N/A with a reason. Any failed evidence, attribution, or authorization item holds the affected draft from publication. A failed handoff item holds that ownership transfer; it does not automatically stop documented production work.

## Intake and source integrity

- [ ] The intended reader and decision are specific enough to guide topic selection.
- [ ] The business objective and success evidence are explicit, without a guaranteed commercial outcome.
- [ ] Founder claims come from supplied experience, authorized notes, or an actual interview.
- [ ] Facts, interpretations, and unanswered questions are distinguishable in working notes.
- [ ] Public-method claims cite the appropriate [S#] source in the method reference.
- [ ] Phase names, interview prompts, artifacts, and diagnostics are presented as package adaptations.

## Interview and idea quality

- [ ] The idea contains a concrete situation and meaningful alternatives.
- [ ] The founder's choice and reason are explicit.
- [ ] The trade-off or rejected option has survived the draft.
- [ ] Outside challenge tested an assumption, likely misunderstanding, or reasonable objection.
- [ ] The position has a meaningful boundary or identified uncertainty.
- [ ] Missing interview answers remain gaps rather than fabricated executive statements.

## Editorial quality

- [ ] An unfamiliar reader can understand the context and vocabulary.
- [ ] The draft preserves a supported sentence revealing executive judgment.
- [ ] Review has corrected actual issues without smoothing away the useful position.
- [ ] Technical details support the reader's problem and are verified where needed.
- [ ] The draft has no invented metrics, named outcomes, client endorsements, or personal history.
- [ ] The executive's voice and reasoning are represented accurately.
- [ ] Multiple executives share product facts while retaining distinct experience lanes.
- [ ] The draft offers a reader implication rather than only a company milestone or broad value.
- [ ] Confidential details and publication permissions have been resolved for the intended use.

## Operations and handoff

- [ ] Interviewing, drafting, review, approval, and distribution have explicit owners or recorded vacancies.
- [ ] The cadence fits the founder's availability and the assignment's quality requirements.
- [ ] Production transfer includes inputs, examples, standards, and responsibilities.
- [ ] Articulation transfer is justified by clear founder perspective and stable company positions.
- [ ] External clarity is evidenced rather than inferred from funding stage or headcount.
- [ ] A functioning challenge mechanism remains where the narrative is still forming.
- [ ] Review tracks comprehension and judgment preservation as well as output or engagement.
- [ ] The next action resolves an actual gap and has an agreed owner and timing.

## Common failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Diagnostic signal | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polished but interchangeable content | Draft says innovation, trust, or leadership without a decision | Return to the episode and recover the choice, cost, and reason |
| Added controversy | Draft is stronger than the founder's evidence | Narrow the claim and retain the real exception |
| Leading extraction | Interview question supplies the desired opinion | Ask what happened and what alternatives were considered |
| Insider bubble | Readers must know internal acronyms or unstated product assumptions | Explain the context and test with an unfamiliar reader |
| Consensus erases judgment | Interview has a useful boundary; approved draft has only safe values | Identify the review objection and preserve a supportable boundary |
| Premature internalization | New hire inherits an unsettled founder narrative | Transfer documented production first and keep outside challenge |
| Permanent outsourcing by default | No readiness gate or ownership learning exists | Define the next transferable task and evidence for progression |
| Co-founder voice collapse | CEO and CTO posts are interchangeable | Use shared product facts with distinct decisions and expertise |
| Cadence overwhelms substance | More posts create more founder clarification | Reduce the agreed scope and resolve articulation or training gaps |
| Vanity measurement | Success is asserted solely from impressions or likes | Return to the audience decision and agreed business objective |
| False source authority | Package phases are described as Flores's published framework | Relabel them as implementation choices and cite only supported claims |
| Fabricated founder evidence | Plausible first-person story has no notes or interview support | Hold the story and deliver questions or an explicitly incomplete outline |

The checklist operationalizes the principles in [method.md](../references/method.md). It is not a checklist published by Jay Flores.
