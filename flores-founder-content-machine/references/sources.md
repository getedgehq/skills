## Evidence boundaries

The identity used is the author at the supplied /jay-flores post URLs, whose older posts discuss Project 33. Search surfaced unrelated people also called Jay Flores, including STEM and marketing practitioners using other handles; their material was excluded.

Discovery also checked agency resources and podcast/newsletter leads. The old Project 33 resource URLs for the early-stage SaaS founder episode and the $350k ARR LinkedIn episode redirected to the same current Finn Thormeier advisory page. Their historical article bodies were not retrievable with curl and are not cited. Public profile fetches also did not return usable profile text. Search snippets were used for discovery, never as substitutes for fetched source bodies.

These five posts support the core method. They do not establish a fixed interview cadence, a numbered handoff framework, a universal revenue promise, or a formal metric system. S3 mentions a four-month ROI window for a particular offer; this package does not generalize that window. S1's unnamed customer narrative is an author-reported illustration, not an independently verified case study. The implementation details in the other files are explicitly distinguished from Flores's direct guidance.
