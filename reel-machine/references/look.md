# The look

Measured from creator-native reels that outperform designed ones, and kept because they held
up across daily posting. Native beats corporate: no title cards, no badges, no bouncing emoji.

## Frame 0: hook + signal
- White, bold, sentence case, exactly 2 lines, centered at the top (y = 285 px of 1920), over a
  soft dark scrim. Never over the face, never UPPERCASE.
- Says the claim plainly, in the speaker's framing: a number, an insider claim, a surprising
  first-person statement. 3 to 9 words.
- **Signal:** an inline logo after the brand's name or a flag after the place
  ("SF 🇺🇸", "Claude [mark]"). It tells a stranger in one glance who or where this is.
- Face from frame 0. The hook is the thumbnail, so frame 0 must read with the sound off.

## Captions
- Small white bold (44 px), lower third, whole phrase at once (up to 3 words, broken on
  sentence ends, pauses > 0.35 s and cut points), soft shadow, no boxes, no word-by-word
  highlight, no periods.
- Exactly what was said. Jargon gets a (parenthetical). Optional smaller translation line.

## Stat cards
- Bare: big number + one small line, white, soft shadow, no plate. Above the head, on screen
  exactly while the number is spoken (fade 0.3 s). Only for a sourceable number.
- Brand logos follow the same rule: bare, at the moment the brand is named.

## Closer
- Instagram glyph + handle at the hook position, on the last ~0.9 s and held 0.6 s after the
  final word, so the reel lands instead of cutting mid-breath.

## Sound and pace
- Wind high-pass, loudness -14 LUFS, 12 ms fades at every splice, 1.1x speed-up overall.
- One continuous take beats a cut every 1.5 s. Cut repetitions before pauses.

## Grade
- Light: `eq=contrast=1.05:saturation=1.14:gamma=1.02,colorbalance=rm=-0.015:bm=0.015`, or none.
