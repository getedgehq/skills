# The look

Measured from creator-native reels that outperform designed ones, and kept because they held
up across daily posting. Native beats corporate: no title cards, no badges, no bouncing emoji.

## Frame 0: hook + signal
- White, bold, sentence case, exactly 2 lines, centered at the top (y = 285 px of 1920), over a
  soft dark scrim. Never over the face, never UPPERCASE.
- Says the claim plainly, in the speaker's framing: a number, an insider claim, a surprising
  first-person statement. 3 to 9 words.
- **Signal:** an inline logo after the brand's name or a flag after the place
  ("SF 🇺🇸", "Claude [mark]"). It tells a stranger in one glance who or where this is.
- Face from frame 0. The hook is the thumbnail, so frame 0 must read with the sound off.

## Captions
- White bold, 55 px on 1080x1920, whole phrase at once (up to 3 words, broken on sentence
  ends, pauses > 0.35 s and cut points), soft shadow, no boxes, no word-by-word highlight, no
  periods.
- Placement: the caption block ends at y~1425, under the chin and above the bottom ~420 px that
  Instagram and TikTok cover with the caption, buttons and audio bar. Lower sits under the UI;
  higher sits on the lips.
- Exactly what was said. Jargon gets a (parenthetical). Optional smaller translation line.

## Stat cards
- Bare: big number + one small line, white, soft shadow, no plate. Above the head, on screen
  exactly while the number is spoken (fade 0.3 s). Only for a sourceable number.
- Brand logos follow the same rule: bare, at the moment the brand is named.

## Cutaways
- A named place, product or thing a stranger may not picture gets a full-screen card while it is
  named: blurred ground from the same photo, title, photo across the width. Hard cut in and out,
  1.5 to 3 s, captions stay on top. Credit every Commons photo in the post caption.

## Closer
- Instagram glyph + handle at the hook position on the last second. The voice ends on a whole
  sentence and fades; the footage keeps playing under the closer for up to 1.15 s, so the reel
  lands on a face instead of cutting mid-breath.
- Product reels: the product logo + the landing page instead of the handle, and the same link
  in the post caption.

## Sound and pace
- The real voice: high-pass, a small mud cut, a small presence lift, loudness -15 LUFS. No voice
  isolation or heavy denoise: it sounds robotic and viewers notice.
- 1.1x overall, then jump cuts on every pause: silences down to ~0.1 s, lead-in under 0.15 s,
  10 ms fades at every splice. No pause over 0.35 s, dead air at most 8%, at least ~2.6 words
  per second (a further 1.1x to 1.15x if slower).
- First and last frames: eyes open, face sharp and whole.

## Grade
- Light: `eq=contrast=1.05:saturation=1.14:gamma=1.02,colorbalance=rm=-0.015:bm=0.015`, or none.
