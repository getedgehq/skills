# UGC farming operating brief

Use this template for a new program, expansion, or repair. Replace brackets with known information. Write unknown plus an owner and resolution date where evidence is missing. Label every numerical target as observed, published, modeled, or proposed. Published claims require [S#] references from the source register. All contractual and decision thresholds below are user-specific recommendations, not a published standard contract.

## 1. Decision and objective

- **Request type:** [new farm / discovery / expansion / repair]. State the decision this brief enables.
- **Product and audience:** [product, use case, category, language, geography]. Name the people likely to adopt, not just watch.
- **Primary event:** [verified install / activation / purchase / other]. Define exactly what makes it count.
- **Success ceiling:** [EUR per event, payback, retention if relevant]. Explain how business economics set the threshold.
- **Budget and owner:** [total cap, period, accountable owner]. Distinguish preparing a plan from authorized external execution.

## 2. Readiness and sequencing

- **Existing evidence:** [funnel conversion, activation, retention, current winning formats]. Identify source and date of each observation.
- **Phase choice:** [prove adoption first / bounded farm discovery / sustained farm / additional capacity]. Explain the choice using method section 2.
- **Unknowns:** [question, resolver, deadline, dependent decision]. Do not silently replace missing information with optimism.
- **Channel roles:** [organic farm, clipping, high-intent creators, paid, product pages]. Explain each selected channel's job.

## 3. Creator pool and accounts

- **Cohort:** [active creators, markets, backup candidates]. Justify the size and distinguish experimental cohorts from the published working farm range.
- **Sourcing surplus:** [researched prospects, outreach plan, offers required, selection criteria]. Use actual offers when available; planned outreach is not completed work.
- **Creator fit:** [category knowledge, language, demonstrated formats, capacity, proposed economics]. Record why each slot helps a hypothesis.
- **Account register:** [account ID, platform, market, creator operator, owner, recovery access, health status]. Dedicated accounts need explicit stewardship.
- **Posting cadence:** [per-account cadence, start date, review owner]. Derive from sustainable creator capacity and account health; do not invent a sourced warming recipe.
- **Replacement and payment owners:** [names, response times, reserve capacity]. Show how departures or payment delays are handled.

## 4. Performance agreement and rights

- **Compensation:** [fixed component if any, payable event, rate, minimum, cap]. State whether compensation rewards views, clicks, or verified outcomes.
- **Settlement:** [data source, attribution rule, event window, invalid-event exclusions, dispute process, payout date]. Specify which evidence settles payment.
- **Usage permissions:** [organic channels, clipping, paid amplification, Spark authorization if applicable, product-page use, territories, term]. Obtain agreement before producing or reusing material.
- **Disclosure and claims:** [brand relationship labels, allowed product claims, reviewer]. Preserve truthful creator presentation.

## 6. Clipping and asset reuse

- **Source inventory:** [parent ID, footage owner, permissions, source duration, useful moments]. Include only cleared material.
- **Clip plan:** [derivatives, hypothesis, opening change, context, platform cuts, editor, deadline]. Explain what each variation tests.
- **Output totals:** [originals, derivatives, platform postings]. Do not present exports as distinct original ideas.
- **Reuse destinations:** [paid, Spark Ads, storefront, product page]. Match destinations to rights and the business funnel.

## 8. Weekly iteration and decision gates

- **Review schedule:** [date, owner, mature assets, event reconciliation]. Read qualified performance each week.
- **Winner action:** [observed evidence, next variant, responsible person, due date]. Describe development rather than a repost order.
- **Weak-format action:** [stop / rebrief / repair funnel / collect more evidence]. State what evidence would change the decision.
- **Scale gate:** [numerical outcome threshold, observation window, capacity requirement, next budget release]. Derive from the business, not an agency headline.
- **Hold and stop gates:** [spend ceiling, account health, unresolved rights, overdue payments, attribution failure, conversion failure]. Name the affected scope and fix owner.
