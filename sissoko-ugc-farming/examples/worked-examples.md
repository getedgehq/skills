## Illustration 1: Consumer travel app with a working activation funnel

### Weak first draft

Hire 50 travel influencers, give each a feature list, and order 30 polished videos. Post everywhere. EUR 1.20 CPM means 100M views and one million users. Repost whichever video gets the most views. Pay creators later when the campaign ends.

### Improved result

An invented travel app, WeekendMap, has a functioning self-serve funnel. Its primary event is saving a complete itinerary, with a learning-phase ceiling of EUR 4 per activated user. The growth lead owns a four-week farm with a EUR 30,000 fully loaded cap. This is a proposal, not authorization to hire or publish.

Start with 15 category-fit creators posting on dedicated creator-operated accounts. Build a sourcing surplus using the published 200 prospects, 100 contacts, and 45 real offers illustration, adjusting if the niche cannot supply that pool. Selection favors credible trip-planning demonstrations and native storytelling. Agree account ownership, recovery access, payout rules, and organic, clipping, and paid reuse rights before filming.

Monthly capacity is 15 x 30 = 450 original videos, distributed as 900 TikTok and Instagram postings. Separately, an editor makes 40 differentiated clips from cleared walkthrough recordings; posting each to both platforms adds 80 postings. Totals are 450 originals, 40 derivatives, and 980 postings, not 980 unique videos.

Brief three hypotheses: rescuing a rainy weekend plan, choosing between two realistic itineraries, and demonstrating how to save a usable route. Each has product proof and one measurable CTA. The two-option participation structure adapts the format principle in Sissoko's post; it does not copy his JD Sports execution. Central review catches broken destinations and inaccurate travel claims while preserving creator voice.

The working forecast assumes 10M recorded views, a 0.8% view-to-click rate, 25% click-to-signup conversion, and 50% signup-to-activation conversion. That produces 80,000 clicks, 20,000 signups, and 10,000 activated users. At EUR 30,000 program cost, CPM is EUR 3 and cost per activated user is EUR 3. A downside case of 5M views with the same conversion assumptions yields 5,000 activated users, EUR 6 CPM, and EUR 6 per activation. The downside misses the proposed ceiling, so expansion depends on observed results.

For this illustration, contracts propose EUR 1 per verified attributed activation within seven days of the tracked click, a EUR 500 monthly creator cap, weekly reconciliation, and payment within seven days of reconciliation. These are invented commercial terms. A maximum EUR 7,500 payout reserve leaves EUR 22,500 for production guarantees if negotiated, editing, management, account operations, and measurement. Contract review must resolve attribution across creators and treatment of late conversions.

Review weekly by hypothesis, creator, and platform with a consistent seven-day asset window. An invented observation finds one itinerary comparison produces high watch-through but few activations. Fix the destination and CTA before multiplying that format. Scale only after two mature weekly cohorts stay at or below EUR 4 per activation, payouts remain current, and the review team can support additional accounts. These gates are proposed controls, not published Sissoko thresholds.

### Rules that changed the result

- Dedicated-account distribution and sustained pool operations replaced a one-off influencer purchase. [S1][S8]
- Format mechanics plus product proof replaced a generic feature script. [S3][S7]
- Weekly winner development replaced simple reposting. [S8]
- Explicit funnel stages, cost scope, and derivative counts prevented false acquisition and output claims. These are package measurement recommendations.
- Payment and rights planning addressed the operator failure points described in the service pages. [S7][S8]

## Illustration 2: Prosumer AI writing product before PLG is proven

### Weak first draft

Launch with 50 new accounts and 3,000 monthly posts. Claim that everyone in modern marketing already uses the tool. Spend the entire EUR 75,000 launch budget on farming and expect enterprise revenue immediately. Clip popular podcast interviews whether or not we own them.

### Improved result

The invented product BriefForge has promising demonstrations but no reliable evidence that new self-serve users activate or retain. Its immediate objective is proving that solo marketers can create a usable campaign brief, rather than maximizing short-form views or enterprise pipeline claims.

Follow the software playbook's usual sequence: first prove the PLG motion using relevant high-intent channels. The published example allocates a launch sprint to YouTube and topic pages, leaving UGC for Phase 2; it is an illustration of channel roles, not a required budget split. In this invented plan, the growth lead authorizes an initial EUR 20,000 learning allocation from the EUR 75,000 envelope and holds the rest pending results. Validate the landing page, first-use path, and cohort measurement before creator volume expands. [S5]

Define proposed progression gates: 30% of verified signups produce an accepted brief, at least 20% of activated users return during week two, and acquisition cost fits a modeled six-month payback ceiling. These invented thresholds need adjustment to the product's margins and actual retention. If the gates fail, repair onboarding or product value instead of buying more attention.

After an invented successful proof phase, design a 30-creator farm with three initial videos each on dedicated niche-relevant accounts. This is 90 originals and 180 postings across two platforms. It follows the smaller Phase 2 discovery batch in the software article, rather than prematurely applying the 30-video monthly production illustration. Expand cadence only after repeatable qualified winners and operational capacity are visible. [S1][S5]

The improved brief demonstrates transforming scattered notes into a usable campaign brief, with truthful before-and-after evidence. It tests creator-authored storytelling alongside adapted organic structures. It avoids unsupported social-proof claims. Cleared founder walkthroughs supply 20 clips with parent IDs, varied openings, and independent context. External podcast material remains excluded until permissions cover editing and distribution.

If an invented discovery result shows 2M views, 12,000 clicks, 2,400 signups, and 720 activations for EUR 9,000, report EUR 4.50 CPM and EUR 12.50 per activation. Do not label 12,000 clicks as users or suppress the CPM because it exceeds the agency headline range. Retention and payback determine the next budget release. A format with strong qualified activation earns new creator adaptations and, if cleared, a limited paid test with separate reporting.

### Rules that changed the result

- Explicit objective and Phase 2 sequencing prevented premature farming scale. [S5]
- Initial asset batches preserved discovery before monthly production commitments. [S1][S5]
- Product demonstrations and modular variants replaced unsupported hype. Modular production comes from the adjacent paid UGC workflow. [S6]
- Rights-cleared clipping and separate paid reporting made reuse operationally usable. [S7]
- Observed funnel stages and retention gates prevented view counts from standing in for business validation. These are package recommendations.
