# Public sources

Credit: Simon Kaiser and the Klein aber team supplied the public thinking and case material summarized in this package. Other authors and organizations are credited below. This package is an independent English paraphrase.

All ten sources below were fetched with curl on 2026-10-02 and returned HTTP 200. Readable public text was extracted from their HTML. These are distinct documents, not ten independent validations. Agency case studies are self-reported evidence. No paywalled body text was used, and no podcast audio or transcript was reviewed.

[S1] **Simon Kaiser: 5 Gründe, warum Langform die bessere Idee ist**, MEEDIA, 2 May 2025. https://meedia.de/news/beitrag/19178-simon-kaiser-5-gruende-warum-langform-die-bessere-idee-ist.html
Contribution: Public introduction supports the relevance of longer content and complex communication; the five reasons themselves are behind MEEDIA+, so their wording and order remain unverified.

[S2] **Wie Klein aber aus Marken Youtube-Creator mit Millionen Views macht**, Martin Gardt, OMR, 15 June 2020. https://omr.com/de/daily/klein-aber-youtube-strategie
Contribution: Direct founder interview on creator-community participation, recurring branded shows, newcomer-friendly episodes, organic-to-paid amplification, analytics, and Time with Brand.

[S3] **YouTube Marketing-Strategie mit Simon Kaiser von Klein aber**, OMR Education podcast episode 222, public show notes. https://askomr.podigee.io/222-youtube-marketing-strategie-mit-simon-kaiser-von-klein-aber
Contribution: Show notes establish format thinking, sustained subscriber relationships, repeat viewing, and the possible use of multiple brand accounts; they do not support detailed quotations from the conversation.

[S4] **Die YouTube Agentur für Brand Channels, die eure Audience liebt**, Klein aber homepage, undated. https://www.kleinaber.de/
Contribution: Defines complementary roles for Shorts and regular series, platform-appropriate quality, available format types, and ongoing channel optimization.

[S5] **Wie Kleinanzeigen die Gen Z erfolgreich durch eine organische Webserie und bezahlte Medienaktivierung erreicht**, Klein aber, 2025 case page. https://kleinaber.de/case-study/kleinanzeigen-wg-think-with-google-case-study-2025/
Contribution: Describes the later combination of shorter VOD, Shorts, audience-informed creative changes, and paid activation, with self-reported subscriber, retention, target-audience view, and CPM results.

[S6] **Die Kleinanzeigen-WG YouTube Kanal: Staffel 3**, Klein aber, case describing 2022 results. https://kleinaber.de/case-study/kleinanzeigen-wg-youtube-kanal/
Contribution: Connects audience tensions around affordability and sustainability with recurring characters, serial storylines, watch time, and returning viewers; underlying commissioned research was not separately fetched.

[S7] **Über Uns**, Klein aber, undated. https://kleinaber.de/ueber-uns/
Contribution: Describes audience and topic research, Lean Video Produktion, and the Learn- & Grow-Kreislauf of measurement, analysis, optimization, application, and renewed learning.

[S8] **Kleinanzeigen startet exklusive Video-Serie mit YouTube-Stars**, Kleinanzeigen press release, 18 May 2017. https://themen.kleinanzeigen.de/medien/pressemitteilungen/kleinanzeigen-youtube-kanal-neu/
Contribution: Client-side launch account of entertainment-led original formats, creator-brand fit, recurring publication, and the contributions of TLGG, United Creators, and Klein aber.

[S9] **Eine tierisch gute Performance**, Klein aber, 4 October 2024. https://kleinaber.de/eine-tierisch-gute-performance/
Contribution: Agency analysis associates longer videos with deeper attention, lower cost per minute, and more commenting; stresses respect for audiences and warns that raw views alone change the apparent winning categories.

[S10] **Warum lange Videos trotz kurzer Aufmerksamkeitsspanne boomen**, Simon Kaiser, UPLOAD Magazin, 12 May 2024. https://upload-magazin.de/63127-warum-lange-videos-trotz-kurzer-aufmerksamkeitsspanne-boomen/
Contribution: Kaiser's own public essay on relevant depth, fan communities, meaningful watch time, Shorts as a bridge, and removing unnecessary introductions rather than assuming audiences cannot concentrate.

## Access limitations and exclusions

The Simon Kaiser LinkedIn profile at https://www.linkedin.com/in/simon-kaiser/ returned HTTP 999. No profile or post evidence was used. Search results helped discover public pages but were not treated as fetched source documents. Additional fetched navigation pages and an older Shorts article were not needed for the final synthesis and are not cited. No alternate host was used to access the locked MEEDIA article.

The MEEDIA introduction uses inconsistent labels for its 73% figure: its teaser refers to views while the body describes watch time. This package does not use that statistic or infer a required 30-minute episode length from it. Five numbered reasons in this package are an editorial synthesis, not a reproduction of MEEDIA's inaccessible list.
