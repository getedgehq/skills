Distinguish sourced principles, observed audience evidence, proposed choices, and assumptions. Do not present proposed content or targets as tested results.

## 1. Recommendation and brief

- **Recommendation:** [Build, revise, run a limited pilot, or defer]. Lead with the decision and the audience or resource reason.
- **Brand and owner:** [Name, accountable person or role]. Identify who owns editorial decisions and learning.
- **Business outcome:** [Specific intended change]. Separate awareness, consideration, affinity, or action from platform metrics.
- **Requested deliverable:** [Strategy, format, episode, audit, or pilot]. Preserve the user's scope.
- **Audience:** [Interest, situation, and relevant demographic constraints]. Explain why this group would voluntarily watch.
- **Constraints:** [Budget, team, host access, timing, required approvals, existing assets]. Identify constraints that alter feasibility.
- **Unknowns and assumptions:** [Missing fact, provisional assumption, verification method, consequence if wrong].

## 2. Audience evidence and creator participation

- **Observed viewing interests:** [Interest, source or artifact, date, confidence]. Avoid assuming the most popular overall category fits this audience.
- **Relevant creators and formats:** [Examples and what the audience values about them]. Specify compatible behavior, not imitation of their identity.
- **Audience tension:** [Recurring problem, desire, or conflict]. Show how it can generate several episodes.
- **Brand permission:** [Credible contribution, expertise, resource, or shared value]. Explain why the brand belongs in the conversation.
- **Community contribution:** [Comment approach, relevant collaboration, feedback use, owner, capacity]. Make participation feasible.

## 3. Long-form decision

- **Chosen mechanisms:** [Depth, meaningful time, relationship, serial continuation, attention economics]. Explain the applicable mechanisms using the synthesis in method.md.
- **Value of additional runtime:** [What viewers gain that a short clip cannot adequately deliver]. Remove padding.
- **Runtime hypothesis:** [Range and rationale]. Treat this as testable, not a rule from the historical case.
- **Shorts role:** [Discovery, frequent contact, specific bridge, or no role]. Identify the audience match and what will be measured.
- **Evidence qualification:** [What is sourced and what is inferred]. Do not label the synthesized five reasons as MEEDIA's exact list.

## 4. Channel and format specification

- **Channel promise:** [For this audience, we repeatedly deliver this experience, with this distinctive perspective].
- **Return reason:** [Why people will choose another episode or come back next month].
- **Format type:** [Fiction, challenge, tutorial, reporting, or another justified approach].
- **Host or cast:** [Continuity, expertise, personality, audience fit, availability].
- **Episode engine:** [Repeatable question or conflict plus variables that change].
- **Brand role:** [How the product, service, or values affect the action]. Explain relevance without a promotional monologue.
- **Tone and production:** [Viewer-appropriate style and sustainable complexity]. Specify where quality matters.
- **Content boundaries:** [Required factual review, disclosure, prohibited claims, or existing brand rules]. Include only those relevant to this assignment.
- **Account structure:** [One channel or justified separation]. Describe each audience promise and maintenance cost.

## 5. Pilot episode and slate

- **Pilot title:** [A truthful, specific audience promise].
- **Thumbnail concept:** [Subject, visible stakes, minimal wording]. Match the actual episode.
- **Opening:** [First scene or first few sentences]. Establish value and situation quickly.
- **Newcomer context:** [Only the background needed to understand this episode].
- **Episode beats:** [Setup, development, turning point, payoff]. Give each beat a viewer function.
- **Brand moments:** [Where relevance appears and why it belongs].
- **Current payoff:** [What the episode resolves]. Deliver what the packaging promises.
- **Next-view invitation:** [Relevant episode and why it follows]. Avoid depending on an unresolved current promise.
- **Further episodes:** [Several distinct instances of the same engine]. Demonstrate repeatability rather than unrelated topic ideas.
