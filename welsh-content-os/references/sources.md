# Public sources

All sources below were fetched successfully with curl on 2026-10-02. Only publicly accessible page text was used. The course page contributes its public overview and FAQ, not paid lesson content. Titles identify the sources; all instructional prose in this package is paraphrased.

1. [S1] Justin Welsh, *The Content Operating System*, public course overview and FAQ. https://learn.justinwelsh.me/content
   Contribution: Production scope, ideation, promotion, multiplication, structure curation, and the distinction from a copywriting or shortcut course.
2. [S2] Justin Welsh, *How to Build Digital Leverage (aka work smarter)*, June 10, 2023. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/how-to-build-digital-leverage-aka-work-smarter
   Contribution: Audience-led research, one robust idea feeding smaller assets, trust-building distribution, and the warning against constant brute-force work.
3. [S3] Justin Welsh, *How 1 piece of content becomes 16: The 1-3-5 Method*, July 27, 2024. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/how-1-piece-of-content-becomes-16-the-1-3-5-method
   Contribution: One pillar, three independent core ideas, five treatments per idea, flexible formats, and consistency without exhausting the creator.
4. [S4] Justin Welsh, *My Writing Process for 162.597M+ Impressions On LinkedIn.*, October 8, 2022. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/my-writing-process-for-162-597m-impressions-on-linkedin
   Contribution: Drafting useful information before the opening, creating a compelling preview, and ending with a recap and conversation invitation.
5. [S5] Justin Welsh, *The #1 Reason Content Fails*, June 7, 2023. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/the-1-reason-content-fails
   Contribution: The audience, topic, and relevance context test; unexplained terminology can prevent readers from recognizing value.
6. [S6] Justin Welsh, *Reverse engineering LinkedIn success*, October 12, 2024. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/reverse-engineering-linkedin-success

7. [S7] Justin Welsh, *How to Use 'Engagement Loops' to Grow Your Brand & Business*, January 13, 2024. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/engagement-loops

8. [S8] Justin Welsh, *Create Better Content, Faster, with ChatGPT.*, January 21, 2023. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/create-better-content-faster-with-chatgpt

9. [S9] Justin Welsh, *A Complete Guide to Passive Selling*. https://justinwelsh.me/essays/a-complete-guide-to-passive-selling
