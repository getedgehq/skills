# Content cycle output template

Copy the relevant fields into the deliverable and replace bracketed prompts. Bracketed text is guidance, not publishable copy. Omit sections outside the user's scope. Keep internal strategy notes separate from finished assets. The complete package implements the public production approach, with practical status and review fields added here. [S1][S2]
